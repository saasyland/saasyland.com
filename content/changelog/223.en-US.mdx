- Added: `bun run deploy:<environment>` refuses a placeholder secret before it builds anything. `scripts/prepare-deploy-secrets.ts` now runs right after `scripts/verify-bindings.ts` and fails when a secret that `wrangler.jsonc` declares is missing or empty, or holds a value between brackets, such as the `[UPDATE]` that had reached production as `AUTH_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, or a word such as `TODO` or `changeme`. The error names the keys without printing their values. See [Project scripts](/docs/tooling/scripts).
- Added: the auth area's deploy check, `auth:verify`, stops a deploy before the build unless `AUTH_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `AUTH_GITHUB_CLIENT_ID` in `.env.<environment>` hold client IDs in the formats of their providers, the same formats the live sign-in check applies after the deploy, now defined once in `OAUTH_CLIENTS` in `src/data/auth.ts`. `bun run auth:verify:preview` and `bun run auth:verify:production` run it on their own. See [OAuth providers](/docs/features/oauth) and, for Google's `Error 401: invalid_client`, [Troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting).
