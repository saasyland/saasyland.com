The licence area sells the tiers `core`, `complete` and `agency` as Polar products, on top of the polar area's plugin and webhooks ([Payments](/docs/features/payments)). `POLAR_PRODUCT_IDS` in `src/modules/license/license.utils.ts` maps the tiers to `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_CORE`, `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_COMPLETE` and `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_AGENCY`, and `licenseTierForProduct` reads the tier of a product from the same map. [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup) describes the products, licence keys and discounts the tiers need in Polar.

## Checkout

A signed-in user without an active licence sees `LicenseOffer` on `/app` and `/app/license`: the tier cards of the landing page (`PricingTiers`, see [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page)) with a `CheckoutButton` for each tier, the regional pricing checkbox and the course lessons the purchase unlocks.

### Consent to immediate supply

`CheckoutButton` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/checkout.tsx`) does not start a checkout itself. It opens a dialog that says the tier is digital content, supplied as soon as the buyer pays, and asks for one required checkbox: the buyer asks for immediate supply and acknowledges losing the right of withdrawal, which lasts <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" />, once supply begins with the issue of the licence key. Under the box the dialog says that a full refund remains possible for the same period while no project has been generated, and links the Refund Policy, the Terms of Service and the Commercial Licence. These are the conditions that end the right of withdrawal for digital content in the EU: the buyer's prior express consent, the acknowledgement, and the confirmation on a durable medium, which is the [purchase confirmation](#purchase-confirmation) email.

The form is `CheckoutConsent` (`checkout-consent.tsx`), a TanStack Form validated with `licenseZodSchemas.startCheckout.pick({ withdrawalConsent: true })`. **Continue to payment** with the box empty starts nothing: the box is marked invalid and points `aria-describedby` at `withdrawalConsentRequired` from `license.validations`, which is announced as an alert. The label ticks the box, focus starts in the dialog, and Escape or **Cancel** close it, except while the checkout is starting. The overview's sections load with the skeletons of every page, so `checkout.tsx` loads the form with `lazy` when the dialog opens and TanStack Form stays out of the entry script (see [Performance](/docs/conventions/performance#skeletons-stay-in-the-entry-script)).

### Starting the checkout

`startCheckout` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/start-checkout.ts`) takes `{ locale, tier, withdrawalConsent }`. Its schema accepts `withdrawalConsent` only when it is `true`, so the server refuses a checkout without the consent, and it:

1. returns `{ alreadyActive: true }` instead of a checkout when the account already has an `active` licence. The button shows `buy.alreadyActive` from `pages.license` and refreshes the licence, so a page left open since the purchase switches to the licence;
2. looks up a regional discount with `pppDiscountId` (`license.ppp.ts`): the percentage for the `cf-ipcountry` country, matched by `findRegionalDiscount` (`src/integrations/polar/polar.discounts.ts`) against the automatic percentage discounts in Polar that are redeemable now, skipped when the `regional_pricing` cookie is `off`;
3. creates a Polar checkout for the tier with `polar.checkouts.create`, tied to the account (`external_customer_id`) and filled in with its email and name (`customer_email`, `customer_name`), so the checkout form opens with them. It also carries the buyer's IP address from `cf-connecting-ip` (`customer_ip_address`, read with Better Auth's `getIP`), discount codes disabled, the consent in `custom_field_data`, which ticks Polar's own required checkbox for the buyer, the buyer's language under `locale` (`LICENSE_CHECKOUT_METADATA`) in `metadata`, next to the affiliate referral when the visitor came through an affiliate link (see [Affiliate programme](/docs/features/affiliates)), and the success URL `/app?checkoutId={CHECKOUT_ID}` in the buyer's language (`localizePathname`). Polar copies the metadata and the custom field data onto the order, and replaces `{CHECKOUT_ID}` (`POLAR_CHECKOUT_ID_PLACEHOLDER`) with the checkout ID;
4. returns the checkout URL, which the browser opens.

A refunded (`refunded`) or revoked (`revoked`) licence does not block a new purchase.

### Upgrades

An account has one licence, and a newer paid order replaces the tier of the older one (`grantLicense`). The Polar products have no upgrade prices, so a self-service upgrade would charge the full price of the higher tier on top of the first purchase, and buying a lower tier would downgrade the licence. Checkout is therefore refused while a licence is active, and `LicenseCard` tells owners to email support to change tier. Create upgrade products or discounts in Polar before allowing a second checkout.

### A tier chosen before sign-up

Each tier button on the landing page links to `/auth/sign-up?redirect=/app?tier=<tier>`. The return path survives sign-up, email verification and sign-in (`useAuthRedirect`), and a visitor who is already signed in goes straight to it. `licenseSearchSchema` (`src/data/license.ts`, built with `searchParam`) validates `tier` against `LICENSE_TIER` on `/app/license` and, as a param the licence area adds to the overview, on `/app`, and both `/app` and `/app/license` show `ChosenTier` above the tier cards: the tier, its price with the regional discount and a **Continue to checkout** button, which opens the same consent dialog. A locked paid course lesson links a signed-in viewer to `/app/license?tier=complete` and a guest to the landing page's pricing section; a lesson that needs an account links to sign-in ([Courses](/docs/features/courses)).

The sign-up page already names the tier. The licence area adds `SignUpOrderSummary` (`custom/app/sign-up-order-summary.tsx`) to `SIGN_UP_INTROS` in `src/data/license.sign-up-intros.ts`, and the page shows it in place of its description when `redirectTierSchema` (`src/data/license.ts`) reads a `LICENSE_TIER` from a `redirect` to `/app` or `/app/license`, with or without a locale prefix. The summary shows the tier's name with its `TIER_PRICES` amount, which of the `PURCHASE_STEP` steps the visitor is on (the account, then the payment on Polar), and the full refund within `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS` days of a purchase that has not been used, linked to the refund policy. The sign-up page offers no regional pricing choice, so the summary shows the list price, and checkout applies the regional discount unless the visitor declined it. `SignUpPending` draws `SignUpOrderSummaryPending` for the same redirects, and any other `redirect` keeps the description.

### After payment

Polar returns the buyer to `/app?checkoutId=<id>`, usually before the `order.paid` webhook has granted the licence. While `checkoutId` is present and the licence is not active, the page hides the tier cards and shows `CheckoutActivation`, which refetches `currentLicenseQuery` every <RepositoryFact name="activationPollSeconds" unit="second" /> (`ACTIVATION_POLL_MS`):

- once the licence is active, it thanks the buyer and the licence appears below;
- after <RepositoryFact name="activationTimeoutSeconds" unit="second" /> (`ACTIVATION_TIMEOUT_MS`) without a licence, it stops polling and says that Polar has not confirmed the payment yet, with the support email, the checkout ID as a reference and a **Check again** button.

A `checkoutId` that is not a UUID is ignored.

## Webhooks and licences

`src/data/license.polar-events.ts` adds the licence area to the Polar events (see [Payments](/docs/features/payments#webhooks)) with the events listed in `LICENSE_WEBHOOK_EVENTS` (`license.constants.ts`), whose order events come from `POLAR_WEBHOOK_EVENT`, and its `handle` passes each event to the handler of the same name in `licenseWebhookHandlers`. `licenseWebhookHandlers` satisfies `Record<keyof typeof LICENSE_WEBHOOK_EVENTS, unknown>`, so a handler without its event, or an event without its handler, does not compile. `licenseWebhookHandlers` (`src/modules/license/license.webhooks.ts`) match the user through the Polar customer `external_id`. An event carries the customer as Polar recorded it when it created the event, and a redelivery sends that same copy, so when its `external_id` names no account, `onOrderPaid` and `onBenefitGrantCreated` read the order again with `polar.orders.get` and match its current customer. An event for a customer with no account here is logged when it needed one and acknowledged, so Polar does not retry it:

- `onOrderPaid` → `grantLicense`: upserts the user's single licence with the tier of the purchased product and the buyer's consent to immediate delivery (see [Records and refunds](#records-and-refunds)); a newer purchase replaces an older one. Then `sendPurchaseConfirmationEmail` confirms the purchase (see [Purchase confirmation](#purchase-confirmation)); when it fails, the handler fails, so Polar delivers the event again. An order for a product that is not a licence is only logged, and a licence order whose customer matches no account is logged and reported (see [Orders that match no account](#orders-that-match-no-account)).
- `onBenefitGrantCreated` → `attachLicenseKey`: stores the Polar licence key on the licence. While the paid order is not recorded yet it fails, so Polar retries it; a key for a refunded order or for a product that is not a licence is acknowledged.
- `onOrderRefunded` → `revokeLicense` with the status `refunded`, only once the order is fully refunded; a partial refund keeps the licence.
- `onBenefitGrantRevoked` → `revokeLicense` with the status `revoked`.

`revokeLicense` sets the status of the licence bought with that order and records the order in `revoked_license_order`, so a late webhook cannot grant it again. It never replaces `refunded`: Polar also revokes the benefit of a refunded order, and the two events can arrive in either order. The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api) answers `licenseRefunded` or `licenseRevoked` from this status.

## Records and refunds

The `license` row keeps three records of the current purchase, each empty until it happens:

| Column                  | Records                                                                                                                                    | Written by                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `withdrawal_consent_at` | When the buyer consented at checkout to immediate delivery and acknowledged losing the right of withdrawal: the time the order was created | `grantLicense`, from `order.paid`                                    |
| `activated_at`          | The first activation of the key on any machine                                                                                             | The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api#records-of-use) |
| `delivered_at`          | The first time the CLI downloaded templates with the key                                                                                   | The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api#records-of-use) |

The buyer gives the consent in the [checkout dialog](#consent-to-immediate-supply). Polar's checkout repeats it as a required checkbox on every licence product, which `startCheckout` ticks with the consent just given, and stores it on the order as `custom_field_data.<slug>`, where the slug is <RepositoryFact name="licenseWithdrawalConsentField" /> (`LICENSE_CHECKOUT_FIELD` in `license.constants.ts`). `WITHDRAWAL_CONSENT_FIELD` in `src/lib/polar.ts` holds the field's name, type and English label, and `polar:verify` fails while a product does not require it (see [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#consent-checkbox)). `onOrderPaid` validates the field with Zod and records the consent only when it is `true`. A new order for the same account, such as a purchase after a refund, replaces the consent with its own and empties `activated_at` and `delivered_at`, so the records always describe the current purchase. Licences bought before the records existed have none from that time.

`refundEligibility` (`src/modules/license/license.refunds.ts`) is a pure function of the purchase time, the consent, the first download and the current time. The period is <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> from the purchase, `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS` in `src/data/refunds.ts`, the one place that number is declared. It returns one kind of `REFUND_ELIGIBILITY`:

| Kind                  | When                                                                                         | Carries       |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| `refundable`          | Within the period, the buyer consented and the CLI has not downloaded templates              | `until`       |
| `statutoryWithdrawal` | Within the period, no consent was recorded, so the statutory withdrawal applies, used or not | `until`       |
| `used`                | Within the period, the buyer consented and the CLI has downloaded templates                  | `deliveredAt` |
| `expired`             | The period has ended                                                                         | `endedAt`     |

An admin sees the records and the result in words on the user's profile; see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#profile).

## Purchase confirmation

After `grantLicense`, `onOrderPaid` sends the buyer the confirmation that the consent needs on a durable medium. `sendPurchaseConfirmationEmail` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/send-purchase-confirmation-email.ts`) renders `PurchaseConfirmationEmail` (`src/presentation/emails/purchase-confirmation-email.tsx`, namespace `emails.purchase-confirmation-email`) and sends it to the address of the account that bought:

- **Language**: the order's `metadata.locale`, validated with `localeField`; an order without a language the site speaks gets English.
- **Order**: the invoice number, or the Polar order ID before the order has one; the order date; the tier, named as the pricing cards name it in that language; and `total_amount` in the order's currency, converted from minor units with `fromMinorUnits`.
- **Right of withdrawal**: when `custom_field_data` holds the consent, the email repeats that the buyer asked for immediate supply and acknowledged losing the right of withdrawal, so it does not apply to this order. Without it, the email says that the buyer can withdraw within <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> of the order date.
- **Refund**: the deadline for a full refund before first use, `refundDeadline` (`license.refunds.ts`), the purchase time plus `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS`, the same date `refundEligibility` uses.
- **Links**: the licence page, the Terms of Service, the Commercial Licence and the Refund Policy in the email's language, on the host that received the webhook.

Dates are printed in UTC. The idempotency key is `PURCHASE_CONFIRMATION` (`src/data/license.email-purposes.ts`) with the Polar order ID, so Resend sends one email per order however often Polar delivers `order.paid`, and the email is built from the order alone, so a repeated delivery sends the same request. An order that maps to no account or tier gets no confirmation; a licence order without an account sends the site an alert instead (see [Orders that match no account](#orders-that-match-no-account)). See [Email](/docs/features/email).

## Orders that match no account

`startCheckout` ties every checkout to the signed-in account, so an order matches no account only when it was paid through a checkout that `startCheckout` did not create, such as a checkout link from the Polar dashboard, or when the account was deleted between checkout and payment. The buyer has paid and has no licence, so the site reports the order and lists it until it has one.

### Alert

`onOrderPaid` logs such an order and passes it to `sendUnmatchedOrderAlert` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/send-unmatched-order-alert.ts`), which sends `UnmatchedOrderAlertEmail` (`src/presentation/emails/unmatched-order-alert-email.tsx`, namespace `emails.unmatched-order-alert-email`) from `NOTIFICATIONS_EMAIL` to `CONTACT_EMAIL`, in `I18N.DEFAULT_LOCALE`. It names:

- the Polar order ID and the order date, in UTC;
- the tier and `total_amount` in the order's currency;
- the customer's email and name as the event carries them, or that they were not given;
- a link to the order in the Polar dashboard, which `polarOrderUrl` (`src/modules/license/license.utils.ts`) builds from the host in `POLAR_DASHBOARD_HOSTS` for `POLAR_SERVER`, `POLAR_ORGANIZATION_SLUG` (both in `src/data/polar.ts`) and the order ID;
- the steps that [recover the order](#recovering-an-order).

The idempotency key is `UNMATCHED_ORDER_ALERT` (`src/data/license.email-purposes.ts`) with the Polar order ID. Resend keeps a key for 24 hours, so repeated deliveries of the order within that time send one alert, and a redelivery after it alerts again while the order still matches no account. When the alert cannot be sent, the handler fails, so Polar delivers the event again.

### Admin list

The licence area adds `UnlicensedOrders` (`src/presentation/components/custom/admin/license/unlicensed-orders.tsx`) to the panels of the [admin dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard) in `src/data/license.dashboard.ts`, and the dashboard's loader prefetches its query. `unlicensedOrdersQuery` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/get-unlicensed-orders.ts`) checks `order: ["read"]` and lists, newest first, the [copies of Polar's orders](/docs/features/payments#orders-and-subscriptions-in-d1) that:

- have the status `paid`;
- have a `product_id` in `POLAR_PRODUCT_IDS`;
- are the `polar_order_id` of no licence;
- are not in `revoked_license_order`.

Each row (`unlicensed-order-columns.tsx`) shows the order ID, which opens the order in Polar in a new tab, the date, the Polar customer ID and the amount, converted from minor units. Without such an order the panel says that every paid licence order has its licence. The list also holds an order whose `order.paid` delivery has not succeeded yet, and the order of an account that was deleted, whose licence was deleted with it.

### Recovering an order

1. Find the buyer's account in the admin's user management, or create it with **Add user** and the email from the alert (see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin)). The account's ID is the last part of its profile's address, `/admin/users/<ID>`.
2. Open the order in Polar from the alert or the admin list, then its customer, choose **Edit Customer** in the customer's menu, set **External ID** to the account's ID and choose **Save Customer**. With the API, send `external_id` in `PATCH /v1/customers/{id}` with a token that has `customers:write`. Polar keeps an external ID once it is set and requires it to be unique within the organisation, so this works only for a customer without one whose account has no other Polar customer; otherwise refund the order in Polar and ask the buyer to buy again while signed in.
3. In Polar's webhook settings, open the endpoint's deliveries, find the order's `order.paid` event and choose **Redeliver**, then do the same for its `benefit_grant.created` event. `onOrderPaid` grants the licence and sends the purchase confirmation to the account's address, and `onBenefitGrantCreated` stores the licence key; a `benefit_grant.created` that arrives first fails until the licence exists, and Polar retries it. The order then leaves the admin list.

Polar redelivers an event only while it keeps the event's payload, which Polar's server archives after 90 days.

## Customer screens

`/app/license` shows `LicenseCard` (tier, key, CLI commands, a link to the Polar customer portal at `/api/auth/customer/portal` and the support email for a change of tier; the key and the portal wait for a confirmed address, see [Email verification](/docs/features/email-verification#what-waits-for-the-confirmed-address)) and `LicenseActivations`, which lists key activations from Polar and removes one with `deactivateLicense`; it answers `NOT_FOUND` when Polar no longer holds that activation. The CLI adds and removes activations through the [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api). Polar enforces the activation limit of each tier's licence-key benefit, which must equal `LICENSE_ACTIVATION_LIMITS`: <ActivationLimit tier="core" /> for Core, <ActivationLimit tier="complete" /> for Complete and <ActivationLimit tier="agency" /> for Agency. See [Customer app](/docs/features/customer-app).
