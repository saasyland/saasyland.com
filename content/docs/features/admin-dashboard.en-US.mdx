## Admin shell

`src/routes/admin.tsx` guards every admin page with `requireAdmin` and preloads the shared admin namespaces. The layout renders:

- `AdminSidebar`, built from `ADMIN_NAVIGATION` in `src/data/navigation.ts`, which groups the entries the areas contribute, each with its icon: Overview (Dashboard), Offerings (Products), Sales (Orders), Content (Courses) and Administration (User management, Settings). Every entry opens a working screen; a screen with no real source of data has no route, no menu entry and no palette entry (see [Real data only](#real-data-only)). The entry of the page on screen carries `aria-current="page"` and an accent bar. The button beside the breadcrumbs collapses the sidebar to its icons, each named in a tooltip, and on a phone opens it as a sheet.
- `AdminBreadcrumbs` and the search button of `AdminCommandPalette` in the top bar.
- `AdminPageHeader` (`custom/admin/admin-page-header.tsx`) at the top of every admin page: the title, a description, the page's actions on the right, an optional back link above the title and optional tabs under it, closed by a rule. `AdminPageHeaderPending` draws the same shape in each page's `pendingComponent`.
- The user widget in the sidebar footer, with a link to settings and `SignOutButton`.
- The wordmark at the top of the sidebar, which links to the home page.

### Command palette

⌘K or Ctrl+K, or the search button in the top bar, which shows the shortcut of the visitor's platform, opens `AdminCommandPalette`. Its list scrolls inside the dialog and groups what it finds, each row with its icon, its name and a line of detail:

- **Recent**: the last pages and records opened from the palette, at most `RECENT_LIMIT`, shown while the search is empty. They are kept in the browser's `localStorage` only.
- **Quick actions**: the entries of `ADMIN_COMMAND_ACTIONS` in `src/data/navigation.ts`: **New course** and **Open the website**.
- **Pages**: every entry of `ADMIN_NAVIGATION`, with its sidebar group, filtered in the browser.
- Records: the searches of `COMMAND_SEARCHES`, which `custom/admin/admin-command-palette.tsx` declares in the order of the table below, each with its icon, the full key of its heading, the permission it needs and its query. Once the search holds `SEARCH_TEXT_MIN_LENGTH` characters and typing pauses, the palette runs every search the signed-in role may run, in parallel, and each returns at most `SEARCH_HITS_LIMIT` matches.

| Group   | Area       | Server function | Permission     | Matches                                                     |
| ------- | ---------- | --------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Users   | `auth`     | `searchUsers`   | `user: list`   | Name or email                                               |
| Orders  | `payments` | `searchOrders`  | `order: read`  | Order ID, Polar customer ID, customer name or email         |
| Courses | `courses`  | `searchCourses` | `course: read` | Title in any language, or slug                              |
| Lessons | `courses`  | `searchLessons` | `course: read` | Title in any language, or slug; the detail names the course |

Every search is a server function behind `authorized(...)`, and the palette leaves out the searches the role's permissions do not grant, so it never asks for records the account may not read. The arrow keys move through the results, Enter opens one, Escape clears the search and then closes the palette, and a live region tells a screen reader when the palette is searching and how many results it found.

## Dashboard

`/admin` (`src/routes/admin.index.tsx`) preloads `getUsersQuery` and, through their `prefetch`, the query of every dashboard statistic and panel, and composes the widgets of `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/dashboard` with the panels the areas add:

- `DashboardActions`: a link for each entry of `ADMIN_DASHBOARD_ACTIONS` in `src/data/navigation.ts`, in its order, labelled by the message its `label` names: **Orders** and **Customers**, whose pages belong to the payments area (see [Orders and customers](/docs/features/orders)).
- `DashboardStats`: the statistics of `DASHBOARD_STATS`, which `custom/admin/dashboard/stats.tsx` declares in the order of the table below, each with its `icon`, the full key of its `label` in its area's `dashboard.<area>.json`, a `prefetch` that the loader runs and a `useValue` hook that reads the number, formatted for the page's locale. A statistic with a `to` links its whole card to the page that lists what it counts.
- `DashboardPanels`: the panels of `DASHBOARD_PANELS`, which `custom/admin/dashboard/panels.tsx` declares in their order, each with the skeleton that `AdminDashboardPending` shows for it. Each entry names its panel with `lazyRouteComponent`, so a panel's code, with its charts and tables, loads in a chunk of its own, which the dashboard's loader preloads together with the panel's data; the skeleton comes from the panel's `-pending.tsx` file.
- A users card: the newest accounts, <RepositoryFact name="adminPreviewPageSize" /> rows per page, with search and status filters. Each name links to the user's profile, where the actions are.

| Statistic                  | Area         | Source                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Registered users           | `auth`       | `total` of `getUsersQuery`: every row of `user`                                                               |
| Products in catalog        | `products`   | `total` of `getProductsQuery`: every row of `product`                                                         |
| Pending email verification | `auth`       | `pendingVerification` of `getUsersQuery`: accounts neither verified nor banned                                |
| Learners                   | `courses`    | `learners` of `courseProgressQuery`: accounts with saved progress in a published lesson                       |
| Videos                     | `courses`    | `total` of `getVideosQuery`: every row of `video`; it links to the courses, where each lesson holds its video |
| Newsletter subscribers     | `newsletter` | `newsletterSubscribersQuery`: rows of `newsletter_subscriber` whose status is `subscribed`                    |

| Panel                         | Area       | Source                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Paid orders without a licence | `license`  | `unlicensedOrdersQuery` (see [Licences](/docs/features/licences#orders-that-match-no-account))                                   |
| Licences by tier              | `license`  | `licenseCountsQuery` in `src/modules/license/use-cases/get-license-counts.ts`, see [Licences by tier](#licences-by-tier)         |
| Revenue                       | `payments` | `revenueQuery` in `src/modules/order/use-cases/get-revenue.ts`, see [Revenue](#revenue)                                          |
| New users                     | `auth`     | `userSignupsQuery` in `src/modules/user/use-cases/get-user-signups.ts`, see [New users](#new-users)                              |
| Course progress               | `courses`  | `courseProgressQuery` in `src/modules/lesson-progress/use-cases/get-course-progress.ts`, see [Course progress](#course-progress) |

Every query runs a server function behind `authorized(...)` with a permission the areas already declare, so a signed-in account without it gets an error, never the numbers.

### Periods

The revenue and new-user panels offer the periods <RepositoryFact name="dashboardRanges" /> from `TIME_RANGES` in `src/lib/time-series.ts`, and open on <RepositoryFact name="defaultDashboardRange" />. `timeSeries(range, now)` turns a period into its buckets: the last days up to and including today, or the last months up to and including this one, in UTC. The server function reads the clock and returns every bucket, so the browser draws exactly what the server counted. `bucketOf` in `src/integrations/drizzle-orm/drizzle.time-series.ts` groups the rows with SQLite's `strftime` in UTC, and `pointsOf` puts each total in its bucket and fills the buckets without rows with zero.

Each panel keeps its period in component state. `RangePicker` changes it in a transition, so the panel keeps the current numbers on screen, drained of colour and marked `aria-busy`, until the next period has loaded. `TimeSeriesChart` (`src/presentation/components/custom/admin/dashboard/time-series-chart.tsx`) draws the series as a bar chart with Recharts through `ChartContainer`; the chart is hidden from assistive technology, and a visually hidden table lists every day or month with its value.

### Revenue

`getRevenue` (`order: ["read"]`) reads the D1 copies of Polar's orders (see [Payments](/docs/features/payments#orders-and-subscriptions-in-d1)):

- It counts the orders whose status is one of <RepositoryFact name="revenueOrderStatuses" /> (`REVENUE_ORDER_STATUSES` in `src/modules/order/order.constants.ts`) and that were placed in the period. Draft, pending and void orders are not revenue.
- For each currency it returns the orders, `gross` (the sum of `total_amount`), `refunded` (the sum of `refunded_amount`), `net` (gross minus refunded) and `points`, the net amount of each bucket. Amounts are in the currency's major units, converted with `fromMinorUnits`, and include tax as Polar charged it.
- A refund is subtracted from the order it refunds, in the bucket of the day the order was placed, because the copy holds the refunded amount but not the moment of the refund.
- Currencies are never added together. The panel shows a tab per currency, the one with the most orders first, with its amount after refunds, its orders and its refunds above its chart.
- With no paid order in the period, the panel says so and draws no chart.

### New users

`getUserSignups` (`user: ["list"]`) counts the rows of `user` by the bucket of their `created_at` and returns the series and its total. The panel shows the total and the chart, or says that no account was created in the period.

### Course progress

`getCourseProgress` (`course: ["read"]`) lists every course in the admin's order (`position`) with its published lessons, the accounts that saved progress in one of them, the accounts that completed every one of them, and the lessons completed in all. Progress on draft lessons is not counted, and every account counts, admins included. `learners`, the number of accounts with saved progress in any course, is the **Learners** statistic. The panel lists the courses in a `DataGrid` (`useCourseProgressColumns` in `course-progress-columns.tsx`) that filters by course and by each count, or says that there is no course yet.

### Licences by tier

`getLicenseCounts` (`admin: ["access"]`) reads `license` in one query, grouped by `tier` and `status`, and writes nothing. It returns a row for each tier of `LICENSE_TIERS`, in that order, with the number of its licences in each status of `LICENSE_STATUSES`, and zero where a tier has none in a status. The panel lists the tiers in a `DataGrid` (`useLicenseCountColumns` in `license-count-columns.tsx`), each under its name from `components.custom.pricing.json`, with the counts aligned to the right. An active licence works in the CLI; a refunded or revoked one no longer does (see [Licences](/docs/features/licences)).

### Newsletter subscribers

`getNewsletterSubscribers` (`admin: ["access"]`) counts the subscribers who confirmed their address and have not unsubscribed. Pending confirmations and unsubscribed addresses are not counted.

### Users card

```tsx
<DataGrid
  columns={columns}
  data={users.rows.slice(0, ADMIN_DASHBOARD_PREVIEW_PAGINATION.pageSize)}
  id={DASHBOARD_USER_GRID_ID}
  label={t("users.title")}
  search={t("users.search")}
  selectable
/>
```

The grid works in the browser on the newest users of the first page of `getUsersQuery`, which holds <RepositoryFact name="defaultPageSize" /> users, newest first; it searches, filters by user, status and the day they joined, and sorts them without asking the server again. Its **Joined** column is the account's `created_at`. **View all** links to `/admin/users`.

## Real data only

Every number and row the admin shows is read from D1 by a module use case behind `authorized(...)`. The lint rule `admin-data/no-literal-data` in `scripts/admin-data-lint-plugin.ts` keeps it that way: `bun run check` fails when a file under `src/routes/admin*.tsx`, `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/` or `src/data/admin*.ts` declares

- a list of two or more numbers, which is a hardcoded series;
- a list of two or more records that carry a number, other than their sort position (`order`);
- an e-mail address, an ISO date or an SVG path drawn from numbers;
- a constant named after demo, dummy, fake, lorem, mock or sample data, or an upper-case constant that names a placeholder;
- a dashboard statistic whose `useValue` reads no query with `useSuspenseQuery` or `useQuery`.

Tests build their own rows, so the rule is off for them. `src/__test__/admin-data-lint-plugin.test.ts` lists what it accepts and what it rejects. A screen whose data has no source yet is not built until the source exists.
