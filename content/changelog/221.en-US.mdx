- Added: after `wrangler deploy`, `verify-deployment.ts` starts the sign-in with every provider of the sign-in page as the browser does, and fails the deploy unless the provider's authorize URL is on the provider's host, carries a `client_id` in that provider's format and returns to the deployment's own `/api/auth/callback/<provider>`. A placeholder uploaded as the `AUTH_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` secret had made Google answer `Error 401: invalid_client`, and nothing caught it. The error names the provider and the problem without printing the value. See [Project scripts](/docs/tooling/scripts) and, for Google's error and its fix, [Troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting).
