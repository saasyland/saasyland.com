- Changed: the **Curriculum** of the course editor orders chapters and lessons by drag and drop instead of up and down arrows. Drag a row by its handle, a lesson into another chapter too, or a chapter with all its lessons; with the keyboard, focus the handle, press Enter, choose the place with the arrow keys and press Enter again, while the screen reader announces each step. The new order shows at once and comes back as it was if saving fails. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses).
