- Changed: the course form, the chapter and lesson dialogs, the lesson editor and the materials dialogs no longer show a row of locale tabs above every translated field. One **Language** control at the top of each form lists every supported language with its name, its code and whether it is complete, partly translated, not translated or has errors; typing filters the list, and choosing a language switches every translated field of the form to it, with the English source under each one. The control counts how many languages are complete, works the same with nine languages or fifty, and saving with an error in a language you are not looking at switches the form to that language and focuses the field. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses#languages).
- Added: **All languages** opens a side panel with one field in every language at once, English first, each marked filled or missing, with a search and a **Missing only** filter, so you can review and fill a translation across every language without switching one by one. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses#languages).
