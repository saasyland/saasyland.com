- Added: the EU withdrawal function of Article 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive. **Withdraw from contract here**, in the footer of every page, the Refund Policy, the Terms of Service and the purchase confirmation, opens `/withdrawal`, where a buyer gives their name, the email address for the acknowledgement and the order number and presses **Confirm withdrawal**; on `/app/license` a card says whether the right of withdrawal or the full refund before first use still applies and opens the same statement with the account's details filled in. Every statement is stored with the refund terms at that moment, acknowledged by email with its content, date and time in the buyer's language, reported to the site's contact address and listed on the admin's user profile; refunds stay a decision made in Polar. See [Withdrawal function](/docs/features/licences#withdrawal-function).
- Changed: the Refund Policy, the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy say where the withdrawal function is, that its acknowledgement of receipt comes by email, and what a withdrawal statement stores and how long it is kept.
