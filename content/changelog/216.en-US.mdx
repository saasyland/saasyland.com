- Fixed: the phone menu is a real modal. `MobileMenu` is a React Aria `Modal` with a `Dialog`: focus moves to its close button, which sits where the menu button was, Tab stays inside, Escape and the close button return focus to the menu button, and the page behind is `inert`, so neither the keyboard nor a screen reader reaches the content it covers. It now also ends with **Start building**, which phones showed nowhere, and still moves nothing as it opens and closes. See [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page#navigation-and-back-to-top).
- Fixed: footer links overlapped on phones, by up to 19 pixels at 390 px in German and 36 at 320 px, because the Product list nested two columns inside half the width and a long single word cannot wrap. The footer's brand block and link groups now form one wrapping flex row whose items never get narrower than their longest word, so the groups sit side by side while their labels fit and stack when they do not. The header logo and the footer's contact address grew to tap targets of at least 24 by 24 pixels without moving their text. `footer.spec.ts` checks every locale at 320, 390 and 430 px.
- Fixed: `aria-current` marked links that do not lead to where the visitor is. The footer's **Foundation**, **Pricing** and **FAQ** were current anywhere on `/`; they are now current only on their own section, and **Start building** is never current, not even on `/#pricing`, where it was marked beside **Pricing**. See [Accessibility](/docs/conventions/accessibility).
- Fixed: the footer's language switcher drew no focus ring, because `outline-none` set the outline style to `none` and `focus-visible:outline-2` kept it. As a plain element it now leaves the ring from `globals.css` in place. See [Components and styling](/docs/conventions/components).
