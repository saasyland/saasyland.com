- Fixed: on a phone, a docs page no longer empties its article for a moment after it loads. Fumadocs' sidebar switched to its phone layout as soon as the page started, while the article's content was still loading, and that made the browser draw the article again; the switch now waits for the article. See [Dependencies](/docs/conventions/dependencies#when-the-bug-is-upstream).
- Changed: a new browser test delays a docs page's content and checks that its article stays in place while the page starts, on a wide screen and on a phone. The skip-link test presses Option+Tab in WebKit on macOS, where Tab skips links. See [Docs site](/docs/features/docs-site).
