- Fixed: unit tests that timed out or missed a render on a busy machine. `check-content.test.ts` checks each English page in a test of its own with `pageProblems`, instead of the whole site in one test that ran past Vitest's time limit under load. The translations and time zone tests load their translations before rendering, or resolve them inside `act`, so React shows the content at once instead of after its reveal throttle, and the provider test fills a mocked `TIME_ZONE_SOURCES` instead of importing the provider again for every test; `time-zone.test.ts` checks how the areas' lists are joined. The time zone combobox in `preferences-card.tsx` passes its options to `ComboboxList` as memoized `items`, so React Aria reuses them when the form re-renders instead of building every option again, and the course admin tests fill their forms by pasting instead of replaying every keystroke. [Testing](/docs/conventions/testing) lists these rules.
