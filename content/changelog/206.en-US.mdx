- The webhooks now fill D1's copies of Polar's orders and subscriptions. The billing area lists `order.created`, `order.updated`, `order.paid`, `order.refunded`, `subscription.created` and `subscription.updated` in `src/data/billing.polar-events.ts`, and `recordOrder` and `recordSubscription` write each record in one upsert under Polar's ID that applies only a version at least as new as the stored `changed_at`, so a late event changes nothing and a repeated one writes the same values again. `user_id` is the account that the customer's `external_id` names, resolved in the same statement by `polarCustomerAccount` (`src/integrations/polar/polar.customers.ts`), and stays empty when there is none. One `order.paid` grants the licence and copies the order at once; when the copy fails, the delivery answers 500 and Polar's retry repeats both writes without harm. `POLAR_WEBHOOK_EVENT` names the four new events, so `POLAR_EVENTS` and `polar:verify` now require eight, and every webhook endpoint in Polar must send them. The Polar test double builds subscriptions with `polarSubscription`. The privacy policy says that the copies stay, no longer linked to the account, when an account is deleted. See [Payments](/docs/features/payments) and [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup).
