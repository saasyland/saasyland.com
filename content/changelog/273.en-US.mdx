- Changed: corners are less rounded and the same for every element of a kind across the site, the docs, the workspace, the admin and the emails. Surfaces such as cards, dialogs, popovers and code blocks are <RepositoryFact name="radiusLgPixels" /> px, controls such as buttons, inputs and badges <RepositoryFact name="radiusMdPixels" /> px, and elements inside them such as menu items and `kbd` <RepositoryFact name="radiusSmPixels" /> px; the course badges are no longer pills, and avatars and status dots are round. See [Theme](/docs/ui/theme#corner-radius).
- Added: the lint rule `radius/no-off-scale` fails `bun run check` when code under `src/` uses a radius outside the scale, such as `rounded-xl` or `rounded-[4px]`. See [Lint and format](/docs/tooling/lint-format).
