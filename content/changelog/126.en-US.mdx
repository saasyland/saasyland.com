- Checkout funnel: a tier picked on the landing page survives sign-up (`redirect=/app?tier=<tier>`), and `/app` and `/app/license` show it with a one-press **Continue to checkout**. See [Payments and licences](/docs/features/payments).
- `/app` and `/app/license` show accounts without a licence the landing page's tier cards with regional prices, and the course lessons a Complete or Agency licence unlocks, counted from D1. See [Customer app](/docs/features/customer-app).
- After paying, the buyer returns to `/app?checkoutId=<id>` in their language and watches the licence activate; if Polar has not confirmed the payment within a minute, the page says so and gives the support email and the checkout reference.
- `startCheckout` refuses an account that already has an active licence (`LICENSE_ALREADY_ACTIVE`), and the Polar plugin's `/api/auth/checkout` is disabled over HTTP, so every checkout passes that check.
- The light theme's `--ring` is darker (`oklch(0.55 0.106 208)`), so small teal labels meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
