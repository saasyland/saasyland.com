- Added: security settings in the customer workspace and the admin console. **Settings** has a General and a Security tab: change the email address (the old address confirms, the new one verifies), change the password or set one for an account that signs in with GitHub or Google, turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app (QR code, setup key, backup codes to copy or download, new codes on demand), connect or disconnect GitHub and Google without ever removing the last way to sign in, and see each signed-in device with its browser, system, country and IP address and sign it out. See [Settings](/docs/features/settings).
- Added: **Delete account** in the customer's settings. After the password, an emailed link opens a page that lists what is removed and what is kept, and deletes the account only when **Delete my account** is pressed.
- Changed: deleting an account, by its owner or by an admin, keeps its licence and the licence's delivery records as payment records with no link to the account, and revokes an active licence first, so its key stops working in the CLI. The Privacy Policy, the deletion notices and the admin's delete dialog say so.
- Changed: an email tells the account when its password is changed or set.
