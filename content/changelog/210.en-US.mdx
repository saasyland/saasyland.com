- Fixed: Cloudflare's Bot Fight Mode adds its JavaScript Detections script to every page and can give it a nonce only from the `Content-Security-Policy` header, so the hash-based policy that prerendered pages carried in a meta tag blocked it on the home page, the docs and the blog. Every HTML response, prerendered or rendered, now gets a nonce of its own and `documentPolicy(nonce)` in its header. The Worker streams a prerendered page through `withNonce`, an `HTMLRewriter` that puts the nonce on its scripts and module preload and replaces the nonce that prerendering left in the page, so the build no longer hashes scripts, writes a meta policy or swaps the entry script for a loader. HTML responses are `private, no-cache` and carry no `ETag` or `Last-Modified`, so a browser never reuses a page with another response's nonce. See [Security and hardening](/docs/features/security).
