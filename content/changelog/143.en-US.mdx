- Navigation is composed from areas. Each area declares its paths in `src/data/<area>.routes.ts` and its admin sidebar, dashboard, customer sidebar, header and footer entries in `src/data/<area>.navigation.ts`, each with its own `order`, and the barrels `routes.entries.ts`, `navigation.entries.ts` and `dashboard.entries.ts` join them; `ROUTES` keeps its name and types. The dashboard's product count and **Add product** button now come from the products area. The customer sidebar's labels moved from `pages.app` to the new `pages.app.sidebar`, and the footer's column headings to `columns`. The site looks and works as before. The Remotion project resolves `lucide-react` from its own dependencies, because the admin rail of `app-tour` reads the entries with their icons. See [Paths and navigation](/docs/conventions/routes#paths-and-navigation).
