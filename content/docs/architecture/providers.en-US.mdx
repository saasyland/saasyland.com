`src/routes/__root.tsx` owns the document and application-wide provider composition. The router creates a QueryClient and connects its cache to SSR. The translation provider loads the active route namespaces into `IntlProvider`. Theme, motion, React Aria routing, tooltips, notifications and the offline indicator retain the existing presentation behavior.

Fumadocs uses its TanStack provider and the native Router link adapter. Shared providers belong under `src/providers`; page-only contexts stay beside their feature.
