- Fixed: the content no longer shifts sideways when the scrollbar comes or goes. `html` keeps the scrollbar's room with `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, so a page sits in the same place whether or not it scrolls, while it grows and while a dialog, popover or menu stops it from scrolling; `/app` and `/admin`, which scroll their content area instead of the window, mark it `data-page-scroller` to keep the room there and fill the window. The thin page scrollbar with its transparent track moved from `body` to `html`, the only element browsers style the window's scrollbar from, and `html` takes the dark tokens behind the landing, auth, blog and course pages, so in the light theme the room shows their dark background instead of a light strip. React Aria and Base UI already lock scrolling with `scrollbar-gutter`, so nothing compensates twice; `e2e/specs/scrollbars.spec.ts` checks it all in Chromium with classic scrollbars. See [Scrollbars](/docs/ui/theme#scrollbars).
