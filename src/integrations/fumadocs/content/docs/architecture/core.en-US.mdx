## Contents

- `errors/` — `DomainError`, `ForbiddenError`, `NotFoundError`, `UnauthorizedError`, `ConflictError`, …
- `constants/` — locale (and related) catalogs used by i18n and product knobs
- `utils/` — pure helpers shared across modules

## Why it exists

Cross-cutting **valid values** and **error codes** should not be redefined per feature. App-enabled locales (subset) still live in `i18n.config.ts`; the broader catalog can live in `_core`.
