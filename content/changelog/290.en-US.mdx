- Added: **Orders** at `/admin/orders` lists every order copied from Polar with its account, product, total in its currency, refunds and status, searched and filtered by status, product and period on the server. Each order's page adds Polar's live payment, with the discount and its code, tax, refunds, billing country and invoice, and keeps the saved copy with a notice when Polar cannot be reached. See [Orders and customers](/docs/features/orders).
- Added: a **Customers** tab in user management lists every Polar customer with their account, orders and what they paid after refunds per currency, and each profile lists the account's orders and shows the licence's machine limit. The dashboard links to both.
- Changed: a server-side `DataTable` sends its search and filters to its query and waits until typing pauses before it searches. See [DataTable](/docs/ui/data-table#filters).
