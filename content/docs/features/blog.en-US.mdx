## Content

Posts live in `content/blog/<slug>.<locale>.mdx`, one file per locale. The frontmatter schema in `src/integrations/fumadocs/fumadocs.schema.ts` requires `title`, `authorName` and `date`, and accepts `description`, `authorImage`, `authorUrl`, `excerpt`, `faq`, `featured`, `image`, `published` (default `true`), `tags` and `updated`. A tag is one of `BLOG_TAGS` in `src/data/blog.ts`, and the schema rejects any other value; `PostRow` and `PostMeta` show each tag by its label under `tags` in `pages.blog`, so tags read in the page's language.

- `description` is the post's search description: the `description` and `og:description` tags and the feed use it. `excerpt` is the longer teaser under the title and on the blog index, and stands in for a missing `description`.
- `image` is the cover, a WebP file in `public/images/blog` of the size `BLOG_COVER` in `src/data/blog.ts` declares. The post page gives the cover that `width` and `height`, and the structured data describes it at that size; `src/data/__test__/blog.test.ts` fails when a cover has another size.
- `authorImage` must be a path on this site, such as `/images/blog/authors/piotr-j-borowiecki.webp`, so a reader's browser never asks another host for it; the schema rejects a URL. `authorUrl` is the URL of the author's profile, which the author block, the `article:author` tag and the structured data link to.

## Dates

`date` is the day a post was first published, and it never changes. `updated` is the day the post's facts or substance last changed, such as a price, a feature that shipped or a comparison that no longer holds; it does not move for a typo, formatting or a fix to a translation. Both are written `YYYY-MM-DD`, and every locale of a post carries the same two, because they date the post, not its translation. The blog index keeps both for every post, and they drive every date that readers and search engines see:

- `PostMeta` shows the publication date and, when `updated` is later than `date`, the update date, each in its own `<time>` element with a `dateTime`, formatted with the `calendarDate` format for the page's locale. The labels are the ICU messages `post.published` and `post.updated` in `pages.blog`, which take the formatted date as `{date}`. `PostRow`, on the blog index and in the landing page's Notes, shows only the publication date, which keeps the list compact and is the date the list is sorted by.
- The `BlogPosting` JSON-LD gives `date` as `datePublished`, and `updated`, or `date` when there is none, as `dateModified`.
- `buildPageHead` writes the same two as `article:published_time` and `article:modified_time`, and `sitemap.xml` gives the post's URL in every locale its `article:modified_time` as `<lastmod>`; see [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo).

The frontmatter schema refuses a date in any other format and an `updated` earlier than `date`, so `bun run dev` and the build stop on such a post, and `scripts/check-content.ts` fails when a translation's `date` or `updated` differs from the English post's.

## Figures from the code

A post prints the values the code defines instead of typing them: `<RepositoryFact>` for counts, the coverage threshold (`coverageThreshold`), React's major version (`reactMajor`), the illustrative hourly rate (`illustrativeHourlyRate`, with `unit="currency"`) and the hours the Core price buys (`corePriceInHours`, with `unit="hour"`), and `<TierPrice>`, `<ActivationLimit>` and `<HeadStartTable />` for prices, machine limits and the head start. The `title` and the `faq` questions and answers are ICU messages: `formatPostText` in `src/lib/post-text.ts` formats them with `REPOSITORY_FACTS` and each tier's `<tier>Price` and `<tier>Machines`. `formatPostText` also passes `CLI_GENERATION_VALUES` from `src/data/cli.ts`, so a sentence that changes when project generation ships is written once as `{generation, select, available {…} other {…}}`, and `post-faq.test.ts` renders it with both statuses. It also passes `billingGeneration` (`BILLING_GENERATION` in `src/data/marketing.ts`), `affiliateProgram` and `affiliateHoldDays` from `src/data/affiliates.ts`, and `refundWithdrawalDays` (`REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS`), and the same test renders the selects on `billingGeneration` and `affiliateProgram` with both statuses. `toBlogPostSummary` formats the title, and `<PostFaq entries={frontmatter.faq} />` renders the FAQ that the post's FAQPage JSON-LD also reads. `post-faq.test.ts` formats every post's title, questions and answers in its locale.

## Source

Every build turns the posts into a blog index per locale (see [Fumadocs](/docs/integrations/fumadocs#content-indexes)): the published posts under `/blog`, newest first, with the same i18n settings as the docs, each with its frontmatter, its table of contents and its reading time, counted with `Intl.Segmenter` for that locale. `loadBlogIndex(locale)` imports a locale's index, a file under `/assets/`, and `toBlogPostSummary` in `fumadocs.blog.ts` formats a post's title and localizes its URL. The loaders below read it in the browser on a client navigation, so moving through the blog never reaches the Worker. `relatedBlogPosts` in the same file picks, from the same index, the other posts that share the most tags with a post, the newest first among equals, as many as `RELATED_POSTS` allows.

## Routes

- The `/blog` layout (`_site._marketing.blog.tsx`) sits in the `_site` and `_marketing` layouts, which add the site header, `PageFrame`, `Footer` and `BackToTop` around it (see [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page#navigation-and-back-to-top)). It loads `docs.css` for article typography. The header marks **Blog** as the current section on the index and on every post. Its loader preloads `fumadocs` and `pages.blog` and reads the blog's title, and its head names the feed of the page's locale in a `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">` (`blogFeedLinks`), so feed readers and browsers find it from the index and from every post.
- `/blog` (`_site._marketing.blog.index.tsx`) lists the posts of the blog index with `PostRow`. Under the lead it links the feed of its locale, and it describes itself to search engines as a `Blog` with its posts (`buildBlogStructuredDataHtml`).
- `/blog/$` (`_site._marketing.blog.$.tsx`) finds one post in the blog index (404 when it is missing or unpublished), preloads its compiled MDX with `blogContent`, and composes `PostMeta` and `PostShare` in the bar under the title, `PostToc` (or, on small screens, `InlineTOC` under the same title), and after the article `PostAuthor` beside a second `PostShare`, then `PostCta` and `PostRelated`, all from `src/presentation/components/custom/blog`. It adds JSON-LD from `buildBlogPostStructuredDataHtml` and article metadata from `buildPageHead`, with the blog's title from `pages.blog` as its `article:section`, an `article:tag` for each tag in the page's language and its own social card; see [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo). The post wraps its MDX in Fumadocs' `RootProvider`, with search and the theme switch off and the strings of `useFumadocsI18n`, so the copy buttons of code blocks and heading anchors and the FAQ's accordion items are labelled in the post's language.

The landing page Notes section shows three posts of the blog index, featured ones first, then the newest.

## Sharing

`PostShare` renders the same list twice on every post: in the bar under the title, and after the article beside the author. Every control shares the post's canonical URL in the page's locale, and every network receives the post's title.

- **Copy link** is a React Aria `Button`. It writes the URL to the clipboard, turns its icon into a check for a moment and shows a `toast.success` notification, which the notifications region announces to screen readers. When the browser refuses, `toast.error` says so.
- **X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Hacker News and Reddit** are plain links to each network's own share page, opened in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. The page loads no script, widget or tracking pixel from any of them, sets no cookie for them and adds no tracking parameter to the URL, so the canonical URL is what gets shared and the links work without JavaScript.
- **Share by email** is a `mailto:` link with the title as the subject and the URL as the body, opened in the reader's mail app rather than a new tab.
- **More ways to share** opens the system share sheet with `navigator.share`, where the browser has one, as on phones. The button keeps its place from the first render and becomes visible once the page has hydrated in such a browser, so nothing moves. When the reader cancels the sheet nothing happens; when sharing fails the button copies the link instead.

The networks' marks are their official logos from Simple Icons, in `src/presentation/components/custom/icons.tsx`, drawn in the text colour; the other icons come from Lucide. Each control is named in every locale under `post.share` in `pages.blog`.

Every heading in a post links to itself, and its copy button copies the link to that section, so a reader can share one section as well as the whole post.

## Author and related posts

`PostAuthor` names the author from the frontmatter alone: `authorName`, the image at `authorImage` when there is one, and a link to `authorUrl` with `rel="author"` when there is one. It adds no title, biography or claim the frontmatter does not make.

`PostRelated` lists the posts `relatedBlogPosts` picked, with their dates and reading times, so every post links to others on the same subjects. It renders nothing when the locale has no other post.

## Feed

Each locale has an RSS 2.0 feed of its blog at `BLOG_FEED.PATH` in `src/lib/feed.ts`, localized like a page: `/blog/rss.xml`, `/de-DE/blog/rss.xml` and so on. `scripts/generate-seo-files.ts` writes the feeds after the build with `buildBlogFeeds`, from the heads of the prerendered pages, so they cost the Worker nothing and say what the pages say:

- The channel takes its title, link and description from the locale's blog index, and names its own URL in an `atom:link rel="self"`.
- Each post is an item with its title, canonical URL as `link` and `guid`, `article:published_time` as `pubDate`, the author as `dc:creator`, each tag in the page's language as a `category` and its description. Items are newest first, and `lastBuildDate` is the latest publication or update date of the posts.
- Only pages the sitemap lists are read, and an unpublished post is never prerendered, so a draft never reaches a feed. The build fails when a post has no publication date or a locale has no blog index.

The prerenderer follows links, and the feed link on the blog index points at a file the build writes afterwards, so `shouldPrerender` in `src/data/tanstack.vite.ts` skips every path with a file extension. After a deploy, `verify-deployment.ts` requests the English feed with the rest of the live checks.

## Tags

Tags label posts and fill the `article:tag` tags, the feed's categories and the structured data's `keywords`, but they have no pages of their own. Most tags label very few posts, so a page per tag in every locale would list what the blog index already lists, and search engines treat such near-empty archives as thin content. Tag pages are worth adding once each tag gathers enough posts to need its own list; until then they would only multiply the URLs to crawl.

## Editing

Posts are MDX files in Git. The admin has no blog editor: a post is written, reviewed and published as a pull request.

## Checks

Run `bun run content` after adding a post. `bun scripts/check-content.ts` verifies that every locale exists, that titles, descriptions and excerpts are translated, that code blocks are identical, and that every locale of a post has the same `date` and `updated`.

`blog-routes.component.test.tsx` checks every share target's URL, the copy confirmation, the system share sheet, the author block, the related posts, the feed link and the post's metadata; `feed.test.ts` checks the feeds. In the browser suite, `blog-share.spec.ts` shares a post on a desktop and on a phone, with and without JavaScript, and `seo.spec.ts` reads every locale's feed and compares it with the published posts.
