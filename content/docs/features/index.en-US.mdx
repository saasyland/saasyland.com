Every page in this chapter describes the code as it is. A feature is **live** when it reads or writes real data through a module use case, Better Auth or Polar. A **demo screen** renders static rows from `src/data` or constants inside its widgets and stores nothing.

## Live

- Accounts: [Authentication](/docs/features/authentication), [OAuth providers](/docs/features/oauth), [Email verification](/docs/features/email-verification), [Password reset](/docs/features/password-reset), [Two-factor authentication](/docs/features/two-factor), [Sessions](/docs/features/sessions), [Account](/docs/features/account).
- Admin: the [dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard) counters and users table, the **All users** tab of [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin), the product and category lists of [Products](/docs/features/products) and [Categories](/docs/features/categories), and the **Security** tab of [Settings](/docs/features/settings).
- Customers: the [Customer app](/docs/features/customer-app) with Polar checkout and licences ([Payments and licences](/docs/features/payments)).
- Public site: [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page) with regional pricing and newsletter, [Blog](/docs/features/blog), [Docs site](/docs/features/docs-site), [Legal pages](/docs/features/legal-pages).
- Platform: [Internationalization](/docs/features/internationalization), [Transactional email](/docs/features/email), [HTTP handlers](/docs/features/api-surface), [Security](/docs/features/security).

## Not implemented

- [Organizations](/docs/features/organizations): no teams, memberships or tenant scoping.
- [Impersonation](/docs/features/impersonation): server functions exist, no screen calls them.
- [Content access](/docs/features/content-access): no premium content gating; `/premium` is a placeholder.
