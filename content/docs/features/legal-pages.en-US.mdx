| Route         | Route file                                   | Namespace                |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `/privacy`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.privacy.tsx`    | `pages.legal.privacy`    |
| `/terms`      | `src/routes/_site._marketing.terms.tsx`      | `pages.legal.terms`      |
| `/refunds`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.refunds.tsx`    | `pages.legal.refunds`    |
| `/licence`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.licence.tsx`    | `pages.legal.licence`    |
| `/affiliates` | `src/routes/_site._marketing.affiliates.tsx` | `pages.legal.affiliates` |

Each route file defines its own page component, a static `NAMESPACE`, and a module constant with the section keys in display order:

```tsx
const SECTIONS = ["operator", "accounts", "purchases", "payments", "licence", "conduct", "refunds", "responsibility", "law"] as const
```

The page renders `title` and `description` through `ContentPage`, then `lastUpdated`, then `sections.<key>.title` and `sections.<key>.body` for every key. There is no shared legal component. The privacy policy and the affiliate terms pass the values from `AFFILIATE_TERMS` (`src/data/affiliates.ts`) to their messages, so the commission, cookie duration, hold period and minimum payout are never typed into copy. Both also pass `AFFILIATE_PROGRAM` as `{program}`, so that while the programme is arriving the privacy policy says that Affonso receives nothing and its script does not load, and the privacy policy passes `SIDEBAR_COOKIE.DAYS` (`src/data/sidebar.ts`) as `{sidebarDays}`, the lifetime of the `sidebar_state` cookie that the sidebar sets. The affiliate terms render their bodies with `t.rich`, so that the "How to join" section can link the partner portal at `AFFILIATE_PORTAL_URL`. The refund policy and the terms of service pass `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS` (`src/data/refunds.ts`) to their messages as `{days}`, the period that `refundEligibility` counts as well, and render their bodies with `t.rich`, so that their withdrawal sections link the withdrawal function at `/withdrawal`.

## Editing

- Change copy in `messages/<locale>/pages.legal.<doc>.json` for every locale and run `bun run check:i18n`.
- To add a section, add its key to `SECTIONS` and its messages in every locale.
- The footer links are the footer entries of `LEGAL_NAVIGATION` in `src/areas/legal.navigation.ts`, labelled from `navigation.legal.json`: the legal column, and the affiliate terms in the product column; the legal area adds the consent line under the sign-up form, `SignUpConsent` with its skeleton in `custom/sign-up-consent.tsx`, to `SIGN_UP_NOTICES` in `src/data/legal.sign-up-notices.ts`, so a project without legal pages has a sign-up form without links to them. `LegalPending` (`custom/legal-pending.tsx`) is the skeleton of all five pages, the `pendingComponent` of each page's route.

The licence page separates the public repository's MIT licence from separately supplied paid material, and a refund or chargeback ends the licence for that purchase, including the rights to applications generated with it. The refund policy gives a full refund within <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> before first use, which is a project generated with the CLI or a paid lesson opened; the licence records the first template download and the first paid lesson opened (see [Licences](/docs/features/licences#records-and-refunds)). It explains when the right of withdrawal ends for digital content and keeps mandatory consumer rights. The affiliate terms hold each commission for <RepositoryFact name="affiliateHoldDays" unit="day" /> and deduct the commission of an order refunded or charged back after its payout. The refund policy's "Withdrawing online" section and the terms say where the withdrawal function is and that its acknowledgement of receipt comes by email, the information Article 6(1)(h) of the Consumer Rights Directive asks for; the privacy policy lists what a withdrawal statement stores and how long it is kept. The withdrawal page itself and its footer link belong to the licence area, see [Licences](/docs/features/licences#withdrawal-function). Keep every document aligned with the real operator, checkout, providers and data handling.
