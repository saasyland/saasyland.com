## Orders

The page `/admin/orders` (`src/routes/admin.orders.index.tsx`) lists the D1 copies of Polar's orders ([Payments](/docs/features/payments#orders-and-subscriptions-in-d1)) in the `DataGrid` ([Data grid](/docs/ui/data-grid)) with `useOrderColumns` (`src/presentation/components/custom/admin/payments/order-columns.tsx`). The payments area adds it to the Sales group of the sidebar, and links to it and to the customers from the dashboard, in `src/data/navigation.ts`.

- `getOrdersPageQuery` calls `getOrders` (`src/modules/order/use-cases/get-orders.ts`, `order: ["read"]`). Paging, sorting by date, total, refund or status, the search and the filters all run on the server, and the export asks it for every page.
- The search matches the order ID, the Polar customer ID, the product and the name and email of the matching account, and is sent once typing pauses.
- The filters are the day the order was placed; the customer, matching the account's name and email and the Polar customer ID; the products; a range of totals, compared in each order's own currency; the discount: any discount, no discount, or one discount; the billing countries; and the statuses, from `ORDER_STATUSES`. `getOrderFilters` (`get-order-filters.ts`, `order: ["read"]`) lists the products, the discounts and the billing countries that the copies hold, each product and discount under the name of its newest order.
- Each row shows when the order was placed and its ID, the account, or No account with the Polar customer ID, the product, the total in the order's currency with what was refunded, the discount with its code and amount, the billing country, and the status. The Placed column is pinned to the left. The Refunded column is hidden until the admin shows it from the Columns menu. Amounts are converted with `fromMinorUnits` and include tax.
- The CSV has the date as an ISO moment, the customer as their name and email or the Polar customer ID, amounts with their currency code, the discount's name, the country code and the status.
- With no filter set and no order copied yet, the grid says that no orders exist yet; a search or filter that matches nothing says so and offers to clear them.

## Order page

`/admin/orders/<orderId>` (`admin.orders.$orderId.tsx`) loads `getOrderQuery` (`get-order.ts`, `order: ["read"]`):

| Part       | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `order`    | The D1 copy: placed and last changed, product, total, refunded, the discount and its amount, the billing country, invoice number, subscription and Polar customer, with the matching account. An order copied before the discount and the billing country were kept says Not copied yet for both |
| `polar`    | `polar.orders.get` when the page loads: subtotal, discount, amount after discount, tax, total, refunds and refunded tax, the status, the discount's name and code, the billing name and country, the customer's name and email, and `polar.orders.invoice` once Polar has generated the invoice  |
| `polarUrl` | The order in Polar's dashboard                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

When Polar does not answer, `polar` is `{ read: "unavailable" }` (`POLAR_READ`, `order.constants.ts`) and the page keeps the copy and says that Polar could not be reached. An order the copy does not have is a not found page. The account card links to the profile, where the licence and every order of the account are.

## Customers

The payments area adds the Customers tab to user management (an entry of `USER_TABS`, `/admin/users/customers`) and the orders of an account to its profile (an entry of `USER_PROFILE_SECTIONS`).

- `getCustomersPageQuery` calls `getCustomers` (`get-customers.ts`, `order: ["read"]`), which groups the copies by Polar customer and joins the account their orders matched. The page shows them in the `DataGrid` with `useCustomerColumns` (`customer-columns.tsx`): the account with its sign-up date, the number of orders, what the customer paid after refunds in each currency, counting the orders whose status is one of <RepositoryFact name="revenueOrderStatuses" />, and the last order. Whether the account's email is verified waits in the Columns menu. Currencies are never added together. Its checkboxes select customers to export as CSV.
- The search matches the name, the email and the Polar customer ID. The filters are the customer as text, email verification as yes or no, the order count as a range, and the sign-up and last order as ranges of days; they, the paging and sorting by customer, sign-up, orders and last order run on the server, validated by `orderZodSchemas.getCustomers`.
- The licence area adds the **Machines** column (`license-machines.tsx`) as one element of the columns of `useCustomerColumns`, and its skeleton cell, `CustomerMachinesPending`, as one element of the columns of `AdminCustomersPending`, so a project without the licence has neither: for each customer with an account it shows how many machines the licence has activated against its limit, such as 2 of 5 machines in use, and opens the list of machines with their label, system and activation time. The cells of a page share one `userActivationsQuery` for the accounts on that page (see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#profile)).
- `UserOrders` (`user-orders.tsx`) lists the account's orders on its profile, each linked to its page, with what it paid after refunds per currency.

## Discounts and billing countries of older orders

`recordOrder` copies the discount and the billing country with every order event, so an order Polar changes again gets them on its next webhook. Orders copied before `20261122143452_familiar_tusk.sql` keep `discount_amount` empty until then. `bun run orders:backfill:<local|preview|production>` copies them at once from Polar's API; see [Database](/docs/tooling/database#discounts-and-billing-countries-of-older-orders).
