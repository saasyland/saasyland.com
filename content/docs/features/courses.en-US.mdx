title: "Courses"
description: "Demo only: a static course editor and a course product type, with no course data."
---

What exists:

- `/admin/courses/create` (`src/routes/admin.courses.create.tsx`) renders `CourseDetails`, `CourseSidebar` and a curriculum built from the `CURRICULUM_SECTIONS` constant in `admin/courses/curriculum.tsx`. No route links to it, and **Save** and **Cancel** are not wired.
- `/admin/products?tab=courses` shows an empty state.
- The product `type` column accepts `course`.

What does not exist: a course, lesson or enrolment table, a course module, and any learner screen in `/app`. A course feature needs its own tables and module under `src/modules`.
