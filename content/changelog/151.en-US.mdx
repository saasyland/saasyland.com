- Fixed: three library bugs that visitors hit while a page loads, each carried as a Bun patch until a release fixes it. TanStack Router's first render after hydration scrolled the window back to the top, so a visitor who scrolled before the page had hydrated lost the position (`@tanstack/router-core` 1.171.39). Safari and Firefox cancel the route chunks of a page the visitor leaves, and `lazyRouteComponent` took the cancelled import for a chunk removed by a deployment and reloaded the page over the visitor's navigation (`@tanstack/react-router` 1.170.46, with the fix from [TanStack/router#8830](https://github.com/TanStack/router/pull/8830)). Motion's `LazyMotion` left a feature bundle that failed to load, as on such a departure, as an unhandled rejection (`framer-motion` 14.14.0). `@tanstack/react-router`, `@tanstack/react-start` and `motion` are pinned to the patched versions. `navigation.spec.ts` scrolls the landing page before it hydrates and leaves pages while their route code and animations load, in Chromium and WebKit, and the specs no longer wait for a page to render before leaving it.
