## Layout

`src/routes/app.tsx` belongs to the auth area. It guards the workspace with `requireSignedIn` and preloads its namespaces, with those other areas add to it (`layoutNamespaces("/app")`; the licence area adds `pages.license`):

```tsx
const NAMESPACES = ["auth.errors", "pages.app.sidebar", "timezones", ...layoutNamespaces("/app")] as const

export const Route = createFileRoute("/app")({
  beforeLoad: requireSignedIn,
  component: AppLayout,
  loader: ({ context }) => preloadNamespaces({ locale: getCurrentLocale(), namespaces: NAMESPACES, queryClient: context.queryClient }),
  pendingComponent: AppPending,
  staticData: { namespaces: NAMESPACES },
})
```

`AppLayout` renders `AppSidebar` and `AppHeader` from `src/presentation/components/custom/app` inside the shadcn `SidebarProvider`. The sidebar builds its links from `APP_NAVIGATION` in `src/data/navigation.ts` (Overview and Settings from `auth.navigation.ts`, Licence from `license.navigation.ts`, labelled from `navigation.auth.json` and `navigation.license.json`) and its resources from `APP_RESOURCE_NAVIGATION` (Documentation from `content.navigation.ts`), followed by the support address, reads the user from the route context (`getRouteApi("/app")`) and ends with `SignOutButton`. The header shows the current page and a link back to the website. Admins land on `/admin` after sign-in, everyone else on `/app`. The wordmark at the top of the sidebar links to the home page.

The courses area adds **Courses** to the sidebar (`courses.navigation.ts`, labelled from `navigation.courses.json`) and its badge in `courses.app-badges.ts`: `CourseProgressBadge` (`custom/courses/course-progress-badge.tsx`), the lessons the customer has finished out of every published lesson, from `completedLessonsQuery` (`src/modules/lesson-progress/use-cases/get-completed-lessons.ts`). The sidebar draws the badge an area adds for an entry's path (`appBadge` in `src/data/app-badges.ts`) in a `SidebarMenuBadge` next to its link.

## Pages

| Route           | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/app`          | `LicenseOffer` without an active licence, otherwise `LicenseStatusCard` (tier, status and a link to the licence page) and `GetStarted`, the steps to a first project; links to the [CLI guide](/docs/cli) (`ROUTES.DOCS_CLI`) and to support by email.                                           |
| `/app/license`  | `LicenseOffer` without an active licence, otherwise `LicenseCard` (key and CLI commands to copy, customer portal link, an upgrade link for each higher tier) and, once Polar has issued the key, `LicenseActivations`; a refunded or revoked licence keeps a card that says so, without the key. |
| `/app/settings` | **General**: `PreferencesCard`, the time zone of every date in the workspace, `EmailCard` and `DeleteAccountCard`. **Security**: the password, two-factor authentication, connected accounts and sessions. See [Settings](/docs/features/settings).                                              |

`LicenseOffer` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/license-offer.tsx`) reuses the landing page's `PricingTiers` with a `CheckoutButton` for each tier, which opens Polar's checkout over the page straight away, with `CheckoutNotice` under the cards (see [Licences](/docs/features/licences#consent-to-immediate-supply)), and ends with the sections that other areas add to it (`LICENSE_OFFER_SECTIONS` in `src/data/license-offer.ts`), each with its skeleton and the data its pages load. The courses area adds `CourseUnlocks` (`src/data/courses.license-offer.ts`): the published courses that have paid lessons, with the number of locked lessons in each, counted from D1 by `getCatalogue`. The block is left out while no course has a paid lesson. With a `tier` chosen, the cards fold into a **Compare tiers** disclosure under `ChosenTier`. A refunded licence keeps its card and gets the offer next to it.

`src/routes/app.index.tsx` belongs to the auth area, and the other areas add to the overview in `src/data/<area>.app-overview.ts`, which `src/data/app-overview.entries.ts` joins: the search params they read, which `appOverviewSearchSchema` (`src/data/app-overview.ts`) validates, and their sections, each with its skeleton and the data the page loads. The licence area adds `LicenseOverview` (the activation panel, the chosen tier, `LicenseStatusCard` or the offer) and the two params of `licenseSearchSchema` (`src/data/license.ts`), which `/app/license` validates as well. Both are optional and ignored when invalid:

| Param        | Value                  | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `tier`       | a `LICENSE_TIER` value | Without an active licence, `ChosenTier` above the offer: the tier, its price and a **Continue to checkout** button. With an active licence of a lower tier, `UpgradeOffer`: the price of the [upgrade](/docs/features/licences#upgrades).   |
| `checkoutId` | a UUID from Polar      | `CheckoutActivation` instead of the offer until the licence is active, then a thank-you. With `tier`, the tier the checkout bought, it waits for that tier and the page leaves `UpgradeOffer` out. See [Licences](/docs/features/licences). |

The overview's loader runs the loads of its sections, so the licence area preloads `currentLicenseQuery` and the course catalogue there; `/app/license` preloads both and, once the licence has a key, `licenseActivationsQuery` for that key and tier, so a key that arrives by polling or an upgrade reads the machine limit afresh. `/app/settings` preloads the session list, the sign-in methods and the account deletion notices, and the layout's `NAMESPACES` include `auth.security` for the settings cards, `countries` for the countries of the sessions and `timezones` for the zone names. `/app/settings/delete-account` confirms an account deletion from its emailed link ([Settings](/docs/features/settings#delete-account)). Checkout, webhooks and licence states are described in [Licences](/docs/features/licences).

## CLI instructions

`LicenseCard` lists the commands in `CLI_KEY_LINES`, <RepositoryFact name="cliLicenseCommands" />, each with what it does and a copy button, and says that CI reads the key from <RepositoryFact name="cliLicenseKeyVariable" />. The commands come from `src/data/cli.ts`, which also builds the landing page's <RepositoryFact name="scaffoldTarget" />, and the variable from `CLI_VARIABLE` in `@saasyland/stack/cli`, the name the CLI itself reads. `CLI_RELEASE_CHANNEL` names the npm tag that the CLI runs from. The CLI and `@saasyland/stack` are released together under one version, so `cli.test.ts` fails unless the tag is the one that the installed `@saasyland/stack` was published on: `beta` for a prerelease `x.y.z-beta.n`, npm's default tag for a stable release. Upgrading `@saasyland/stack` to a stable release therefore fails the tests until `CLI_RELEASE_CHANNEL` names the default tag, and the licence page, the landing page, the docs and the [journey suite](/docs/tooling/playwright#journey-suite) follow it. It is a constant rather than an import of `@saasyland/stack/package.json`, which would put the whole file in the Worker and in every page's bundle.

## Skeletons

`AppOverviewPending` and `AppSettingsPending` (`app/auth-pending.tsx`, declared by the auth area in `src/data/auth.skeletons.ts`) draw the page header from `app/pending-blocks.tsx`. Below it, the overview draws the skeleton of each section the areas add, then the docs and support steps, the settings page draws the tabs and the cards of the General tab, and `AppDeleteAccountPending` draws the deletion card. `LicenseOverviewPending`, the skeleton of the licence section, and `AppLicensePending` (`app/license-pending.tsx`; the licence area declares `AppLicensePending` in `src/data/license.skeletons.ts`) mirror the state the page will show: they read the search params with `licenseSearchSchema` and the cached licence and catalogue from the query client, and draw the activation panel, the chosen tier or the upgrade, the tier grid (or, with a chosen tier, the **Compare tiers** line) with one row per course with paid lessons, or the licence cards. Once a licence is active, they also draw the first steps, and the licence card's skeleton follows the licence's status.

There are no billing history or course screens in `/app`, and `/app/settings` has no profile form: the name is set at sign-up.
