- Changed: one serializer, `toScriptJson` in `src/lib/script-json.ts`, now writes every JSON value that becomes code: the docs, blog and search index modules that the build generates, and every JSON-LD script. It escapes `<`, `>`, `&`, U+2028 and U+2029, where the JSON-LD used to escape `<` alone. See [SEO](/docs/features/seo#structured-data) and [Fumadocs](/docs/integrations/fumadocs#content-indexes).
