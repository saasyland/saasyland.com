- Affiliate programme: links with a referral parameter load Affonso's script for that visit only, and checkout passes the `affonso_referral` cookie to Polar as order metadata. Tracking turns on with the `AFFONSO_PROGRAM_ID` Worker variable. `/affiliates` publishes the terms from `AFFILIATE_TERMS`, and the privacy policy describes the referral cookie. See [Affiliate programme](/docs/features/affiliates).
