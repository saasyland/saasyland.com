`/admin/pricing-models` renders `PricingModelStats` and `PricingModelsGrid` from `ADMIN_PRICING_STATS` and `ADMIN_PRICING_MODELS` in `src/data/admin.ts`. The filter and **Create model** buttons are not wired and nothing is stored.

## Where prices come from

- **Landing page**: `TIER_PRICES` in `src/data/marketing.ts`: `core` <TierPrice tier="core" />, `complete` <TierPrice tier="complete" />, `agency` <TierPrice tier="agency" />. `Price` formats them in the page locale. The landing page description takes the Core price as `{price}`, and blog posts and docs pages print a tier's price with `<TierPrice tier="core" />`, so no copy types a price. The consulting card's hourly rate is `CONSULTING_HOURLY_RATE` in the same file; the call is booked by email rather than sold through Polar, so it has no regional price.
- **Checkout**: the Polar products referenced by `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_CORE`, `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_COMPLETE` and `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_AGENCY`.
- **Regional discounts**: `PPP_TIERS` in `src/modules/_core/constants/pricing.ts`, a price multiplier per group of countries, matched at checkout against automatic percentage discounts configured in Polar:

<RepositoryTable name="pppTiers" columns={["Price multiplier", "Countries"]} />

Keep `TIER_PRICES`, the Polar product prices and the Polar discounts in sync; the code does not compare them.
