- Fixed: forms no longer mark a field invalid while the visitor is still typing in it. A field shows its error once the visitor leaves it or tries to submit, and from then on the error clears on the keystroke that fixes the value. This applies to every form on the site, in the customer workspace and in the admin console. See [Forms and tables](/docs/conventions/forms-and-tables).
- Fixed: submitting a form with mistakes shows every error at once and moves focus to the first field to fix. Submit buttons stay enabled, so pressing one always explains what is missing.
- Changed: sign-up asks for a name, an email and one password. The confirmation field is gone, because the eye button shows what was typed, and the empty password field no longer looks filled in. Reset password and the change-password card of the account settings work the same way.
- Changed: a password needs at least 12 characters and nothing else: no uppercase or special character rules, as OWASP and NIST advise. When the visitor continues, the password is checked against known data breaches with Have I Been Pwned, and a breached one is reported under the password field. This also applies to reset password, the change-password card and passwords set by an admin. See [Authentication](/docs/features/authentication).
- Changed: names can be up to 100 characters long, so long real names fit.
- Added: a visitor who picked a licence on the pricing page sees it on the sign-up page with its price, "Step 1 of 2: create your account, then pay once with Polar", and the refund terms.
- Added: after a misspelt email domain such as gmial.com, the sign-up form suggests the likely address, which one press fills in.
- Changed: sign-up no longer celebrates with confetti before anything is bought, and its message now says that an email to continue is on its way.
- Changed: `better-auth` 1.7.27, with a patch to its breach-check plugin that upstream does not ship yet. See [Dependencies](/docs/conventions/dependencies).
