# Support

- **Documentation.** [saasyland.com/docs](https://saasyland.com/docs) covers setup, architecture, features and the conventions.
- **Bugs.** Search the [issues](https://github.com/saasyland/saasyland.com/issues), then open a [bug report](https://github.com/saasyland/saasyland.com/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
- **Ideas.** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/saasyland/saasyland.com/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml).
- **Customers.** For the paid CLI, licence keys, activations, orders and refunds, email hello@saasyland.com from the address you bought with and include your order reference.
- **Security vulnerabilities.** Report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
