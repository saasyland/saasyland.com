- Settings: the time zone is a working preference. The new `/app/settings` page and the **General** tab of `/admin/settings` share `PreferencesCard`, which searches every time zone by name, offers **Detect from this browser** and saves through the new `updatePreferences` use case. `TranslationsProvider` shows every date in the signed-in user's zone, and times carry the zone abbreviation. See [Settings](/docs/features/settings).
- Dates use the named formats in `I18N.FORMATS` through use-intl's `useFormatter`; `useDateFormatter` is removed. Dates without a time, such as blog post dates, stay in UTC, and admin user tables show dates in the viewer's zone. See [Internationalization and locales](/docs/features/internationalization).
- Removed: the unconnected language select and announcements switch of the admin preferences card. The language stays the locale in the URL.
- Fixed: some French and German time zone names showed a historical UTC offset, such as Ho Chi Minh at UTC+08:00, instead of the zone's name.
