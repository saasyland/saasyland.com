## Data

`src/modules/product/product.schema.ts` defines the `product` table: `name`, `description`, `priceCents`, `currency`, `type` (`one_time`, `subscription`, `course`), `status` (`draft`, `published`, `archived`), `billingCycle` and timestamps.

Use cases in `src/modules/product/use-cases`, each behind the matching `product` permission:

- `getProducts` — paginated, with optional `type` and `status` filters; exports `getProductsPageQuery` and `getProductsQuery`.
- `getProduct`, `createProduct`, `updateProduct`, `deleteProduct`.

## Admin screen

`/admin/products` keeps its tab in the `?tab=` search param: `all`, `onetime`, `subscriptions`, `categories`, `collections` and `drafts`. Product tabs render `ProductGrid` (`product-grid.tsx`), the `DataGrid` with `useProductColumns`, and add the tab's type or status to every request:

```tsx
<DataGrid
  columns={columns}
  empty={t("empty")}
  id={PRODUCT_GRID_ID}
  label={label}
  query={(request) => getProductsPageQuery(productZodSchemas.getProducts.parse({ ...request, ...preset }))}
  search={t("search")}
/>
```

`getProducts` searches the name and the description, filters by name, type, status, price in the product's currency and the created and updated days, and sorts by every column on the server. `categories` and `collections` render `CategoryGrid` (`category-grid.tsx`) with `useCategoryColumns` and `getCategoriesPageQuery` (see [Categories](/docs/features/categories)). Courses have their own screen, `/admin/courses` (see [Courses](/docs/features/courses)). The dashboard counter reads `getProductsQuery` too.

The list is read-only. Its columns are the product, its type, its price, its status and the day it changed, with the day it was created in the Columns menu; there is no create form and no row menu, because no screen is wired to `createProduct`, `updateProduct`, `deleteProduct` or `getProduct` yet, and a sales column would need a link between these rows and Polar's products that the table does not have.

`getProducts` checks each row's `currency` with `currencyCodeField` against the ISO 4217 codes of `CURRENCIES`, so the list fails rather than show a price in an unknown currency. The price column converts `priceCents` to the currency's major units with `fromMinorUnits` and formats the amount for the page's locale: on an English page, 4900 minor units show as $49.00 in USD, and as ¥4,900 in JPY, whose exponent is 0.

Checkout does not use this table: purchasable tiers are Polar products (see [Licences](/docs/features/licences)).
