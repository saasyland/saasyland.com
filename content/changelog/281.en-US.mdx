- Changed: the buy and upgrade buttons open Polar's checkout over the page instead of leaving the site. The focus moves into the checkout and back to the button when the buyer closes it, a payment shows the activation and the first steps without a reload, and a browser that cannot show the checkout over the page goes to Polar's checkout page as before. See [Licences](/docs/features/licences#the-checkout-over-the-page).
- Changed: the Content Security Policy lets pages frame only Polar's checkout, `https://polar.sh` in production and `https://sandbox.polar.sh` with the sandbox, and the build fails when a page loads Polar's checkout script before a buyer starts a checkout. See [Security and hardening](/docs/features/security).
- Added: `polar:verify` checks that the organisation lets every host of the environment embed its checkout. See [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#embedding).
- Changed: the journey suite pays in Polar's checkout over the licence page and checks the messages it sends. See [Journey suite](/docs/tooling/playwright#journey-suite).
- Changed: the guide [Content Security Policy with Nonces on Cloudflare Workers](/blog/content-security-policy-nonces-cloudflare-workers) describes the embedded checkout and the `frame-src` it needs.
