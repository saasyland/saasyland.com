- Added: every blog post can be shared from under its title and from its end: copy its link, open the system share sheet where the browser has one, or share it on X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Hacker News, Reddit or by email. The links load no script from those sites, set no cookie and add no tracking parameter, so the post's own address is what gets shared. See [Blog](/docs/features/blog#sharing).
- Added: every post ends with its author, linked to the author's profile, and with the related posts that share its tags.
- Added: the blog has an RSS feed in every language, linked from the blog and named in the head of every post, so feed readers find it. See [Blog](/docs/features/blog#feed).
- Changed: posts tell search engines and social networks who wrote them and what they are about, describe themselves by their search description instead of their longer excerpt, and the organisation now has a logo in its structured data. See [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo).
- Changed: the author's photo is served from saasyland.com instead of GitHub, so reading a post sends no request to another site.
