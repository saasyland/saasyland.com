- D1 keeps copies of Polar's orders and subscriptions in two new tables of the polar area, `order` and `subscription` (`src/modules/order/` and `src/modules/subscription/`), for the order lists, searches and access checks that will read them; nothing writes them yet. Each row is Polar's record under Polar's ID and with Polar's field names, `changed_at` holds the version of Polar's record and `placed_at` when Polar created the order, and deleting an account keeps its orders and subscriptions and only empties their `user_id`. The migration only creates the two tables and their indexes: `migrations.test.ts` fails if it holds any other statement or changes a table or index that existed before it. `polar.constants.ts` names every order status, subscription status and recurring interval of Polar's SDK, and a type test fails when the SDK adds one; `POLAR_ID_MAX_LENGTH` moved there from `license.constants.ts`, and the currency columns take their length from `CURRENCY_CODE_LENGTH`. See [Payments](/docs/features/payments).
