- Fixed: on wide screens, the blog's side panel keeps the search, the active filters, the sort and the topics in place, and only the index of posts scrolls, under its heading and count, filling the rest of the window. Every topic opens in a popover that scrolls on its own, and the topic list keeps its height when you choose a topic. On a very short window the index keeps room for a few posts, and the sort and topics scroll with it under the search. See [Blog](/docs/features/blog#the-finder).
