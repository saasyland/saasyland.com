- The polar area owns the Polar plugin, and the areas handle Polar's webhook events through a list of their own. `src/data/polar.auth-plugins.ts` registers the plugin with `webhooks({ onPayload: handlePolarEvent })`, and `handlePolarEvent` (`src/data/polar-events.ts`) hands each event, all at once, to every area that lists it in `src/data/<area>.polar-events.ts`; when one fails, the delivery answers 500 and Polar sends it again. The licence area lists its four events in `src/data/license.polar-events.ts` and passes each to the unchanged `licenseWebhookHandlers`, so licences are granted, refunded and revoked as before. `polar:verify` checks the webhook endpoint against `POLAR_EVENTS`, the events every area handles. `polar.constants.ts` no longer reads the environment, so the browser suite takes Polar's order statuses from it; `POLAR_PRODUCT_IDS` moved to the licence area's `license.utils.ts`, and `API_AUTH_POLAR` and `API_AUTH_CUSTOMER_PORTAL` to `src/data/polar.routes.ts`. An `overrides` entry gives `@polar-sh/adapter-utils` the application's `@polar-sh/sdk` 1.1.0, so the plugin's types accept the payload it validates. `verify-build.ts` now fails a build whose Worker would load Polar, Drizzle or Resend, or more than `BOOT_CLOSURE_MAX_BYTES`, on a cold start. The docs split [Payments](/docs/features/payments) from a new [Licences](/docs/features/licences) page.
