- The platform follows the areas. `src/server.ts`, `vite.config.ts`, the root route and the deploy scripts name no area: the Worker runs the request and document handlers that the areas declare in `src/data/<area>.worker.ts` (the courses area's signed media and the licence area's regional pricing), the root route mounts the components in `src/data/<area>.root.ts` (the affiliate referral), Vite loads the plugins in `src/data/<area>.vite.ts`, among them TanStack Start's prerender filter and the Polar SDK's optional peers, and `deploy:preview` and `deploy:production` run `polar:verify`, declared in `src/data/polar.deploy.ts`, through `scripts/run-deploy-checks.ts`. The preview host is `APP_PREVIEW_DOMAIN` in the branding, the secrets in `wrangler.jsonc` are grouped by module in the blocks of `.env.example`, and `src/types` declares only what Wrangler cannot derive, one file per area. A copy of the project without the affiliate referral and the signed media handler type-checks, builds and keeps 100% coverage, and no application or vendor module joins the Worker's cold-start imports. See [What the areas add](/docs/architecture/platform-layer#what-the-worker-and-the-build-run).
