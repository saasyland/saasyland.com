SaaSy Land 2.0 is an open-source **SaaS starter kit** maintained as a real product codebase — modular, typed, and opinionated about boundaries.

It is for teams who want months of auth, admin, i18n, and platform plumbing without inheriting an unmaintainable boilerplate maze.
