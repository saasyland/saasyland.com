- Added: `/admin/users/all` is a data grid that searches names and emails and filters by role, status, email verification, two-factor authentication, time zone and the created and updated days on the server, with each user's actions pinned to the right. See [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin).
- Added: the Customers tab searches every customer and filters by email verification, order count and the sign-up and last-order days on the server, sorts by customer, sign-up, orders and last order, and exports the list to CSV. See [Orders and customers](/docs/features/orders).
- Added: the open invitations on `/admin/users/invitations` are a data grid that searches emails and inviters, filters by role, status and the sent and expiry days, and keeps **Resend** and **Revoke** in a menu at the end of each row. See [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin).
