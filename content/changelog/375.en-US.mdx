- Changed: the header, its mobile menu and the footer read their menus from the new `useSiteNavigation`, which shows **Your account** with a link to the visitor's workspace in place of **Sign in**, and takes that workspace from the one hook in its `WORKSPACE_HOOKS`, `useVisitorWorkspace`. A project without sign-in has no such hook, and its menus stay as listed. See [Paths and navigation](/docs/conventions/routes#paths-and-navigation).
- Changed: `useErrorMessage` finds the namespace of an error's message in one `ERROR_NAMESPACES` map, which spreads `AUTH_ERRORS` beside `ERROR_CODES`; `getRouter` passes every failed query and mutation to each handler in `QUERY_ERROR_HANDLERS`, today `recheckRefusedSession`; and the footer's copyright spans the distinct years of `APP_FOUNDED_YEAR` and `REPOSITORY_FACTS.buildYear`. Each names its feature in one element, so `bun run check:areas` finds nothing left in the header, the footer, the error messages or the router that leaving out sign-in or `REPOSITORY_FACTS` cannot remove. See [Platform and testing](/docs/architecture/platform-layer#what-the-worker-and-the-build-run).
