Every page describes itself to search engines and link previews through its route's `head`. The build turns those descriptions into `sitemap.xml` and a social card per prerendered page, so both are static files in `dist/client` and nothing about them runs in the Worker.

## Page metadata

`buildPageHead` in `src/lib/seo.ts` builds the head of every page. Most routes call it through `pageHead(ROUTES.X)`; docs pages, blog posts and course pages call it directly. It emits:

- the title, with the locale's separator and `APP_NAME`, the description, the canonical URL and an `alternate` link with `hreflang` for every locale and for `x-default`;
- Open Graph: `og:site_name`, `og:title` (the page title without the site name, as Open Graph expects), `og:description`, `og:type`, `og:url`, `og:locale` and, for a page that belongs to a section, `article:section`;
- the social image: `og:image`, `og:image:type`, `og:image:width` and `og:image:height` (<RepositoryFact name="socialImageWidth" /> by <RepositoryFact name="socialImageHeight" /> pixels) and `og:image:alt`, with `twitter:card` set to `summary_large_image`, `twitter:title`, `twitter:description`, `twitter:image` and `twitter:image:alt`.

The root route adds `og:locale:alternate` for the other locales. In every build mode except `production` it also adds the `robots` directive `ROBOTS.NON_PRODUCTION` (`noindex, nofollow`), which keeps preview and test deployments out of search results.

A route keeps itself out of search results with `pageHead(ROUTES.X, { indexable: false })`, which adds `ROBOTS.NOINDEX` (`noindex`). `/premium`, a placeholder page, does this.

Docs pages name the section their breadcrumb shows: `getDocsPage` in `fumadocs.docs.ts` reads it with Fumadocs' `getBreadcrumbItems`, so `/docs` and the first page of each section have none. Blog posts name the blog, with the title from `pages.blog`.

## Social cards

Each prerendered page has its own card at `/images/og/<canonical path>.png`, and the home page at `/images/og/index.png` (`socialImagePath`). The card shows the wordmark, the section in the accent colour when there is one, the page's `og:title` and the domain, over the grid and glow of the site's backgrounds. Its alt text is that text in the page's locale: the title, the section and `APP_NAME`, joined by the locale's title separator.

Pages that are not prerendered, <RepositoryFact name="unlistedRoutes" /> and every path below them, share `/images/og/site.png`, which shows the wordmark alone and has `APP_NAME` as its alt text. `UNLISTED_ROUTES` in `src/routes.ts` is the one list of them: `vite.config.ts` leaves them out of prerendering and `buildPageHead` gives them the site card.

The cards are React components, `PageCard` and `SiteCard` in `src/presentation/social-card.tsx`. `createSocialCardRenderer` in `src/integrations/takumi/takumi.render.tsx` draws them with [Takumi](https://takumi.kane.tw/docs/) (`@takumi-rs/core`), a Rust renderer with native Node bindings that needs no browser:

- The colours are the light theme's tokens. The renderer reads `src/presentation/styles/globals.css`, so `var(--background)`, `var(--foreground)`, `var(--ring)` and the `field-grid` and `field-signal` classes are the site's own.
- The fonts are the files the site serves. `takumi.fonts.ts` reads every `@font-face` rule with a `url()` source from `fonts.css`, the Geist and Geist Mono subsets with their unicode ranges, and from `@fontsource-variable/noto-sans-jp`, because the site leaves Japanese to the system font. Takumi loads only the subsets a card's text uses. Each face is registered under `APP_NAME` followed by its family name: Takumi bundles its own Latin-only Geist, which would otherwise be chosen instead of the site's Cyrillic and Latin Extended subsets.
- The logo is `APP_LOGO_PATH` from `src/presentation/branding`, the path `Wordmark` draws.

To change the design, edit `social-card.tsx`, run `bun run build:test` and open the cards in `dist/client/images/og`.

## Sitemap and robots.txt

`public/robots.txt` allows every path and names `https://saasyland.com/sitemap.xml`; `robots.test.ts` fails when that line does not match `APP_URL` and `SITEMAP_PATH`.

`scripts/generate-seo-files.ts` runs at the end of every build script, after `optimize-prerendered-html.ts`. It reads the head of every prerendered page in `dist/client` with `readPageHead` from `src/lib/page-head.ts`, then:

1. Writes `dist/client/sitemap.xml` with every page whose `robots` directive allows indexing and whose path `robots.txt` does not disallow (`listedPages` and `buildSitemap` in `src/lib/sitemap.ts`, with the RFC 9309 matching of `src/lib/robots.ts`). Each `<url>` lists the page's own `alternate` links as `xhtml:link` entries, `x-default` included. The build fails when a page names an alternate that the sitemap does not list, or when a URL appears twice. `ROBOTS.NON_PRODUCTION` does not count as a page's own directive, so every build mode writes the same sitemap.
2. Renders the site card and every page's card into `dist/client/images/og`, and fails when two pages name the same image.

It prints how many pages the sitemap lists, how many cards it rendered and how long it took. The sitemap has no `lastmod`, because docs pages carry no reliable modification date. Course pages are not in it: they are read from D1 on each request, and the sitemap is a file written by the build.

The Worker does not change. Takumi and the script run only at build time, and `src/server.ts` serves `sitemap.xml` and the cards from static assets like any other file, with the cache policy `public/_headers` sets for `/images/*`.

## Tests

- `seo.test.ts`, `page-head.test.tsx`, `sitemap.test.ts` and `robots.test.ts` in `src/lib/__test__` cover the tags, reading them back, the sitemap and the robots rules. `takumi.render.test.ts` renders cards in English, German, Ukrainian and Japanese and checks their size.
- `e2e/specs/seo.spec.ts` compares the served sitemap with the prerendered pages that allow indexing, requests every URL in it and expects `200` without a redirect, and opens the home page, a docs page and a blog post in English and Japanese to check their social tags and the image they name.
- After a deploy, `verify-deployment.ts` requests <RepositoryFact name="liveSiteCheckPaths" />, the sitemap and the site card among them.
