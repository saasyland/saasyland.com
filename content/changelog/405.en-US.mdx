- Changed: for a signed-in visitor, the header, the phone menu and the footer show **Your account** from the first paint instead of switching from **Sign in** after the page has loaded, and **Pricing**, **Courses**, **Blog** and **Docs** no longer move when they do. The Worker marks the page from the session cookie before it leaves, and the link holds both labels in one place. See [Paths and navigation](/docs/conventions/routes#paths-and-navigation) and [Performance](/docs/conventions/performance#the-header-knows-the-session-before-its-scripts).
- Changed: a visitor who has signed out no longer asks the server for a session on every page view. A session that ended elsewhere turns the link back to **Sign in** in the same place, and its pages ask no more after the first. See [Caching and SSR](/docs/architecture/caching-and-ssr#prerendering).
