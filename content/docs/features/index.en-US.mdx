Every page in this chapter describes the code as it is. A feature is **live** when it reads or writes real data through a module use case, Better Auth or Polar. Every admin screen is live: a screen whose data has no source yet is not built, and a lint rule keeps demo data out of admin code (see [Admin dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard#real-data-only)).

## Live

- Accounts: [Authentication](/docs/features/authentication), [OAuth providers](/docs/features/oauth), [Email verification](/docs/features/email-verification), [Password reset](/docs/features/password-reset), [Two-factor authentication](/docs/features/two-factor), [Sessions](/docs/features/sessions), [Impersonation](/docs/features/impersonation), [Account](/docs/features/account).
- Admin: the [dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard) with its counters, revenue, new users, course progress, licences by tier and users table, the **All users** tab of [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin), the product and category lists of [Products](/docs/features/products) and [Categories](/docs/features/categories), [Settings](/docs/features/settings), the course editor of [Courses](/docs/features/courses), and the video library of [Videos](/docs/features/videos).
- Customers: the [Customer app](/docs/features/customer-app) with Polar checkout and licences ([Licences](/docs/features/licences), [Payments](/docs/features/payments)), and the [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api) that activates the CLI on customers' machines.
- Courses: the public [course catalogue and player](/docs/features/courses), with lessons that are free, need an account or need a Complete or Agency licence ([Content access](/docs/features/content-access)), and saved watch progress.
- Public site: [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page) with regional pricing and newsletter, [Blog](/docs/features/blog), [Docs site](/docs/features/docs-site), [Legal pages](/docs/features/legal-pages), and the metadata, sitemap and social cards of [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo).
- Affiliates: the [Affiliate programme](/docs/features/affiliates), with referrals tracked by Affonso, credited on the Polar order and terms published on `/affiliates`.
- Platform: [Internationalization](/docs/features/internationalization), [Transactional email](/docs/features/email), [HTTP handlers](/docs/features/api-surface), [Security](/docs/features/security).

## Not implemented

- [Organizations](/docs/features/organizations): no teams, memberships or tenant scoping.
- [Analytics](/docs/features/analytics): no page analytics; the admin dashboard shows revenue and sign-ups from D1.
- Admin screens for [pricing models](/docs/features/pricing-models), payments, blog posts, the landing page, invitations and roles: none yet. Prices are set in code and in Polar, posts in Git.
