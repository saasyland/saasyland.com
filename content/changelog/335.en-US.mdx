- Added: `/admin/courses` is a data grid that searches the titles in every language and the slugs, filters by type, status, lesson count and the day of the last change, and sorts on the server; the arrows still move a course within the whole catalogue. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses).
- Added: the product, category and collection tabs of `/admin/products` are data grids that search names and descriptions, filter by type, status, price in each product's currency, visibility and the created and updated days, sort by every column and export to CSV.
