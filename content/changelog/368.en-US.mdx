- Added: the admin course list orders the catalogue by drag and drop. Each row starts with a checkbox, a grip handle and the course's place in the catalogue (`#`). Dragging a course by its handle, or pressing the up or down arrow on a focused handle, moves it to any place in the whole order, across pages, while the list is neither sorted nor filtered, and `moveCourse` saves that place. Selected courses are deleted together after one confirmation. See [Data grid](/docs/ui/data-grid#row-order).
- Changed: every admin table and list ends a row with one ⋯ menu instead of a row of icon buttons: courses (**Edit**, **View on site**, **Copy link**, **Delete**), users, invitations, and the chapters, lessons and materials in the course editor. Lesson materials are reordered by dragging, like the curriculum. Products, categories, collections, customers, invitations and orders without a licence get checkboxes to export the selected rows.
- Fixed: table headers stay on one line instead of breaking inside a word, as the Lessons header did, and every admin table's columns fit their headers in every language; a browser test checks every admin table in English and Ukrainian.
