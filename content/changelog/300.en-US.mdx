- Added: `/admin/orders` shows each order's discount with its code and amount and its billing country, and filters by any discount, no discount, one discount and the billing country. The order page shows the same terms from the D1 copy beside Polar's own. See [Orders and customers](/docs/features/orders).
- Added: `bun run orders:backfill:<local|preview|production>` copies the discount and the billing country of orders mirrored before this release from Polar's API, once, without overwriting what a webhook has already copied. Until then those orders say Not copied yet. See [Database](/docs/tooling/database#discounts-and-billing-countries-of-older-orders).
- Added: the admin's Customers tab has a Machines column, such as 2 of 5 machines in use, that opens the list of machines with their label, system and activation time, and the licence card on a user's profile lists the same machines. They are read from Polar, and when Polar does not answer the rest of the page stays and says so. See [Orders and customers](/docs/features/orders#customers) and [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#profile).
- Added: the licence card on a user's profile names the first paid lesson the licence opened and its course.
- Changed: with no filter set and no order copied yet, `/admin/orders` says that no orders exist yet instead of showing an empty table.
