- Fixed: when one area failed on a Polar webhook event, `handlePolarEvent` rejected at once, so the webhook answered 500 while the other areas were still writing, and Polar's retry could start from an area that had stopped halfway. It now waits for every area to finish and then rejects with an `AggregateError` of every failure, so Polar still sends the event again, after each area has finished. See [Payments](/docs/features/payments#webhooks).
