- Changed: blog posts no longer add an `FAQPage` to their structured data. Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026, the landing page already left it out, and every question and answer stays on the page, in the prerendered HTML. See [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo#structured-data).
- Added: the Polish glossary has the terms of the CLI's new options, such as „wiadomość do wszystkich” for a broadcast message, „cofnąć dostęp” for revoking access, „blokada konta” for a ban and „podmienić timery” for fake timers, while „haczyk” and „gniazdo” stay allowed where they mean a catch and a TCP socket. See [Polish style guide](/docs/conventions/polish-style).
- Fixed: the Polish testing guide and changelog write „podmienione timery” instead of the calque „fałszywe timery”.
