- The course block on the landing page shows the published courses instead of a typed lesson list: `getCourseShowcase` returns the first two courses with their first six lessons and each lesson's access level, every course and lesson links to its page, and **Watch a free lesson** opens the first free preview with a video. The browser loads it once the block scrolls near, so the prerendered page stays the same for every visitor. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses).
- The course block plays `course-tour`, a new silent Remotion composition that tours the course pages, on request with the browser's controls. It ships as WebM with an MP4 fallback and WebP and AVIF posters at 1600 and 800 pixels wide.
