- Added: a course has a level, an icon, what learners will be able to do and its prerequisites, and a chapter has a summary, all written in every language in the admin, which is the single place to edit courses. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses#course-details).
- Added: a lesson editor at `/admin/courses/<courseId>/lessons/<lessonId>` writes a lesson's description, with a preview of its paragraphs and code, and its key moments, replaces its video in one step and manages its materials: files uploaded to R2 and links to the docs or to `https://` addresses. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses#lesson-editor).
- Added: lesson materials download through signed paths that expire, given only to visitors who may open the lesson. See [Courses](/docs/features/courses#materials).
- Added: a video can be marked as a placeholder in the video library. A lesson on a placeholder, or without a video, is listed as being recorded and does not play, and replacing its video with a real recording makes it play everywhere. See [Videos](/docs/features/videos#placeholder-videos).
