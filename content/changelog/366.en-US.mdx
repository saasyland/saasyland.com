- Changed: each area declares its menu entries in `src/areas/<area>.navigation.ts`, under the area's `name`, so no entry repeats its area. Menu entries stay a pure file kind of their own, because the Remotion project reads the admin menu. What an area adds to the pages, its namespaces in other areas' layouts, its time zone and its root components, moved to its browser file, `src/areas/<area>.ts`, which `src/areas/browser.ts` joins; the core's `src/areas/core.ts` holds only its name. A test now imports every area file first in a fresh module graph and checks that it is named after its file, and another checks that every area with menu entries has its `navigation.<area>.json` in every locale. See [Paths and navigation](/docs/conventions/routes#paths-and-navigation).
