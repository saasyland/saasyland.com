## Content

Posts live in `content/blog/<slug>.<locale>.mdx`, one file per locale. The frontmatter schema in `src/integrations/fumadocs/fumadocs.schema.ts` requires `title`, `authorName` and `date`, and accepts `description`, `authorImage`, `excerpt`, `faq`, `featured`, `image`, `published` (default `true`), `tags` and `updated`. A tag is one of `BLOG_TAGS` in `src/data/blog.ts`, and the schema rejects any other value; `PostRow` and `PostMeta` show each tag by its label under `tags` in `pages.blog`, so tags read in the page's language.

## Figures from the code

A post prints the values the code defines instead of typing them: `<RepositoryFact>` for counts, the coverage threshold (`coverageThreshold`), React's major version (`reactMajor`), the illustrative hourly rate (`illustrativeHourlyRate`, with `unit="currency"`) and the hours the Core price buys (`corePriceInHours`, with `unit="hour"`), and `<TierPrice>`, `<ActivationLimit>` and `<HeadStartTable />` for prices, machine limits and the head start. The `title` and the `faq` questions and answers are ICU messages: `formatPostText` in `src/lib/post-text.ts` formats them with `REPOSITORY_FACTS` and each tier's `<tier>Price` and `<tier>Machines`. `formatPostText` also passes `CLI_GENERATION_VALUES` from `src/data/cli.ts`, so a sentence that changes when project generation ships is written once as `{generation, select, available {…} other {…}}`, and `post-faq.test.ts` renders it with both statuses. It also passes `billingGeneration` (`BILLING_GENERATION` in `src/data/marketing.ts`), `affiliateProgram` and `affiliateHoldDays` from `src/data/affiliates.ts`, and `refundWithdrawalDays` (`REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS`), and the same test renders the selects on `billingGeneration` and `affiliateProgram` with both statuses. `toBlogPostSummary` formats the title, and `<PostFaq entries={frontmatter.faq} />` renders the FAQ that the post's FAQPage JSON-LD also reads. `post-faq.test.ts` formats every post's title, questions and answers in its locale.

## Source

Every build turns the posts into a blog index per locale (see [Fumadocs](/docs/integrations/fumadocs#content-indexes)): the published posts under `/blog`, newest first, with the same i18n settings as the docs, each with its frontmatter, its table of contents and its reading time, counted with `Intl.Segmenter` for that locale. `loadBlogIndex(locale)` imports a locale's index, a file under `/assets/`, and `toBlogPostSummary` in `fumadocs.blog.ts` formats a post's title and localizes its URL. The loaders below read it in the browser on a client navigation, so moving through the blog never reaches the Worker.

## Routes

- The `/blog` layout (`_site._marketing.blog.tsx`) sits in the `_site` and `_marketing` layouts, which add the site header, `PageFrame`, `Footer` and `BackToTop` around it (see [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page#navigation-and-back-to-top)). It loads `docs.css` for article typography. The header marks **Blog** as the current section on the index and on every post. Its loader preloads `fumadocs` and `pages.blog`.
- `/blog` (`_site._marketing.blog.index.tsx`) lists the posts of the blog index with `PostRow`.
- `/blog/$` (`_site._marketing.blog.$.tsx`) finds one post in the blog index (404 when it is missing or unpublished), preloads its compiled MDX with `blogContent`, and composes `PostMeta`, `PostToc` (or, on small screens, `InlineTOC` under the same title), `PostCta` and `PostShare` from `src/presentation/components/custom/blog`. It adds JSON-LD from `buildBlogPostStructuredDataHtml` and article metadata from `buildPageHead`, with the blog's title from `pages.blog` as its `article:section` and its own social card; see [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo). The post wraps its MDX in Fumadocs' `RootProvider`, with search and the theme switch off and the strings of `useFumadocsI18n`, so the copy buttons of code blocks and heading anchors and the FAQ's accordion items are labelled in the post's language.

The landing page Notes section shows three posts of the blog index, featured ones first, then the newest.

## Admin blog (demo)

`/admin/blog` and `/admin/blog/create` render demo posts and stats from `src/data/admin-blog.ts` and a static editor. They do not read or write `content/blog`.

## Checks

Run `bun run content` after adding a post. `bun scripts/check-content.ts` verifies that every locale exists, that titles, descriptions and excerpts are translated, and that code blocks are identical.
