title: "Legal pages"
description: "Privacy, terms, refunds, licence and affiliate terms pages, each composed by its own route in every supported language."
---

| Route         | Route file                                   | Namespace                |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| `/privacy`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.privacy.tsx`    | `pages.legal.privacy`    |
| `/terms`      | `src/routes/_site._marketing.terms.tsx`      | `pages.legal.terms`      |
| `/refunds`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.refunds.tsx`    | `pages.legal.refunds`    |
| `/licence`    | `src/routes/_site._marketing.licence.tsx`    | `pages.legal.licence`    |
| `/affiliates` | `src/routes/_site._marketing.affiliates.tsx` | `pages.legal.affiliates` |

Each route file defines its own page component, a static `NAMESPACE`, and a module constant with the section keys in display order:

```tsx
const SECTIONS = ["operator", "accounts", "purchases", "payments", "licence", "conduct", "refunds", "responsibility", "law"] as const
```

The page renders `title` and `description` through `ContentPage`, then `lastUpdated`, then `sections.<key>.title` and `sections.<key>.body` for every key. There is no shared legal component. The privacy policy and the affiliate terms pass the values from `AFFILIATE_TERMS` (`src/data/affiliates.ts`) to their messages, so the commission, cookie duration, hold period and minimum payout are never typed into copy.

## Editing

- Change copy in `messages/<locale>/pages.legal.<doc>.json` for every locale and run `bun run check:i18n`.
- To add a section, add its key to `SECTIONS` and its messages in every locale.
- The footer links are the footer entries of `LEGAL_NAVIGATION` in `src/data/legal.navigation.ts`, labelled from `navigation.legal.json`: the legal column, and the affiliate terms in the product column; the legal area adds the consent line under the sign-up form, `SignUpConsent` with its skeleton in `custom/sign-up-consent.tsx`, to `SIGN_UP_NOTICES` in `src/data/legal.sign-up-notices.ts`, so a project without legal pages has a sign-up form without links to them. `LegalPending` (`custom/legal-pending.tsx`) is the skeleton of all five pages, declared in `src/data/legal.skeletons.ts`.

The licence page separates the public repository's MIT licence from separately supplied paid material. The refund policy keeps mandatory consumer rights. Keep every document aligned with the real operator, checkout, providers and data handling.
