- Added: each course can have a cover. The **Cover** card in the course editor uploads, replaces and removes it; the browser crops the image to 16:9 and saves it at three widths in R2, and the Worker serves it from `/media/covers/` with a year-long immutable cache. The catalogue, the course page, the lesson page and the landing page's course block show it with the course title as its alternative text, and the course page shares it as its link preview. A course without a cover keeps its icon on a teal glow. See [Course covers](/docs/features/courses#course-covers).
- Changed: email addresses may have up to 254 characters, the RFC 5321 limit, in every form and on Better Auth's endpoints. The database columns keep their declared lengths, which SQLite does not enforce, so no table is rebuilt. See [Migrations](/docs/tooling/database#migrations).
