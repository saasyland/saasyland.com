`src/routes/app.settings.tsx` and `src/routes/admin.settings.tsx` render two tabs from `SETTINGS_TABS` (`src/data/auth.ts`), **General** and **Security**. The tab is the `tab` search param, validated by `settingsSearchSchema` and dropped from the address when it is the default, so an OAuth link returns to `?tab=security`. The cards live in `src/presentation/components/custom/settings`, share the `auth.security` namespace, which the `/app` and `/admin` layouts load, and serve customers and admins alike; only the customer's General tab ends with **Delete account**. Both loaders preload the session list (`getActiveSessionsQuery`) and the sign-in methods (`listAccountsQuery`), and `/app/settings` also runs the loads of the account deletion notices.

| Tab      | `/app/settings`                                                          | `/admin/settings`                                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General  | `PreferencesCard`, `EmailCard`, `DeleteAccountCard`                      | `BusinessProfileCard`, `BusinessLogoCard`, `PreferencesCard`, `EmailCard` |
| Security | `PasswordCard`, `TwoFactorCard`, `ConnectedAccountsCard`, `SessionsCard` | `PasswordCard`, `TwoFactorCard`, `ConnectedAccountsCard`, `SessionsCard`  |

## Business profile [#business-profile]

`/admin/settings` opens its General tab with two cards about the business that runs the site rather than the signed-in admin: `BusinessProfileCard` and `BusinessLogoCard`, both in `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/business-profile-card.tsx`. They read `businessProfileQuery`, which the page's loader prefetches, and every use case behind them checks `settings: ["manage"]`.

- The `business_profile` table (`src/modules/business-profile`) holds one row, `BUSINESS_PROFILE_ID`, which the migration that creates the table inserts. The name and the support email start as `APP_NAME` and `CONTACT_EMAIL`, the postal address is empty and there is no logo.
- **Save profile** calls `updateBusinessProfile`, which trims the three fields and validates them with `businessProfileZodSchemas.updateBusinessProfile`: a name of at most `BUSINESS_NAME_MAX_LENGTH` characters, a valid email and an address of at most `BUSINESS_ADDRESS_MAX_LENGTH` characters, kept line by line as typed.
- **Upload logo** scales the chosen image in the browser to fit `BUSINESS_LOGO_MAX_SIDE` on its longest side with `encodeContainedImage` (`src/lib/image-encoding.ts`), keeping its proportions and transparency, as WebP, or PNG in a browser that cannot encode WebP. `uploadBusinessLogo` (rate limited) stores it in R2 under `logos/<logoId>.<extension>`, records the key and deletes the logo it replaces; **Remove logo** calls `removeBusinessLogo`. `serveBusinessLogo` serves the logo under `/media/logos/`; see [Cloudflare R2](/docs/integrations/cloudflare-r2).
- Nothing outside these cards reads the profile yet: the footer, the emails, the legal pages and the structured data still print the constants of `src/presentation/branding`.

## Preferences

`PreferencesCard` (`src/presentation/components/custom/preferences-card.tsx`) edits the signed-in user's time zone, the one display preference stored with the account. It reads the saved zone from the current session query.

- A searchable combobox lists every zone in `TIMEZONE_CODES` (`src/modules/user/user.constants.ts`, derived from `TIMEZONES`) by its name in the `timezones` namespace, which the `/admin` and `/app` layouts load.
- **Detect from this browser** selects the zone the browser reports. It compares zones as `Intl.DateTimeFormat` resolves them, so an alias such as `Asia/Calcutta` finds `Asia/Kolkata`; a zone that is not in the list shows a message and keeps the current choice.
- **Save preferences** calls `updatePreferences` (`src/modules/user/use-cases/update-preferences.ts`, signed in), which validates the zone with `userZodSchemas.updatePreferences` and stores it through Better Auth's `updateUser`. The card then invalidates the current session query, and `TranslationsProvider` shows every date in the new zone ([Internationalization and locales](/docs/features/internationalization)).

The language is not stored: it is the locale in the URL, switched with `LocaleSelect`, and it also decides the date order, the 12- or 24-hour clock and the number separators.

## Email address

`EmailCard` shows the address and whether it is verified, and **Change email** opens `ChangeEmailForm` (`custom/auth/change-email-form.tsx`), the form the verification banner uses. It calls `changeEmail` (signed in, rate limited). A verified address changes in two steps: Better Auth emails the current address a confirmation link (`ChangeEmailConfirmationEmail`), and once it is opened, sends a verification link to the new address, which stores the change. Both links return to the page the change started on. An unverified address changes at once and gets a new verification link. When the new address already has an account, Better Auth sends nothing and answers as for any other address, so the toast says that the link is on its way if the address can be used.

## Password

`PasswordCard` reads the sign-in methods. With a password, it asks for the current and the new one and calls `changePassword` (signed in, rate limited), which always signs out every other session. An account that signs in only with GitHub or Google gets **Set a password** instead, which calls `setPassword` (signed in, rate limited): Better Auth's server-only `setPassword`, then `revokeOtherSessions`. Both forms tick off the length rule with `PasswordRequirements`, show a password found in a known data breach under the field, and end with `PasswordChangedEmail` to the account, sent by the `password-change-notice` hook for a change and by `setPassword` for a new password.

## Two-factor authentication

`TwoFactorCard` shows whether TOTP is on and opens `TwoFactorDialog` to turn it on, to turn it off or to create new backup codes, each after the current password. An account without a password is told to set one first, because the second factor guards the password sign-in. See [Two-factor authentication](/docs/features/two-factor).

## Connected accounts

`ConnectedAccountsCard` lists GitHub and Google from `OAUTH_PROVIDERS` with the date each was connected. **Connect** calls Better Auth's `linkSocial` from the browser, which goes through the provider and comes back to `?tab=security`; a failure comes back as `?error=` and shows its `auth.errors` message in the card. Better Auth links an account whose provider address differs from the account's own (`accountLinking.allowDifferentEmails`), because the signed-in owner starts the link. **Disconnect** calls `unlinkAccount` (signed in, rate limited) with the account's row ID. The card offers it only while the account has another way to sign in, and Better Auth refuses to unlink the last one in any case; an account whose only way in is a provider is told to set a password or connect another account first.

## Sessions

`SessionsCard` lists the signed-in browsers, this one first. See [Sessions](/docs/features/sessions).

## Delete account

`DeleteAccountCard` lists what deletion removes: the profile, the sign-in methods, the two-factor settings and the course progress, followed by the notices of `ACCOUNT_DELETION_NOTICES`, which `custom/settings/delete-account-card.tsx` declares beside the card. Each notice names its component and the data that both settings pages load for it, and each names a component of the [feature](/docs/conventions/testing#tests-follow-the-features) it belongs to, so a site built without that feature has no notice for it. The legal feature's `AccountDeletionRecords` comes first: payment and tax records, and the licence with its delivery records, are kept as the Privacy Policy describes, no longer linked to the account. The licence feature's `LicenseDeletionNotice` follows, shown while the account holds an active licence: the licence ends with the account, its key stops working, deleting refunds nothing, and the withdrawal function comes first.

**Delete account** opens a dialog that asks for the current password, or nothing for an account without one, and calls `requestAccountDeletion` (signed in, rate limited). Better Auth checks the password and calls `sendDeleteAccountVerification`, and `sendDeleteAccountEmail` emails a link to `/app/settings/delete-account?token=…` in the language of the page. The link works for `EMAIL_LINK_LIFETIME_HOURS`. That page (`src/routes/app.settings_.delete-account.tsx`) is behind the `/app` sign-in, repeats the consequences and deletes the account only when **Delete my account** is pressed: `confirmAccountDeletion` (signed in, rate limited as a token) passes the token to Better Auth's `deleteUser`, which deletes the user and its sessions and expires the cookie, and the page removes the account's data from the query cache, goes to the home page and says so. A link opened elsewhere asks for sign-in first, and a page without a token offers the way back to Settings. Opening the link does nothing by itself, so a mail scanner that follows links cannot delete an account.

Deleting the user cascades to its sessions, sign-in methods, two-factor row and course progress; its licence, the copies of Polar's orders and subscriptions and any withdrawal statement keep their rows with no user, as the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) says, and an active licence is revoked first ([Licences](/docs/features/licences#records-and-refunds)).

## Skeletons

`PreferencesCardPending`, in `preferences-card-pending.tsx`, and `SettingsTabsPending`, `EmailCardPending` and `DeleteAccountCardPending`, in `custom/settings/settings-pending.tsx`, draw `AdminSettingsPending`, `AppSettingsPending` and `AppDeleteAccountPending`. They have files of their own because every page loads the skeletons, and the cards' files hold the forms (see [Performance](/docs/conventions/performance#skeletons-stay-in-the-entry-script)). A skeleton draws the General tab. `BusinessProfileCardPending` and `BusinessLogoCardPending`, in `custom/admin/business-profile-pending.tsx`, open the admin's General tab in `AdminSettingsPending`.
