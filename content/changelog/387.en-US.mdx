- Added: `/admin/newsletter` lists every newsletter subscriber with their status, sign-up and confirmation days and language, searched and filtered on the server, and exports them to a CSV in which a cell that starts like a formula stays text. See [Newsletter](/docs/features/newsletter#subscribers).
- Added: the admin writes an issue of the build log in every language with a preview and sends it to the confirmed subscribers, each in their own language or in English. A retry, a second click or a resumed send never sends an email twice. See [Newsletter](/docs/features/newsletter#sending).
- Added: every issue links to its reader's unsubscribe page and carries the one-click `List-Unsubscribe` headers, so a mail client's own unsubscribe button unsubscribes at once. See [Newsletter](/docs/features/newsletter#unsubscribing).
- Changed: the footer, the confirmation page and email and the empty course catalogue now say that the build log arrives with its next issue.
