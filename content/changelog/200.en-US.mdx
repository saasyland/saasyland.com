- Every page loads less JavaScript: about 26 KiB less gzipped on `/admin`, `/app` and the docs, and 11 KiB on `/` and `/blog`, whose layouts load TanStack Form for the newsletter anyway. `PreferencesCardPending` was exported from `preferences-card.tsx`, so the `/admin` and `/app` skeletons, which are in every page's entry script, brought the preferences form, TanStack Form, the time zone list and the user schemas with them; it now has a file of its own, `preferences-card-pending.tsx`, and `pending-imports.test.ts` fails when a skeleton reaches `@tanstack/react-form`. Splitting the skeletons into chunks of their own was measured and rejected: `/`, `/app` and `/admin` still loaded every skeleton through `PAGE_SKELETONS`, and a client navigation waited for its skeleton's chunk. See [Skeletons stay in the entry script](/docs/conventions/performance#skeletons-stay-in-the-entry-script).
