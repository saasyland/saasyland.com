- Changed: what the areas add to the admin console and the customer workspace now sits in their browser files, `src/areas/<area>.ts`, under fields that the auth area declares on `BrowserArea`. These are the dashboard's statistics and panels, the command palette's searches, the user management tabs, the cards of a user's profile, the account deletion notices, the workspace's menu badges, the overview's sections and search params, and the sign-up form's intro and notices. Each page reads them from `BROWSER_AREAS`, and an entry takes its area from the area's `name`. The 42 registries, barrels and area files these points kept in `src/data` are gone, among them the auth area's empty lists; `src/data/dashboard.ts` still joins the dashboard. The components the areas add, apart from their skeletons, now load on demand through `lazyRouteComponent`, and the loader of each page preloads them, so every page loads less JavaScript up front. See [What the areas add](/docs/architecture/platform-layer#what-the-areas-add).
