A module exists only for a real database table. Each table is registered in `src/integrations/drizzle-orm/drizzle.schemas.ts`.

<RepositoryTable name="moduleUseCases" columns={["Module", "Tables", "Use cases"]} />

`_core` owns no table. It holds the error codes, HTTP constants, catalogues, Zod fields and use-case inputs described in [Core shared kernel](/docs/architecture/core).

## Files beyond the standard set

Most modules contain only `<table>.schema.ts`, `.zod.ts`, `.constants.ts`, `.types.ts`, `.utils.ts` and `use-cases/`. A few have more:

- `course/course.outline.ts`: `readCourseOutline`, a published course's chapters and lessons for a viewer, shared by `get-course` and `get-lesson`
- `course/course.curriculum.ts`: the curriculum as one list of chapter and lesson rows, the places a dragged chapter or lesson may go and the order a move leads to (`curriculumRows`, `canDropInCurriculum`, `moveInCurriculum`, `curriculumOrder`); pure
- `lesson/lesson.access.ts`: `hasLessonAccess` and the `Viewer` type, and who may open paid lessons: `COURSE_ACCESS_GRANTS`, with the licence's grant inline, `holdsCourseAccess` and `recordCourseAccessUse`
- `lesson/lesson.playable.ts`: `playableVideo`, the join to the video of a video lesson, and `isBeingRecorded`, which tells a video lesson without a video from a text lesson
- `lesson-attachment/lesson-attachment.files.ts`: the R2 object keys of uploaded files, their safe names and the content types that may be uploaded and served
- `lesson-attachment/lesson-attachment.download.ts`: signed download paths (`signDownload`, `verifyDownload`)
- `lesson-attachment/lesson-attachment.delivery.ts`: `serveAttachment`, which answers `/media/downloads/` requests from R2
- `lesson-attachment/lesson-attachment.objects.ts`: the uploaded files of the lessons being deleted, and their removal from R2
- `lesson/lesson.viewer.ts`: `resolveViewer`, which builds the viewer from the session, the role and the course access of `COURSE_ACCESS_GRANTS`, and `recordPaidLessonOpened`, which records a paid lesson that course access opened
- `license/license.webhooks.ts`: the Polar webhook handlers passed to Better Auth
- `license/license.api.ts`: `licenseApiHandlers`, the CLI licence API that `src/routes/api/cli.v1.*.ts` serve
- `license/license.bundles.ts`: the R2 key and the signed URL of a release's template bundle (`bundleObjectKey`, `signBundle`, `verifyBundle`)
- `license/license.delivery.ts`: `serveBundle`, which answers `/bundles/` requests from R2
- `license/license.ppp.ts`: `buyerTierPrice`, the price checkout charges a buyer for a tier, regional price included
- `license/license.pricing.ts`: the regional pricing bands (`PPP_TIERS`), their multipliers and percentages, and the opt-out cookie
- `license/license.upgrade-price.ts`: `upgradePrice`, the higher tier's price less what the customer has paid for licences
- `license/license.refunds.ts`: `refundEligibility`, whether a purchase can be refunded now, from its consent and its first use: the first template download or the first paid lesson
- `withdrawal/withdrawal.contracts.ts`: `matchContract` and `accountContract`, the active licence a withdrawal statement names and its refund terms at that moment
- `video/video.files.ts`: the R2 object keys and the files that may be stored and served, with their media types
- `video/video.playback.ts`: signed playback paths (`createPlayback`, `verifyPlayback`)
- `video/video.delivery.ts`: `serveMedia`, which answers `/media/` requests from the Workers cache or R2
- `video/video.encoding.ts`: the FFmpeg plan (renditions, arguments, storyboard) that `scripts/publish-video.ts` and `scripts/encode-videos.ts` run
- `video/video.objects.ts`: `removeVideoObjects`, which deletes every file of a video from R2
- `video-upload/video-upload.files.ts`: the R2 key of an upload's source, the recording types it accepts, its parts and their form data
- `video-upload/video-upload.objects.ts`: the uploads of the lessons being deleted, and their removal from R2, unfinished multipart uploads aborted
- `video-upload/video-upload.encoder.ts`: the queries the encoder runs on an upload (`queuedUploadsQuery`, `startEncodingQuery`, `failEncodingQuery`, `finishEncodingQueries`), shared by `scripts/encode-videos.ts` and the browser suite's test Worker
- `account/account.links.ts`: `accountEmailLinks`, the sign-in and password-reset links in the locale of an account email's callback
- `course/course.cover.ts`: the cover's R2 keys, sizes and widths, its upload form and the images a course page shows and shares (`courseCoverImage`, `courseShareImage`)
- `course/course.delivery.ts`: `serveCover`, which answers `/media/covers/` requests from the edge cache or R2
- `course/course.objects.ts`: `removeCoverObjects`, which deletes a replaced or removed cover from R2
- `course/course.transfer.ts`: the course document that export writes and import reads, its dry run and its import (`exportCourseDocument`, `planCourseImport`, `applyCourseImport`)
- `license/license.upgrades.ts`: the tiers above a licence's tier and the metadata of an upgrade checkout (`higherTiers`, `upgradeMetadata`)
- `order/order.polar.ts`: `polarOrderTerms`, the discount and billing country of a Polar order
- `invitation/invitation.plugin.ts`: `invitationPlugin`, the server-only Better Auth endpoint that creates the invited account, spends the invitation and starts the session
