description: "A public catalogue of courses whose lessons are free, need an account or need a licence, a player that saves progress, and admin screens to write courses, chapters and lessons."
---

Courses, tutorials and projects live in D1 and are watched under `/courses`. Each lesson plays a video from the [Videos](/docs/features/videos) library, and its access level decides who may watch it.

## Data model

Each table has its own module in `src/modules`, created by the migration `20261113004409_salty_slipstream.sql`:

| Table             | Columns                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course`          | `slug` (unique), `kind` (`course`, `tutorial`, `project`), `status` (`draft`, `published`), `position`, `title`, `summary`                                  |
| `chapter`         | `course_id`, `position`, `title`                                                                                                                            |
| `lesson`          | `course_id`, `chapter_id`, `slug` (unique within its course), `access` (`preview`, `account`, `paid`), `status`, `position`, `title`, `summary`, `video_id` |
| `lesson_progress` | `user_id` and `lesson_id` (the primary key), `position_ms`, `completed_at`                                                                                  |

- `title` and `summary` hold JSON keyed by locale, such as `{"en-US": "Billing", "pl-PL": "Płatności"}` (`LocalizedText` in `src/modules/_core/utils/localized-text.ts`). `localize()` returns the visitor's locale and falls back to `en-US`, so always fill in `en-US`. The admin requires it; rows inserted straight into D1 are not checked.
- New rows are `draft`, and new lessons are `paid`. Only `published` courses and lessons are listed or served; any other slug shows the not-found page.
- Courses are ordered by `position`, chapters by `position` within their course and lessons by `position` within their chapter. Lessons are numbered across the whole course.
- `video_id` refers to the `video` table and becomes `NULL` when the video is deleted. Deleting a course or a chapter deletes its lessons, and deleting a lesson or a user deletes their progress.

## Access levels

| `access`  | Who can watch                                                                           |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `preview` | Everyone, signed in or not                                                              |
| `account` | Any signed-in user                                                                      |
| `paid`    | A user with an `active` licence of tier `complete` or `agency` (`COURSE_LICENCE_TIERS`) |

Roles with `course: ["read"]`, which admins have, can watch every lesson. `hasLessonAccess` (`src/modules/lesson/lesson.access.ts`) makes the decision for a viewer that `resolveViewer` (`lesson.viewer.ts`) builds from the session, the role and `getLicense`. Every server function that serves course content applies it, not only the pages:

- `getCatalogue` (public) returns the published courses with their lesson count, total video length and number of free previews.
- `getCourseShowcase` (public) returns, for the landing page, the first two published courses (`COURSE_SHOWCASE`) with their lesson count, total video length, number of free previews and first six published lessons, and the first free preview that has a video. It reports each lesson's access level, not the viewer's locks.
- `getCourse` (public) returns a published course with its chapters and published lessons in the visitor's locale, each with `locked`, `completed` and `positionMs` for the viewer.
- `getLessonPlayback` (public) returns signed playback paths from `createPlayback`. It answers `UNAUTHORIZED` to a guest and `FORBIDDEN` to a signed-in user who may not watch the lesson, and `NOT_FOUND` for an unpublished lesson or one without a video.
- `saveLessonProgress` requires a session and checks the same access before it writes.

## Pages

The `/courses` layout (`courses.tsx`) has its own navigation, and its labels are in the `pages.courses` namespace. The pages read D1 for each viewer, so they are not prerendered: `ROUTES.COURSES` is in `UNLISTED_ROUTES` in `vite.config.ts`.

- `/courses`: one card per published course with its kind, lesson count, minutes and free lessons.
- `/courses/<course>`: the summary, a progress bar for signed-in viewers, a **Start the course** or **Continue with …** link to the next lesson to watch, and the curriculum by chapter with **Free preview** badges, durations, and lock and completed icons. When a lesson is locked, the **Every lesson comes with Complete** panel links to the pricing section, or to `/app/license` for a signed-in viewer.
- `/courses/<course>/<lesson>`: the player, or a locked panel with **Sign in to watch**, which returns to the lesson after sign-in through `redirect`, or **Get Complete**. A lesson without a video says that its recording is on its way. Below it are the access level, **Mark as complete** for signed-in viewers and links to the previous and next lessons; the course contents stay beside it on wide screens.

## On the landing page

The course block in the landing page's **Toolkit** section (`course-showcase.tsx` in `src/presentation/components/custom/landing-page/components/`) shows what D1 holds, so it never lists a course or lesson that does not exist:

- The landing page is prerendered, so its HTML contains the block's heading, the course tour and placeholders. When the block comes within one screen height of the viewport, the browser calls `getCourseShowcase` once through `courseShowcaseQuery` and fills it in. Visitors who never scroll that far send no request.
- Each course shows its kind, lesson count, minutes and free lessons, then its first lessons with their access level. Every course and lesson links to its page, and a course with more lessons ends with a link to all of them.
- **Watch a free lesson** opens the first free preview that has a video. Without one, the link is left out.
- With no published course, the block says so; if the request fails, it points to the course catalogue. **Browse all courses** links to `/courses` in every case.
- The course tour is the video of the `course-tour` composition in the `remotion/` project. It is a static file in `src/presentation/assets/motion/` with AVIF and WebP posters, and plays in place with the browser's controls when pressed. It does not use the video library.

## Progress

For a signed-in viewer, the player starts at the saved `position_ms` and `useLessonProgress` (`src/hooks/use-lesson-progress.ts`) saves the position at most every 15 seconds while it plays, through the `startAt` and `onProgress` props of `VideoPlayer`. When the video ends (`onEnded`) or the viewer presses **Mark as complete**, `completed_at` is set, the saved position returns to the start and the course page refreshes its progress. `completed_at` is kept once set. Guests can watch free previews, but nothing is saved.

## Lessons and videos

A lesson stores no video files of its own. Publish the recording with `bun run video:publish:<environment>` (see [Videos](/docs/features/videos)), then choose it in the lesson's **Video** field in the admin, which sets `video_id`. Several lessons can use the same video. Durations on the catalogue and in the curriculum come from `video.duration_ms`.

## Managing courses

**Content → Courses** in the admin sidebar opens the course screens. Their layout, `admin.courses.tsx`, preloads the `course.validations` and `pages.admin.courses` namespaces, and like every page under `/admin` they require `admin: ["access"]`. The components are in `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/courses/`.

- `/admin/courses` (`admin.courses.index.tsx`) lists every course, drafts included, in `position` order: the title with its `/courses/<slug>` path, the type, the status, the number of lessons and the date of the last change. Each row has arrows that move the course up or down, an edit link and a delete button. **New course** opens the form.
- `/admin/courses/create` (`admin.courses.create.tsx`) shows an empty course form. **Create course** saves the course and opens its editor.
- `/admin/courses/<courseId>` (`admin.courses.$courseId.tsx`) shows the course form with **Save details**, the **Curriculum** and **View course**, which opens `/courses/<slug>` in a new tab. **View course** appears only once the course is published, because drafts are not served publicly.

A new course is added after the others. The arrows in the list set the order of the public catalogue (`moveCourse`).

### Course details

The course form (`course-form.tsx`) has these fields:

- **Title** and **Summary**, with one tab per locale. The `en-US` tab is required; a locale left empty falls back to English.
- **URL slug**: lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens, at most 96 characters, unique across courses.
- **Type**: **Course**, **Tutorial** or **Project**.
- **Status**: **Draft**, the default, or **Published**. Only published courses appear under `/courses`.

### Curriculum

The curriculum editor (`curriculum-editor.tsx`) shows the chapters in order, each with its lessons.

- **Add chapter** opens `chapter-dialog.tsx`, which asks for a **Chapter title** per locale. Each chapter has buttons to move it up or down, edit its title and delete it.
- **Add lesson**, below a chapter's lessons, opens `lesson-dialog.tsx`. The same dialog edits a lesson.
- Each lesson shows its slug, its video and length or **No video**, its access level and its status, with buttons to move it up or down within its chapter, edit it and delete it. A lesson cannot move to another chapter.

The lesson dialog has these fields:

- **Lesson title** and **Summary**, with one tab per locale; `en-US` is required.
- **URL slug**, unique within the course.
- **Who can watch**: **Everyone (free preview)** (`preview`), **Signed-in visitors** (`account`) or **Complete and Agency licences** (`paid`, the default).
- **Status**: **Draft**, the default, or **Published**.
- **Video**: the 100 most recently published videos in the library, with their length. **No video yet** leaves the lesson without a video, or detaches the one it has.

### Deleting

Every delete asks for confirmation first.

- Deleting a course removes its chapters, its lessons and everyone's progress in them.
- Deleting a chapter removes its lessons and their progress.
- Deleting a lesson removes its progress.

The foreign keys do this with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Videos are never deleted with a course, a chapter or a lesson: they stay in the library.

### Server functions

| Module    | Use cases                                                        | Permission           |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| `course`  | `getCourses`, `getCourseEditor`                                  | `course: ["read"]`   |
| `course`  | `createCourse`                                                   | `course: ["create"]` |
| `course`  | `updateCourse`, `moveCourse`                                     | `course: ["update"]` |
| `course`  | `deleteCourse`                                                   | `course: ["delete"]` |
| `chapter` | `createChapter`, `updateChapter`, `deleteChapter`, `moveChapter` | `course: ["update"]` |
| `lesson`  | `createLesson`, `updateLesson`, `deleteLesson`, `moveLesson`     | `course: ["update"]` |

- The video list of the lesson dialog comes from `getVideos`, which requires `video: ["read"]`.
- A slug that is already taken answers `CONFLICT`: `rethrowAsConflict` (`src/integrations/drizzle-orm/drizzle.errors.ts`) turns D1's unique-constraint error into an `AppError`. An id that matches no row answers `NOT_FOUND`.
- A new course, chapter or lesson takes the highest `position` among its siblings plus one (`nextPosition`). A move swaps the row with its neighbour and renumbers the siblings from 1 (`reorder`) in one `db.batch`. Both functions are in `src/modules/_core/utils/ordering.ts`, and the directions are `MOVE_DIRECTION` in `src/modules/_core/constants/ordering.ts`.
- Every write validates titles and summaries with `localizedTextSchema`: `en-US` is required and each text has at most 2,000 characters. A slug has at most 96 characters. The form messages are `defaultLocaleRequired`, `textTooLong`, `slugInvalid` and `slugTooLong` in `course.validations`, and `COURSE_VALIDATION_PARAMS` (`course.constants.ts`) supplies their limits.
- `courseEditorQuery` keeps `staleTime: COURSE_STALE_MS` like the public course queries, and every change invalidates the `course` queries, so the list and the editor refetch.

## Tests

`scripts/seed-test.ts` seeds a published course with one chapter and four lessons for the browser tests in `e2e/specs/courses.spec.ts`. In `e2e/specs/course-admin.spec.ts`, an admin creates a course, a chapter and a free lesson with the seeded video, a visitor in another browser page watches the lesson, and the admin deletes the course. `e2e/specs/landing.spec.ts` checks that the landing page links the seeded course and its lessons, opens the free lesson and plays the course tour.
