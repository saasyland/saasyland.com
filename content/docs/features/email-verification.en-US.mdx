---
title: "Email verification"
description: "Required email verification with localized links and a resend form."
---

Better Auth runs with `requireEmailVerification`, `sendOnSignUp`, `sendOnSignIn` and `autoSignInAfterVerification` enabled, so a new account must confirm its address before it can sign in, and each sign-in with the right password before then sends a fresh link. A link works for `EMAIL_LINK_LIFETIME_HOURS` (`emailVerification.expiresIn`), and the email says so.

## Sending

`emailVerification.sendVerificationEmail` in `auth.server.ts` passes the email to the auth email senders, and Resend's is `sendVerifyEmail` (`src/modules/verification/use-cases/send-verify-email.ts`, joined in `src/data/resend.auth-emails.ts`). It builds a link to `/auth/verify-email` on this site with the locale and the `redirect` of the flow's `callbackURL`, plus the `token`, and sends the `VerifyEmail` template (see [Transactional email](/docs/features/email)). It sends at most <RepositoryFact name="recipientEmailsPerHour" /> verification emails per address per hour (`withinRecipientRateLimit`); further requests send nothing and get the normal response. When someone signs up again with an unverified address, `onExistingUserSignUp` sends a fresh link instead of revealing that the account exists; with a verified address it sends the existing-account email instead (see [Signing up with a known address](/docs/features/authentication#signing-up-with-a-known-address)). A link that sign-in sends takes its locale and `redirect` from the sign-in page.

## Verifying

`/auth/verify-email` resolves the link in `beforeLoad`:

- With `?token=`, it reloads the document at `/api/auth/verify-email` with a `callbackURL` of `/auth/verify-email?verified=true` in the same locale, keeping `redirect`.
- With `?verified=true` and no `error`, it redirects to `/auth/callback` with `redirect`, which sends the user there, or to `/admin` or `/app`.
- Otherwise it renders the page; with `?error=` it shows the invalid-link message.

## Resending

`VerifyEmailForm` prefills the address from `?email=`, which sign-up and sign-in set when they redirect here. Sign-in also sets `?unverified=true`, and the page then says that a fresh link was sent instead of the generic sign-up text. It calls the `sendVerificationEmail` server function (`src/modules/verification/use-cases/send-verification-email.ts`), rate limited with `RATE_LIMITS.SENSITIVE`.
