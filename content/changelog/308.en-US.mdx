- Added: admins invite people by email from **User management → Invitations**, with a role and the language of the email. The invitee opens a single-use link that works for seven days, chooses a name and a password or continues with Google or GitHub, and lands signed in with that role. Admins see the open invitations, resend a new link or revoke one, and the invitation records who invited, sent, accepted or revoked it and when. See [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#invitations).
