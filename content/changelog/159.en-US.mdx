- The areas own their permissions, skeletons, demo data and labels. Each area declares its permission statements and the roles it grants them to in `src/data/<area>.access.ts`, which `access.entries.ts` joins into `APP_STATEMENTS` and `grantsOf(role)`, and `auth.access.ts` builds Better Auth's access control and roles from them; the role codes moved to `auth.roles.ts`. Every role keeps exactly its permissions, which `auth.access.website.test.ts` compares with a snapshot taken before the change. The customer app and the public pages pick their page skeletons from the areas' lists too, now `PAGE_SKELETONS` (formerly `ADMIN_SKELETONS`), and their skeleton files are one per area (`app/core-pending.tsx`, `app/license-pending.tsx`, `legal-pending.tsx`, `newsletter-pending.tsx` and others). The admin demo rows are split per page into `admin-analytics.ts`, `admin-payments.ts`, `admin-products.ts` and `admin-users.ts`. The labels of each area's menu entries, breadcrumbs and dashboard statistics moved from the shared menu files into its own `navigation.<area>.json` and `dashboard.<area>.json`, loaded at the root, and the field, switch and upload zone skeletons have a test of their own. A copy of the project without the products area type-checks and keeps 100% coverage without editing a shared file. Nothing changes on screen. See [Paths and navigation](/docs/conventions/routes#paths-and-navigation) and [Roles and permissions](/docs/guides/roles-and-permissions).
