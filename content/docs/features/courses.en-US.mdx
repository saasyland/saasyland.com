---

Courses, tutorials and projects live in D1 and are watched under `/courses`. A video lesson plays its own recording, uploaded in its lesson editor (see [Videos](/docs/features/videos)); a text lesson has no video and is read. Its access level decides who may open it and download its materials.

The admin is the single place to edit courses. Every title, summary, level, icon, outcome, prerequisite, chapter, lesson, description, key moment, video, upload and material is a row in D1 that `/admin/courses` edits in every locale; no course content is written in the source code. The code only fixes the choices the admin offers: the kinds, levels, statuses, access levels, lesson formats and icon names are named constants. For changes to many courses at once, the admin exports the courses to a JSON file and imports the edited file; see [Import and export](#import-and-export).

## Data model

Each table has its own module in `src/modules`. The migration `20261113004409_salty_slipstream.sql` created the first four, `20261117161820_safe_stardust.sql` added `lesson_attachment` and the columns marked _new_, and `20261123021636_steady_microchip.sql` added `video_upload` and the lesson's `format`, each with a default, so existing rows stay valid:

| Table               | Columns                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `course`            | `slug` (unique), `kind` (`course`, `tutorial`, `project`), `status` (`draft`, `published`), `position`, `title`, `summary`, _new_: `level` (`beginner` by default), `icon` (`book-open` by default), `outcomes` and `prerequisites` (`{}` by default)                                                               |
| `chapter`           | `course_id`, `position`, `title`, _new_: `summary` (`{}` by default)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `lesson`            | `course_id`, `chapter_id`, `slug` (unique within its course), `access` (`preview`, `account`, `paid`), `status`, `position`, `title`, `summary`, `video_id`, _new_: `description` (`{}` by default), `key_moments` (`[]` by default) and `format` (`video` by default, or `text`)                                   |
| `lesson_attachment` | _new_: `lesson_id`, `position`, `title`, `kind` (`file`, `link`), `object_key`, `size_bytes` and `content_type` for a file, `url` for a link                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `lesson_progress`   | `user_id` and `lesson_id` (the primary key), `position_ms`, `completed_at`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `video_upload`      | _new_: one per lesson (`lesson_id`, unique): the file's `file_name`, `size_bytes`, `content_type` and `last_modified`, the R2 `object_key` and `multipart_id`, `part_bytes`, the stored `parts` and their ETags, `status` and `failure`; see [Videos](/docs/features/videos#from-the-upload-to-a-lesson-that-plays) |

- `title`, `summary` and `description` hold JSON keyed by locale, such as `{"en-US": "Billing", "pl-PL": "Płatności"}` (`LocalizedText` in `src/modules/_core/utils/localized-text.ts`). `localize()` returns the visitor's locale and falls back to `en-US`, so always fill in `en-US`. The admin requires it for titles and summaries; a chapter summary and a lesson description may stay empty. Rows inserted straight into D1 are not checked.
- `outcomes` and `prerequisites` hold one list per locale, such as `{"en-US": ["Deploy to Cloudflare"]}` (`LocalizedList`). `localizeList()` returns the visitor's list, or the English one when it is missing or empty. A list has at most <RepositoryFact name="localizedListMaxItems" /> items of at most <RepositoryFact name="localizedListItemMaxLength" /> characters.
- `level` is one of <RepositoryFact name="courseLevels" /> (`COURSE_LEVEL`), and `icon` one of <RepositoryFact name="courseIcons" /> (`COURSE_ICON`). `COURSE_ICON_COMPONENTS` in `src/data/course-icons.ts` is the one place that maps each name to its lucide icon.
- A lesson `description` is plain text of at most <RepositoryFact name="lessonDescriptionMaxLength" /> characters per locale: a blank line starts a new paragraph, and text between backticks is code. `parseRichText` (`src/lib/rich-text.ts`) splits it into paragraphs and code spans, and `RichText` (`src/presentation/components/custom/rich-text.tsx`) renders them as React elements, so no stored text is ever parsed as HTML.
- `key_moments` is a list of `{ "seconds": 135, "label": { "en-US": "Install the CLI" } }`, at most <RepositoryFact name="lessonKeyMomentsMaxItems" />, kept in time order. The admin writes times as `2:15` or `1:02:15`; `updateLesson` stores the seconds.
- New rows are `draft`, and new lessons are `paid`. Only `published` courses and lessons are listed or served; any other slug shows the not-found page.
- Courses are ordered by `position`, chapters by `position` within their course and lessons by `position` within their chapter. Lessons are numbered across the whole course.
- `video_id` refers to the `video` table and becomes `NULL` when the video is deleted. Deleting a course or a chapter deletes its lessons, and deleting a lesson deletes its materials and its upload; deleting a lesson or a user deletes their progress.
- `format` is <RepositoryFact name="lessonFormats" /> (`LESSON_FORMAT`). A `text` lesson has no video and is never being recorded; see [Text lessons](#text-lessons).
- `cover`, added by `20261121231021_overjoyed_bastion.sql` and `NULL` by default, holds the R2 key of the course's cover, never a URL; see [Course covers](#course-covers).

## Being recorded

A video lesson is being recorded while it has no video: before its recording is uploaded, while it uploads and while it is encoded. Such a lesson is listed in the curriculum and can be opened, but it never plays. There is no lesson status for it: the public reads join a lesson to its video with `playableVideo` (`src/modules/lesson/lesson.playable.ts`), which joins only `video` lessons, and `isBeingRecorded` in the same file decides, so for a lesson being recorded:

- `getCourse` and `getLesson` return `recording: true` and `durationMs: null`, and `getLesson` returns no caption locales;
- `getLessonPlayback` answers `NOT_FOUND`, so no signed stream exists;
- `getCatalogue` and `getCourseShowcase` leave it out of the course length and count only the lessons that are ready, those that play and the text lessons (`recorded` and `recordedCount`), and the showcase marks it with `recording`.

A course can be published while its lessons are being recorded, and the pages say so instead of selling what does not play yet:

- The catalogue, the course page and the chapter headers show **Recording now**, with a pulsing dot, in place of the minutes while none of their lessons is ready, and **3 of 23 lessons ready** once some are.
- The landing page's course block marks each lesson being recorded with **Coming soon** beside an hourglass and does not link it to a player.
- The course page's panel says that the course is being recorded and that each lesson plays as soon as its recording is published, and offers no **Start the course** link. Once some lessons play, **Start the course** and **Continue with …** lead only to a lesson that plays (`nextLessonToWatch` in `course.utils.ts`).
- The curriculum and the lesson page show **Coming soon** beside an hourglass where a lesson's length would be.
- A lesson being recorded shows its number, **Recording now** and that its recording is on its way over the dimmed course cover in the player's frame, even when it is locked, so its page never asks the visitor to buy a lesson that cannot be watched, and it offers no **Mark as complete**.

Once its recording is encoded, or a video is picked under **Use an existing video**, the lesson plays everywhere at once, with no other change.

## Text lessons

A lesson whose `format` is `text` has no video by design: a lesson to read, or one that only offers materials. The admin makes one by choosing **Text lesson, no video** in the **Add lesson** dialog or by switching on **This lesson has no video** in the lesson editor, which `setLessonFormat` refuses with `CONFLICT` while the lesson has a video or an upload. The public pages read it as a lesson that is ready, never as one being recorded:

- `getCourse`, `getLesson` and `getCourseShowcase` return `format: "text"`, `recording: false` and `durationMs: null`, and `getLessonPlayback` answers `NOT_FOUND`, because `playableVideo` joins no video to it.
- The curriculum, the course contents and the landing page's course block mark it with a document icon and **Text lesson** where a video lesson shows its length (`lesson-meta.tsx`); it adds nothing to the course length, and it counts among the lessons that are ready.
- Its page has no player and no stage: the title, the access level, the description and the materials come first. A locked text lesson shows the same unlock panel as a locked video lesson. A signed-in viewer who may open it gets **Mark as complete**, and **Start the course** and **Continue with …** can lead to it (`isAvailable` in `course.utils.ts`, while `isPlayable` is for video lessons only).
- The lesson editor hides the key moments, which are times in a video.

## Access levels

| `access`  | Who can watch                                                                           |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `preview` | Everyone, signed in or not                                                              |
| `account` | Any signed-in user                                                                      |
| `paid`    | A user with an `active` licence of tier `complete` or `agency` (`COURSE_LICENSE_TIERS`) |

Roles with `course: ["read"]`, which admins have, can watch every lesson. `hasLessonAccess` (`src/modules/lesson/lesson.access.ts`) makes the decision for a viewer that `resolveViewer` (`lesson.viewer.ts`) builds from the session, the role and the course access that the grants of `COURSE_ACCESS_GRANTS` give (`holdsCourseAccess`). Course access counts only for a confirmed email address; a holder whose address is not confirmed gets `mustConfirmEmail`, and the page asks them to confirm it. Every server function that serves course content applies it, not only the pages:

- `getCatalogue` (public) returns the published courses with their lesson count, total video length, number of free previews and paid lessons and the lessons the viewer has completed, and `viewer`: whether someone is signed in, whether they may open paid lessons, and whether they hold access but must confirm their address first (`mustConfirmEmail`).
- `getCourseShowcase` (public) returns, for the landing page, the first <RepositoryFact name="courseShowcaseCourses" /> published courses (`COURSE_SHOWCASE`), each with its lesson count, recorded lessons, free lessons (`previewCount`), total video length and its first <RepositoryFact name="courseShowcaseLessons" /> lessons in curriculum order, each with its access level, its length (`durationMs`), whether the viewer may open it (`locked`) and whether it is being recorded.
- `getCourse` (public) returns a published course in the visitor's locale: its kind, level, icon, outcomes, prerequisites and number of materials, its chapters with their summaries, and its published lessons, each with `locked`, `recording`, `captioned`, `durationMs`, `completed` and `positionMs` for the viewer. The outline comes from `readCourseOutline` (`src/modules/course/course.outline.ts`), which `getLesson` shares.
- `getLesson` (public) returns everything the lesson page shows in one call: the same outline, the lesson with its description, key moments, caption locales and materials, and the previous and next lessons. It answers `NOT_FOUND` for an unpublished course or lesson.
- `getLessonPlayback` (public) returns signed playback paths from `createPlayback`. It answers `UNAUTHORIZED` to a guest and `FORBIDDEN` to a signed-in user who may not watch the lesson, and `NOT_FOUND` for an unpublished lesson or one [being recorded](#being-recorded). The lesson page keeps it as a separate query that stays fresh for an hour, so the player's address does not change while it plays.
- `saveLessonProgress` requires a session and checks the same access before it writes.

When a viewer with course access opens a paid lesson, `getLesson` and `getLessonPlayback` record it with `recordPaidLessonOpened` (`lesson.viewer.ts`) before they answer, so the lesson's materials and video are never handed out unrecorded. Free previews, lessons for any account and staff without course access record nothing.

Course access is a list of the lessons' own, `COURSE_ACCESS_GRANTS` in `src/modules/lesson/lesson.access.ts`, beside `hasLessonAccess`: a feature that sells it adds a `CourseAccessGrant` as one element, and a person reviews every change to the file. A grant says whether a user holds access (`holds`, a server-only function), what includes it (`includedWith`, message keys of the names the licence page lists) what it costs (`price`, from `TIER_PRICES`), where to get it (`unlock`, a link built from the lesson the viewer is on) and how a paid lesson it opened is recorded (`recordUse`, a server-only function that `recordCourseAccessUse` calls with the lesson and the user). Its one element today is the licence's grant, written inline from the licence's use cases `getLicense` and `recordLessonOpened` and the tiers in `license.constants.ts`. It grants access to an `active` licence of a tier in `COURSE_LICENSE_TIERS`, Complete or Agency, offers `OFFERED_COURSE_TIER`, Complete, records the first paid lesson it opens on the licence as the first use of the purchase (`recordLessonOpened`, see [Licences](/docs/features/licences#records-and-refunds)), and sends a signed-in viewer to `/app/license?tier=complete` with the lesson as `redirect`; a guest goes there through sign-up. The checkout carries `redirect` to Polar and back, and the activation panel offers **Continue where you left off**. A project without the licence loses that element with the licence's files, and without a grant paid lessons are for staff only and their locked panels offer no link; a site that grants access another way, for example with a subscription, adds its own element. The course copy takes two more names from the grants instead of naming a tier: `offer`, what the `unlock` links sell, in the access label, the unlock panels and the catalogue description, and `holders`, who holds access, in the admin's paid access level. The licence gives Complete and the `courseAccess.holders` text of `components.custom.pricing`, so the pages read as before.

## Materials

A lesson's materials are files and links in `lesson_attachment`, ordered by `position`.

- A file is stored in the `MEDIA` bucket under `attachments/<attachmentId>/<fileName>` (`attachmentObjectKey` in `lesson-attachment.files.ts`). Uploads accept <RepositoryFact name="attachmentFileTypes" /> files of up to <RepositoryFact name="attachmentMaxMegabytes" /> MB (`LESSON_ATTACHMENT_FILE_TYPES`, `LESSON_ATTACHMENT_MAX_MEGABYTES`). The content type comes from the extension, never from the browser, and the file name keeps only letters, digits, dots, hyphens and underscores.
- A link is a docs path, such as `/docs/getting-started`, or an `https://` address.
- `getLesson` returns each material's title, kind and, for a file, its name and size. It adds `href` only when the viewer may open the lesson: the decision is the lesson's own `locked`, computed once by `hasLessonAccess`. For a file, `href` is a signed path, `/media/downloads/<expiry>.<signature>/<attachmentId>/<fileName>` (`signDownload` in `lesson-attachment.download.ts`), valid for at least <RepositoryFact name="signedDownloadHours" unit="hour" />. `serveAttachment` (`lesson-attachment.delivery.ts`) checks the signature and serves the file with `Content-Disposition: attachment`; a wrong or expired signature gets `403`. A signed path works as a bearer link until it expires.
- Deleting a material, a lesson, a chapter or a course deletes the uploaded files with the rows.

## Pages

The `/courses` layout (`_site._marketing.courses.tsx`) sits in the same `_site` and `_marketing` layouts as the blog, so it has the same header and footer. The pages read D1 for each viewer, so they are not prerendered: `COURSES` is in `REQUEST_RENDERED_ROUTES` in `src/routes.ts`. Search engines still find them: the Worker lists the catalogue, every published course and every lesson a signed-out visitor may watch in `/sitemap-courses.xml`, from D1 with the day each last changed, and a lesson that needs an account or a licence, or is being recorded, carries `noindex`. The course page describes the course as a `Course` in structured data, with its level, outcomes, prerequisites, offers and length, and the catalogue lists the courses as an `ItemList`. The course page and its lessons share the course's cover as their link preview, and a course without a cover shares the site card; see [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo) and [Course covers](#course-covers).

- `/courses` (`course-card.tsx`): a heading and lead over a soft teal glow, then the first published course as a wide featured card and the others in two columns. Each card leads with the course's cover, which zooms slightly on hover, and shows its kind and level as badges, its title and summary, **Recording now** or how much is recorded, its lessons, minutes and free lessons, and, for a signed-in viewer who has completed lessons, a progress bar. The cards lift two pixels on hover; neither moves under reduced motion. Below them, **Every lesson comes with Complete** offers the licence to a viewer who cannot open paid lessons, and **Sign in** to a guest who has it; a holder whose address is not confirmed is offered nothing to buy. With no published course, the page says that the first courses are being recorded and points to the build log signup in the footer.
- `/courses/<course>`: the course's kind and level, title, summary and figures over a blurred glow of its cover, a progress bar for signed-in viewers, **What you'll learn** and **Before you start** from the outcomes and prerequisites, and the curriculum. A course with several chapters shows them as an accordion (`curriculum.tsx`, the shadcn `Accordion` on React Aria's `DisclosureGroup`) with the first chapter open: each header gives the chapter's number, title, lessons, free lessons, minutes or **Recording now**, and, for a signed-in viewer, the lessons completed, such as `2/9`. Each chapter header sits on a tinted band that darkens on hover, so it never reads as a lesson, and every lesson's title starts in the chapter title's column, with its icon, number and lock on the title's first line: lesson and chapter numbers are set in the title's own type and sit on its baseline, and the icon, the lock and the chapter's plus sign are centred on the cap height of that line, so all of them share one centre line at any width, zoom and line count. The course contents beside a lesson follow the same rule, and the landing page's course block centres its icons on the first line too. A course with one chapter lists its lessons without a heading. Each lesson row shows a play icon or a check once completed, its number and title, and under the title its access as a tag (**Free preview**, **Free with an account** or **Included with Complete**) and its length or **Coming soon** (`lesson-meta.tsx`), with a lock when the viewer cannot open it. A locked title stays as bright and as heavy as an open one; the lock says it is locked, and screen readers hear **Locked: included with Complete** in place of the tag. The tag is a `Badge` in the `accent` tone for a free preview and the `neutral` tone otherwise, and it shares one centre line with the length, a fixed gap below the title, on the course page and on the landing page alike (see [Theme](/docs/ui/theme#badges)). Beside the content, a hatched rule separates the panel (`course-panel.tsx`): the cover, the course's state (**Recording now**, **You can watch every lesson** or how much is recorded), **Start the course** or **Continue with …** and **Get Complete** when they apply, and **What's included**, built only from the course's data: its lessons, minutes of video, free lessons, materials, captions and transcripts when a lesson has them, saved progress, level and new lessons with the offer. From the `lg` breakpoint the panel sticks at the header's height (`--spacing-header`) plus a spacing step, and scrolls on its own when it is taller than the window; on phones it follows the course's introduction and does not stick. When a lesson is locked, **Get Complete** links to `/app/license?tier=complete`, through sign-up for a guest, where Complete is already chosen, and the panel offers a guest who has it **Sign in**; a holder whose address is not confirmed gets **Confirm your email to watch** instead, which opens `/auth/verify-email` and returns to the course.
- `/courses/<course>/<lesson>`: the course contents on the left (`course-contents.tsx`), with the breadcrumb, the course title, the viewer's progress and the chapters as an accordion that opens the current lesson's chapter and highlights the lesson. **Hide contents** gives the lesson the whole width; on phones **Course contents** opens the same list in a sheet, which closes on the next lesson. The player comes first; a locked lesson or one being recorded shows its panel over the dimmed course cover instead (`lesson-stage.tsx`), and an open [text lesson](#text-lessons) has no stage at all. A lesson that needs an account offers **Create a free account** and, for someone who has one, **Sign in**; both return to the lesson through `redirect`. A paid lesson names the offer and its price from `TIER_PRICES`, offers **Get Complete** and, to a guest, **Already have Complete? Sign in**; a holder whose address is not confirmed sees **Confirm your email to watch** with **Resend link**. A lesson [being recorded](#being-recorded) says that its recording is on its way, locked or not. Under it, **Mark as complete** for signed-in viewers of a lesson that plays or a text lesson they may open, and **Up next** with the next lesson's title. Then the lesson's title, access level and length, **On this page** with links to the description, the key moments and the materials when the lesson has two of them, the summary, the description, the **Key moments**, the **Transcript**, the **Materials** and links to the previous and next lessons. The page loads with one `getLesson` call.

The player and its companions share one Video.js store: `LessonMedia` (`lesson-media.tsx`) wraps the lesson in the `VideoPlayer` provider of `@videojs/react/video`, which renders no element, and the player's skin and media load in their own chunk (`video-player.tsx`):

- **Key moments** (`key-moments.tsx`) are buttons that bring the player into view, seek to the moment and play. They are also added as a `chapters` track, so the time slider shows them. A lesson that does not play lists its key moments without buttons.
- **Transcript** (`lesson-transcript.tsx`) loads the captions in the page's language, else in English, else the first ones, as a `metadata` track, lists their lines with their times, marks the line playing now and follows it, and seeks to a line when it is pressed. A lesson whose video has no captions, or whose captions have no lines, shows no transcript.

### Course covers

`course.cover` holds the R2 key of the cover's full size, `covers/<courseId>/<coverId>.webp`, and its smaller sizes sit beside it with their width in the name, such as `<coverId>-480.webp`. `courseCoverImage` in `src/modules/course/course.cover.ts` turns the key into a `src` and a `srcset` under `/media/covers/`, at <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverWidths" /> pixels wide. `CourseCover` (`course-cover.tsx`) draws it at <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverWidth" /> × <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverHeight" /> with `srcset` and `sizes`, at high priority where it is the largest image of the page, lazily elsewhere, with the course title as its alternative text, and as decoration inside a card whose text already names the course. A course without a cover gets its icon on a teal glow instead. The catalogue, the course page and its panel, the lesson page and the landing page's course block read the cover from their own queries (`getCatalogue`, `getCourse`, `getLesson`, `getCourseShowcase`).

The Worker serves covers itself: behind the courses area's `/media/` handler, `serveCover` (`course.delivery.ts`) answers `GET` and `HEAD` for a path that names a course id, a cover id, an optional width and `.webp` or `.jpg`, with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, and keeps a copy in the Workers cache. Every new cover gets a new id, so no cached copy goes stale. The course page and each of its lessons share the full-size cover as their link preview (`courseShareImage`): `og:image` and `twitter:image` with its type, its size and the course title as the alternative text. A course without a cover shares the site card.

## On the landing page

The course block in the landing page's **Toolkit** section (`course-showcase.tsx` in `src/presentation/components/custom/landing-page/components/`) shows what D1 holds at the moment it is viewed, so it never lists a course or lesson that does not exist and needs no deploy after a change in the admin:

- The landing page is prerendered, so its HTML contains the block's heading, the course tour and placeholders, never course data. When the block comes within one screen height of the viewport, the browser calls `getCourseShowcase` once through `courseShowcaseQuery`, which reads D1 for that request and is not cached between visitors, and fills it in. Visitors who never scroll that far send no request. An admin's change to a course reaches the block on the next page view; the admin's own tab refetches at once, because every course change invalidates the `course` queries.
- Each course is a tile with its cover as a thumbnail, its kind and level, its title, which links to its page, the figures of its catalogue card (`course-facts.tsx`: **Recording now** or how much is recorded, its lessons, minutes and free lessons), and its first lessons by title. Each lesson carries the curriculum's tag, **Free preview**, **Free with an account** or **Included with Complete**, and its length or **Coming soon**; in a tile too narrow for both, **Coming soon** shrinks to its hourglass and stays in the lesson's accessible name. A lesson the viewer can open has a play icon and links to its page; a locked one keeps a title as bright as an open one, shows a lock, has a hidden label such as **Locked: included with Complete** in place of its tag, and links to its page with the purchase option; a lesson being recorded links nowhere. **+ 18 more lessons** links to the course page when a course has more.
- From the `md` breakpoint the tiles sit in two columns and the first spans both, listing its lessons in two columns; below it they scroll sideways.
- Placeholder tiles hold the room of <RepositoryFact name="courseShowcaseCourses" /> courses only while the request is pending. Once it returns they are gone, and the block is as tall as what it shows: the courses, or one line saying that no course is published or that the courses could not be loaded. The request starts a screen height before the block comes into view, so the block usually changes height below the viewport and nothing on screen moves. `landing.spec.ts` checks the height of each outcome and the layout shifts on a desktop and on a phone.
- With no published course, the block says so; if the request fails, it points to the course catalogue. **Browse all courses** links to `/courses` in every case.
- The course tour is the video of the `course-tour` composition in the `remotion/` project. It is a static file in `src/presentation/assets/motion/` with AVIF and WebP posters, and plays in place with the browser's controls when pressed. It is a static asset, not a lesson video.

## Progress

For a signed-in viewer, the player starts at the saved `position_ms` and `useLessonProgress` (`src/hooks/use-lesson-progress.ts`) saves the position at most every <RepositoryFact name="lessonProgressSaveSeconds" unit="second" /> while it plays, through the `startAt` and `onProgress` props of `VideoPlayer`. When the video ends (`onEnded`) or the viewer presses **Mark as complete**, `completed_at` is set, the saved position returns to the start and the course page refreshes its progress. `completed_at` is kept once set. Guests can watch free previews, but nothing is saved.

## Lessons and videos

Each video lesson gets its recording in its own lesson editor; there is no separate video page. Durations on the catalogue and in the curriculum come from `video.duration_ms`, and a lesson being recorded has none. To give a lesson its video:

1. Open the lesson in the lesson editor and drop the recording on the **Video** card, or press **Choose a file**. It can also be dropped in the **Add lesson** dialog, and then uploads as soon as the lesson is saved.
2. The card shows the upload's progress, then **Waiting for the encoder** with the command to run, such as `bun run video:encode:production` (see [Encode the uploads](/docs/features/videos#encode-the-uploads)).
3. Once the encoder has finished, the lesson plays on every page at once, its length counts towards the course, and **Coming soon** disappears.

**Use an existing video** puts a video that is already encoded on the lesson, so several lessons can share one recording. Lessons imported from a file without a video, like the curriculum below, have no video at all, which is also [being recorded](#being-recorded); the same steps give them one.

## Managing courses

**Content → Courses** in the admin sidebar opens the course screens. Their layout, `admin.courses.tsx`, preloads the `course.validations` and `pages.admin.courses` namespaces, and like every page under `/admin` they require `admin: ["access"]`. The components are in `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/courses/`.

- `/admin/courses` (`admin.courses.index.tsx`) lists every course, drafts included, in the `DataGrid` with `useCourseColumns` (`course-columns.tsx`), in `position` order until the admin sorts it: the title with its `/courses/<slug>` path, the type, the status, the number of lessons and the date of the last change. `getCourses` searches the titles in every language and the slugs, and filters by title, type, status, lesson count and the day of the last change on the server, validated by `courseZodSchemas.getCourses`. Each row starts with a checkbox, a handle and the course's place in the catalogue (`#`), which the server counts over the whole order, so it stays right whatever the grid shows. It ends with `CourseActions`, one ⋯ menu: **Edit**, **View on site** once the course is published, **Copy link**, and **Delete**, which asks for confirmation. Selected courses get **Delete** in the selection bar too, behind the same confirmation, and `deleteCourses` removes them in one request. **New course** opens the form.
- `/admin/courses/create` (`admin.courses.create.tsx`) shows an empty course form. **Create course** saves the course and opens its editor.
- `/admin/courses/<courseId>` (`admin.courses.$courseId.tsx`) shows the course form with **Save details**, the **Curriculum** and **View course**, which opens `/courses/<slug>` in a new tab. **View course** appears only once the course is published, because drafts are not served publicly.
- `/admin/courses/<courseId>/lessons/<lessonId>` (`admin.courses.$courseId_.lessons.$lessonId.tsx`) is the lesson editor: the lesson's text, its key moments, its video and its materials. **View lesson** appears once the course and the lesson are published. It reads the same `courseEditorQuery` as the course editor, so going back and forth costs no request.

There is no separate page for videos: each lesson's recording is uploaded, replaced and deleted in its lesson editor, and the **Use an existing video** dialog there lists every encoded video with the lessons that use it.

A new course is added after the others. Dragging a course by its handle, or pressing the up or down arrow on a focused handle, sets the order of the public catalogue while the list is neither sorted nor filtered. `moveCourse` moves the course by the number of places it was dragged or stepped, from the place it has in the whole order when the move arrives, onto whichever page that takes it, and numbers the courses from 1 again; a course another administrator added or deleted elsewhere meanwhile does not change where it lands.

### Languages

The course form, the chapter and lesson dialogs, the lesson editor and the materials dialogs each have one language control above their fields (`form-languages.tsx`), whatever the number of locales:

- **Language** lists every locale of `I18N.SUPPORTED_LOCALES` with its name, its code and its state: complete, partly translated, not translated or with errors. Typing filters the list by name or code. Choosing a language switches every localized field of the form to it, and each field shows the code of the language it holds.
- Under a field in another language, its English source text is shown.
- Beside the list, the form counts the languages that fill every field English fills (`localeStatuses` in `src/lib/localized-form.ts`).
- Saving with an error in a language that is not shown switches the form to the first such language and focuses the field. An error left in another language is named above the fields, with a button that shows it.
- **All languages** opens a panel with one field in every language, English first, each marked filled or missing. It searches the languages, can show only the missing ones and switches between the form's text fields. Text typed in the panel belongs to the form and is saved with it.

### Course details

The course form (`course-form.tsx`) has these fields:

- **Title** and **Summary**, in the chosen language. `en-US` is required; a locale left empty falls back to English.
- **URL slug**: lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens, at most <RepositoryFact name="slugMaxLength" /> characters, unique across courses.
- **Type**: **Course**, **Tutorial** or **Project**.
- **Status**: **Draft**, the default, or **Published**. Only published courses appear under `/courses`.
- **Level**: **Beginner**, the default, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**.
- **Icon**: one of the named icons, shown beside the field.
- **What learners will be able to do** and **Prerequisites**, in the chosen language: **Add an outcome** and **Add a prerequisite** add a line, and the cross beside a line removes it. An empty line is refused.

### Cover

The **Cover** card under the course form (`course-cover-card.tsx`) shows the course's cover, or its icon on a teal glow. **Choose an image** takes a <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverSourceTypes" /> file of at most <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverMaxMegabytes" /> MB. **Upload cover**, or **Replace cover** once there is one, crops it to 16:9 from its centre in the browser and encodes it at each width with `encodeImages` (`src/lib/image-encoding.ts`), as WebP, or as JPEG in a browser that cannot encode WebP, then sends the sizes in one request. **Remove cover** brings back the icon. A course gets a cover once it exists, so the card is on the course editor and not on the create form.

### Curriculum

The curriculum editor (`curriculum-editor.tsx`) shows the chapters in order, each followed by its lessons, as one React Aria `GridList`. Each lesson row gives the title its own line, the slug and video under it, and the tags and its ⋯ menu a row below that wraps as the card narrows, so no text overlaps another at any width.

### Ordering the curriculum

Every chapter and lesson starts with a handle. Dragging a lesson by it moves the lesson within its chapter or into another one, an empty chapter included; dragging a chapter moves it with all its lessons. The keyboard does the same: focus a row, press the right arrow to reach its handle, press Enter, choose the place with the up and down arrows and press Enter again, or Escape to leave it where it was. React Aria announces each step to screen readers in the page's language, and the card explains how it works under its title.

- Only the places that make sense are offered: a lesson cannot go above the first chapter, and a chapter goes only before another chapter or after the last lesson (`canDropInCurriculum` in `src/modules/course/course.curriculum.ts`, which also computes the new order with `moveInCurriculum`).
- The new order shows at once, before the server answers. `reorderCurriculum` then saves the whole curriculum, every chapter with its lessons in order, and writes only the rows whose chapter or position changed, in one `db.batch`. It answers `CONFLICT` when the chapters and lessons it receives are not exactly the course's own, as after a change in another tab; the editor then puts the previous order back, says why and refetches the course. Moves are saved one after the other, in the order they were made.

- **Add chapter** opens `chapter-dialog.tsx`, which asks for a **Chapter title** and an optional **Summary** per locale. Each chapter shows its summary under its title, **Add lesson**, and a ⋯ menu that edits or deletes it.
- **Add lesson**, in a chapter's row, opens `lesson-dialog.tsx`, which creates a lesson at the end of that chapter.
- Each lesson shows its slug, its video and length, its access level and its status, and the state of its video: **No video yet**, **Uploading** with its percentage while this tab uploads it, **Upload stopped**, **Waiting to encode**, **Encoding**, **Encoding failed** or **Text lesson**. Its ⋯ menu opens it in the lesson editor or deletes it.

The lesson dialog has these fields:

- **Lesson title** and **Summary**, in the chosen language; `en-US` is required.
- **URL slug**, unique within the course.
- **Who can watch**: **Everyone (free preview)** (`preview`), **Signed-in visitors** (`account`) or **Complete and Agency licences** (`paid`, the default).
- **Status**: **Draft**, the default, or **Published**.
- **Lesson type**: **Video lesson**, the default, or **Text lesson, no video** (`format`).
- **Recording**, for a video lesson: a drop zone that takes the recording, optional. It starts uploading as soon as the lesson is saved, and the curriculum shows its progress while you go on working.

### Lesson editor

The lesson editor has three cards:

- **Lesson** (`lesson-form.tsx`): the **Lesson title**, **Summary** and **Description** per locale, the **URL slug**, **Who can watch**, **Status** and the **Key moments**. The description shows a preview of its paragraphs and code as learners will see it. Each key moment is a time, such as `2:15`, and a label per locale; **Add a key moment** adds one, the cross removes it, and **Save lesson** stores them in time order.
- **Video** (`lesson-video.tsx`): a drop zone for the recording (`video-drop-zone.tsx`, React Aria's `DropZone` with a `FileTrigger`), which refuses a file of another type or over the size limit before anything is sent; while it uploads, its progress and **Cancel upload**; once uploaded, its state on the way to the encoder (see [Videos](/docs/features/videos#from-the-upload-to-a-lesson-that-plays)); once ready, the player with the video's length, resolution, size and caption languages, **Replace video** and **Delete video**, or **Remove from this lesson** when other lessons use it. **Use an existing video** (`video-picker-dialog.tsx`) lists the encoded videos with their use, puts one on the lesson (`replaceLessonVideo`) and deletes the ones no lesson uses. Under them, **This lesson has no video** makes it a [text lesson](#text-lessons).
- **Materials** (`lesson-materials.tsx`): the lesson's files and links in order, as a React Aria `GridList` like the curriculum, each with a handle, its file name and size or its address, and a ⋯ menu that edits or deletes it. Dragging a material by its handle, or the keyboard steps of the curriculum, moves it; the new order shows at once, `moveLessonAttachment` saves the material's new place, and a refused move puts the order back. **Upload a file** asks for a title per locale and a file; **Add a link** asks for a title per locale and the address. Editing a link changes its title and address; editing a file renames it.

### Deleting

Every delete asks for confirmation first.

- Deleting a course removes its chapters, its lessons, their materials and everyone's progress in them.
- Deleting a chapter removes its lessons, their materials and their progress.
- Deleting a lesson removes its materials, its progress and its upload.
- Deleting a material removes it from the lesson.

The foreign keys remove the rows with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, and the delete use cases remove the uploaded files from R2 (`attachmentObjectKeys` and `removeAttachmentObjects` in `lesson-attachment.objects.ts`, `uploadObjects` and `removeUploadObjects` in `video-upload.objects.ts`, which also aborts an unfinished multipart upload). Videos are never deleted with a course, a chapter or a lesson, because other lessons may play them: one no lesson uses any more is listed as **Not used by any lesson** under **Use an existing video**, where it can be deleted.

Deleting a course also deletes the files of its cover (`removeCoverObjects` in `course.objects.ts`).

### Import and export

The course list has **Import** and **Export all**, and the course editor has **Export** for its own course. Export writes a JSON file, `courses-<date>.json` or `<slug>-<date>.json`, and import reads one. The components are `import-courses-dialog.tsx` and `export-courses-button.tsx`, and the work is done in `src/modules/course/course.transfer.ts`.

The file holds every course in course order with its chapters, its lessons in order, their text in every locale, access levels, statuses, descriptions, key moments, links, the id of each lesson's video and `"format": "text"` for a text lesson:

```json
{
  "courses": [
    {
      "chapters": [
        {
          "lessons": [
            {
              "access": "preview",
              "description": {},
              "keyMoments": [{ "at": "2:15", "label": { "en-US": "Install the CLI" } }],
              "links": [{ "title": { "en-US": "Getting started" }, "url": "/docs/getting-started" }],
              "slug": "what-you-will-build",
              "status": "published",
              "summary": { "en-US": "The finished application." },
              "title": { "en-US": "What you'll build" },
              "videoId": "0195a2b4-6c8d-7e9f-8a0b-1c2d3e4f5a6b"
            }
          ],
          "summary": {},
          "title": { "en-US": "Start" }
        }
      ],
      "icon": "rocket",
      "kind": "course",
      "level": "beginner",
      "outcomes": { "en-US": ["Deploy to Cloudflare"] },
      "prerequisites": { "en-US": ["TypeScript and React"] },
      "slug": "empty-folder-to-production",
      "status": "published",
      "summary": { "en-US": "From an empty folder to production." },
      "title": { "en-US": "Empty folder to production" }
    }
  ],
  "format": "courses",
  "version": 1
}
```

- `format` is <RepositoryFact name="courseDocumentFormat" /> and `version` is <RepositoryFact name="courseDocumentVersion" /> (`COURSE_DOCUMENT`). Each course, chapter, lesson and link is checked with the schemas the admin forms use (`createCourse`, `createChapter`, `updateLesson` and `createLessonAttachment`), joined in `courseDocument` in `course.zod.ts`. A field the format does not have is refused, so a misspelt key cannot be dropped silently.
- Uploaded files do not travel in the file: it carries links only, and the dialog says so. Uploaded files stay on their lessons when a file is imported.
- A lesson without `videoId` keeps the video it has, and a new one starts without a video, so it is [being recorded](#being-recorded). An import never takes a video off a lesson. A lesson without `format` keeps its format, and a new one is a video lesson. A `videoId` that this database lacks, as after an export from another environment, leaves the lesson's video as it is and is listed as a warning.

**Import** opens a dialog: choose the file, then **Check file**. The check is a dry run (`importCourses` with `dryRun: true`) that writes nothing and lists, for courses, chapters, lessons and links, how many the import adds, updates, leaves unchanged, removes and keeps although the file lacks them, how many uploaded files removal deletes, and the warnings. **Import** then runs the same plan for real. A file that breaks a rule lists its problems instead, each with the path of the field, such as `courses.0.chapters.1.lessons.2.slug`, and the message the admin form would show; the first <RepositoryFact name="courseImportProblemsMax" /> are listed and the rest counted. Nothing is imported until the file has no problem.

The import matches what is already in the database by natural keys, never by row ids:

- A course by its slug, and a lesson by its slug within its course, so a lesson can move to another chapter.
- A chapter by its order within its course: the first chapter in the file updates the course's first chapter.
- A link by its order among the lesson's links.

It writes only what differs, so importing the same file twice changes nothing the second time. New courses go after the existing ones, in file order. The lessons of a chapter take the file's order; lessons the file leaves out stay after them. Every write of one import runs in one `db.batch`, a single D1 transaction, so an import is applied whole or not at all.

**Remove what the file does not contain** is off by default. When it is ticked, the import also deletes the courses, chapters, lessons and links that the file lacks, with everyone's progress in them, and removes the uploaded files and the video uploads of the deleted lessons from R2. Exporting one course and importing it with this option deletes every other course, and the check shows that before anything is written.

`importCourses` runs `withRateLimit` with <RepositoryFact name="courseImportsPerMinute" /> checks or imports per address a minute and requires `course: ["create", "update", "delete"]`, because one file can do all three. It reads the file as text of at most <RepositoryFact name="courseDocumentMaxLength" /> characters; split a larger catalogue into several files. `exportCourses` requires `course: ["read"]`.

After an import, the database is the source of truth: edit courses in the admin, and for a change to many lessons at once, export, edit the file and import it.

The file holds no covers. An import leaves each course's cover as it is, and one that removes a course deletes its cover's files too, which the dry run counts among the files. `content/courses/covers/` holds the covers of saasyland.com's courses at each width, named by slug: `bun run db:seed:local` uploads them for the local courses that have none, and on production an admin uploads `<slug>.webp` in each course's **Cover** card.

`content/courses/saasy-land-courses.json` is the curriculum of saasyland.com in every locale, published, with every lesson being recorded. It is the file to import into a new database, not data the application reads: the courses exist only once an admin imports it on production, `bun run db:seed:local` imports it into the local database with the same code, or `bun run courses:sync:preview` copies production's courses to preview (see [Database](/docs/tooling/database#course-data-in-each-environment)). `course-curriculum.test.ts` checks that the import accepts it.

### Server functions

| Module              | Use cases                                                                                                                      | Permission                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| `course`            | `getCourses`, `getCourseEditor`, `exportCourses`                                                                               | `course: ["read"]`                        |
| `course`            | `createCourse`                                                                                                                 | `course: ["create"]`                      |
| `course`            | `updateCourse`, `moveCourse`                                                                                                   | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `course`            | `uploadCourseCover`, `removeCourseCover`                                                                                       | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `course`            | `deleteCourses`                                                                                                                | `course: ["delete"]`                      |
| `course`            | `importCourses`                                                                                                                | `course: ["create", "update", "delete"]`  |
| `course`            | `reorderCurriculum`                                                                                                            | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `chapter`           | `createChapter`, `updateChapter`, `deleteChapter`                                                                              | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `lesson`            | `createLesson`, `updateLesson`, `replaceLessonVideo`, `setLessonFormat`, `deleteLesson`                                        | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `lesson`            | `removeLessonVideo`                                                                                                            | `course: ["update"]`, `video: ["delete"]` |
| `lesson-attachment` | `createLessonAttachment`, `uploadLessonAttachment`, `updateLessonAttachment`, `moveLessonAttachment`, `deleteLessonAttachment` | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `video-upload`      | `startVideoUpload`, `uploadVideoPart`, `completeVideoUpload`, `cancelVideoUpload`, `retryVideoUpload`                          | `course: ["update"]`                      |
| `video`             | `getVideos`, `getVideoPlayback`                                                                                                | `video: ["read"]`                         |
| `video`             | `deleteVideo`                                                                                                                  | `video: ["delete"]`                       |

- `getCourseEditor` returns every lesson with its description, key moments, format, video (with its length, size, resolution, caption languages and the number of lessons that use it), upload (with the bytes stored so far) and materials, so the course editor and the lesson editor share one query.
- `uploadLessonAttachment` takes `FormData` (the file, the lesson and the title per locale, built by `uploadFormData`), and also runs `withRateLimit` with <RepositoryFact name="attachmentUploadsPerMinute" /> uploads per address a minute. It writes the row, then the file, and removes the row again if R2 refuses the file.
- `uploadCourseCover` takes `FormData` built by `coverFormData`: the course and one image per width. It also runs `withRateLimit` with <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverUploadsPerMinute" /> uploads per address a minute, and refuses a missing size, an empty file, one over <RepositoryFact name="courseCoverMaxMegabytes" /> MB, a type other than WebP or JPEG and sizes of different types. It stores the sizes in R2, records the key on the course, and only then deletes the files of the cover it replaces; when R2 refuses a size or the course is gone, it deletes what it stored. `removeCourseCover` clears the key and deletes the files.
- The uploads change what learners can watch, so they check `course: ["update"]`, like the other lesson writes; `startVideoUpload` also runs `withRateLimit` with <RepositoryFact name="videoUploadsPerMinute" /> starts per address a minute. `removeLessonVideo` can delete a video, so it also requires `video: ["delete"]`.
- The list under **Use an existing video** comes from `getVideos`, which requires `video: ["read"]`.
- A slug that is already taken answers `CONFLICT`: `rethrowAsConflict` (`src/integrations/drizzle-orm/drizzle.errors.ts`) turns D1's unique-constraint error into an `AppError`. An id that matches no row answers `NOT_FOUND`.
- A new course, chapter or lesson takes the highest `position` among its siblings plus one (`nextPosition`). Chapters and lessons are ordered with `reorderCurriculum`, above. A course or a material moves to a place among its siblings, counted from `FIRST_POSITION` (`src/modules/_core/constants/ordering.ts`): `reorder` puts it there, keeps a place past either end at that end, numbers the siblings from 1 and returns only the rows whose `position` changes, which the use case writes in one `db.batch`. Both functions are in `src/modules/_core/utils/ordering.ts`.
- Every write validates titles and summaries with `localizedTextSchema`: `en-US` is required and each text has at most <RepositoryFact name="localizedTextMaxLength" /> characters. A slug has at most <RepositoryFact name="slugMaxLength" /> characters. The form messages, such as `defaultLocaleRequired`, `textTooLong`, `slugInvalid`, `itemRequired`, `timeInvalid`, `urlInvalid` and `fileTooLarge`, are in `course.validations`, and `COURSE_VALIDATION_PARAMS` (`course.constants.ts`) supplies their limits.
- A course's slug and a lesson's slug within its course are unique, so they identify a course and a lesson across databases; the curriculum of a course is its chapters in order.
- `courseEditorQuery` keeps `staleTime: COURSE_STALE_MS` like the public course queries, and every change invalidates the `course` queries, so the list and the editor refetch.

`scripts/seed-test.ts` seeds a published course with its level, icon, outcomes and prerequisites and two chapters with summaries.
