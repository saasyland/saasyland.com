- Whether project generation has shipped is one constant, `CLI_GENERATION` in `src/data/cli.ts`. Every message that says generation is arriving, on the landing page, in the blog's call to action, on the Core card and on the `/app` documentation card, is an ICU `select` that also holds the sentence for when `init` ships, and the Core features that generation delivers take their status from the same constant, so one change flips them in every locale. The configurator marks the options that `CLI_TEMPLATE_SUPPORT` lists: the default stack and `--no-auth`. The blog posts print the coverage threshold, React's major version, the illustrative hourly rate and the hours the Core price buys from the code, and their titles and FAQ questions are formatted like their answers. See [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page) and [Blog](/docs/features/blog).
