The licence area sells the tiers `core`, `complete` and `agency` as Polar products, on top of the polar area's plugin and webhooks ([Payments](/docs/features/payments)). `POLAR_PRODUCT_IDS` in `src/modules/license/license.utils.ts` maps the tiers to `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_CORE`, `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_COMPLETE` and `POLAR_PRODUCT_ID_AGENCY`, and `licenseTierForProduct` reads the tier of a product from the same map. [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup) describes the products, licence keys and consent checkbox the tiers need in Polar.

## Checkout

A signed-in user without an active licence sees `LicenseOffer` on `/app` and `/app/license`: the tier cards of the landing page (`PricingTiers`, see [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page)) with a `CheckoutButton` for each tier, the regional pricing checkbox and the course lessons the purchase unlocks.

### Consent to immediate supply

The buyer gives the consent once, on Polar's checkout page, right before paying. Every licence product in Polar requires the checkbox field <RepositoryFact name="licenseWithdrawalConsentField" /> ([Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#consent-checkbox)), whose label asks for immediate supply and acknowledges losing the right of withdrawal, which lasts <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" />, once supply begins with the issue of the licence key. `startCheckout` sends no `custom_field_data`, so the box starts empty and only the buyer can tick it, and Polar takes no payment while it is empty: when the buyer pays, Polar validates the fields against the product and accepts only `true` for a required checkbox. Polar stores the ticked box on the order, and the [purchase confirmation](#purchase-confirmation) email confirms it on a durable medium. These are the conditions that end the right of withdrawal for digital content in the EU: the buyer's prior express consent, the acknowledgement and the confirmation.

`CheckoutButton` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/checkout.tsx`) starts the checkout as soon as it is pressed, with no dialog of its own, opens Polar's checkout over the page ([The checkout over the page](#the-checkout-over-the-page)) and turns the other tier buttons off until it is open. `CheckoutNotice`, in the same file, under the tier cards and under the chosen tier, says beforehand that Polar is the Merchant of Record and asks for the consent on the payment page, that a full refund remains possible for <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> until a project is generated or a paid lesson opened, and links the Refund Policy, the Terms of Service and the Commercial Licence.

### Starting the checkout

`startCheckout` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/start-checkout.ts`) takes `{ locale, returnPath, tier }`, where `returnPath` is the page the buyer pressed the button on, validated with `callbackPathSchema` so that it stays on the site. It reads the origin of the request with `trustedRequestOrigin` (`src/lib/request-origin.ts`), which refuses with `FORBIDDEN` a host that `appHostsForMode` does not list for the build mode, or plain HTTP outside development and test, and it:

1. returns `{ alreadyActive: true }` instead of a checkout when the account already has an `active` licence of the same or a higher tier. The button shows `buy.alreadyActive` from `pages.license` and refreshes the licence, so a page left open since the purchase switches to the licence;
2. prices the tier with `buyerTierPrice` (`license.ppp.ts`): its `TIER_PRICES` amount, times the multiplier of the `cf-ipcountry` country in `PPP_TIERS` unless the `regional_pricing` cookie is `off`, rounded down to whole dollars by `regionalPrice` (`license.pricing.ts`), the function the tier cards format their prices with. For an account whose active licence has a lower tier, it is the [upgrade](#upgrades) price instead;
3. creates a Polar checkout for the tier with `polar.checkouts.create`, tied to the account (`external_customer_id`) and filled in with its email and name (`customer_email`, `customer_name`), so the checkout form opens with them. It also carries the buyer's IP address from `cf-connecting-ip` (`customer_ip_address`, read with Better Auth's `getIP`), discount codes disabled, the price from step 2 in `prices` when it differs from `TIER_PRICES`, as a fixed price for the tier's product that holds for this checkout only, the buyer's language under `locale` (`LICENSE_CHECKOUT_METADATA`) in `metadata`, with the upgraded order and tier under `upgrade_from_order` and `upgrade_from_tier` for an upgrade, next to the affiliate referral when the visitor came through an affiliate link (see [Affiliate programme](/docs/features/affiliates)), the language of Polar's page from `polarCheckoutLanguage` in `locale`, the page in `returnPath` as `return_url`, so Polar shows a back button to it, the success URL `/app?checkoutId={CHECKOUT_ID}&tier=<tier>` in the buyer's language (`localizePathname`), and the request's origin as `embed_origin`, the only page that may frame the checkout and receive its messages. Polar copies the metadata and the consent the buyer gives onto the order, and replaces `{CHECKOUT_ID}` (`POLAR_CHECKOUT_ID_PLACEHOLDER`) with the checkout ID;
4. returns the checkout's ID and URL, which `CheckoutButton` opens over the page.

A refunded (`refunded`) or revoked (`revoked`) licence does not block a new purchase.

`POLAR_CHECKOUT_LANGUAGES` (`src/data/polar.ts`) maps each locale the site speaks to a language Polar's checkout translates. `polarCheckoutLanguage` falls back to `POLAR_CHECKOUT_DEFAULT_LANGUAGE`, English, for a locale without one, and for that locale `isPolarCheckoutTranslated` makes `CheckoutNotice` add that the payment page opens in English. Polar shows its page in the language only while checkout localization is on for the organisation, which `polar:verify` checks ([Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#checkout-language)).

### The checkout over the page

`openCheckoutOverlay` (`src/lib/checkout-overlay.ts`) shows Polar's checkout in an overlay on the page the buyer is on. It imports `PolarEmbedCheckout` from `@polar-sh/checkout/embed` only when a checkout starts, so no page loads Polar's script before a buyer presses a button, and `scripts/verify-build.ts` fails the build when a page loads it as its entry script or through a static import. `PolarEmbedCheckout.create` adds Polar's frame to the page in the site's resolved theme, and once Polar says the checkout has loaded, the overlay:

- names the frame with `buy.overlay` from `pages.license`, moves the focus into it and makes the rest of the page `inert`, so the keyboard and screen readers stay in the checkout;
- on `close` before the payment, closes the checkout and gives the page back, leaving the buyer where they were, with the button ready and focused again;
- on `success`, or on a `close` after Polar's `confirmed`, closes the checkout instead of letting Polar's script redirect, and `CheckoutButton` navigates in place to `/app?checkoutId=<id>&tier=<tier>`, the success URL Polar's checkout page returns to, without reloading the page ([After payment](#after-payment)).

The button shows `buy.pending` from the press until the overlay is open. When the overlay cannot open, the button sends the browser to Polar's checkout page for the same checkout, whose `return_url` and success URL bring the buyer back as before: when the script fails to load, when Polar's frame does not appear, or when the frame loads but Polar does not say the checkout is ready within <RepositoryFact name="checkoutOverlayGraceSeconds" unit="second" /> (`CHECKOUT_OVERLAY_GRACE_MS`), as when Polar refuses to be framed by a host its organisation does not list ([Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#embedding)).

The page's Content Security Policy frames only Polar's checkout: `documentPolicy` adds `frame-src` with the origin `polarCheckoutOrigin` (`src/data/polar.ts`) gives for `POLAR_SERVER`, `https://polar.sh` in production and `https://sandbox.polar.sh` with the sandbox ([Security](/docs/features/security)).

### Upgrades

An account has one licence, and its owner moves it to a higher tier by paying the difference. `LicenseCard` links to `/app/license?tier=<tier>` for each tier above the licence's own (`higherTiers` in `license.upgrades.ts`). With an active licence and a higher `tier`, `/app` and `/app/license` show `UpgradeOffer` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/upgrade-offer.tsx`): the amount to pay, how it is made up, the machines the key activates before and after, and a `CheckoutButton` with `CheckoutNotice` under it. `upgradePriceQuery` (`get-upgrade-price.ts`) reads the amount from `upgradePrice` (`license.upgrade-price.ts`):

- the price of the higher tier for this buyer, from `buyerTierPrice`, regional price included;
- less the credit: for every paid or partially refunded licence order of the Polar customer in `DEFAULT_CURRENCY_CODE`, listed with `polar.orders.list`, the price it charged, `subtotal_amount` less `discount_amount`, less `refunded_amount` and, when that price included tax, the `refunded_tax_amount` refunded with it. `net_amount` would leave out the VAT that an EU price includes, and credit less than the buyer paid;
- or nothing when less than `POLAR_MINIMUM_PRICE_AMOUNT` (`polar.constants.ts`), Polar's smallest US dollar price, is left, since Polar takes a free checkout but no smaller price.

`startCheckout` charges that amount for the higher tier's product and names the licence's order and tier in `metadata`. A licence that is not active, or has no Polar order or key yet, gets no upgrade, and checkout stays refused for it. A move to a lower tier still goes through support.

When the upgrade order is paid, `onOrderPaid` calls `upgradeLicense` (`use-cases/upgrade-license.ts`) instead of `grantLicense`. While the licence is still active on the order in `upgrade_from_order`, it raises the activation limit of the licence's Polar key to the new tier's `LICENSE_ACTIVATION_LIMITS` with `polar.licenseKeys.update` and sets the new tier, so the key, its activations, the first order and its records stay. Polar grants the upgrade order a key of its own, which `onBenefitGrantCreated` disables in Polar, so the customer keeps one key. An upgrade order whose licence has meanwhile been refunded, revoked or replaced is granted as a licence of its own. A full refund of the upgrade order calls `revertLicenseUpgrade`, which puts back the earlier tier and activation limit while the licence still has the upgraded tier; a refund of the first order revokes the licence as before.

### A tier chosen before sign-up

Each tier button on the landing page links to `/auth/sign-up?redirect=/app?tier=<tier>`. The return path survives sign-up, email verification and sign-in (`useAuthRedirect`), and a visitor who is already signed in goes straight to it. `licenseSearchSchema` (`src/data/license.ts`, built with `searchParam`) validates `tier` against `LICENSE_TIER` on `/app/license` and, as a param the licence area adds to the overview, on `/app`, and both `/app` and `/app/license` show `ChosenTier` above the offer: the tier, its regional price and a **Continue to checkout** button, which starts the same checkout, with `CheckoutNotice` under it. With a tier chosen, the offer folds the tier cards into a **Compare tiers** disclosure (`ComparedTiers` in `license-offer.tsx`), so the chosen tier's button is the one in view. A locked paid course lesson links a signed-in viewer to `/app/license?tier=complete`, where a Core owner gets the [upgrade](#upgrades), and a guest to the landing page's pricing section; a lesson that needs an account links to sign-in ([Courses](/docs/features/courses)).

The sign-up page already names the tier. The licence area adds `SignUpOrderSummary` (`custom/app/sign-up-order-summary.tsx`) to `SIGN_UP_INTROS` in `src/data/license.sign-up-intros.ts`, and the page shows it in place of its description when `redirectTierSchema` (`src/data/license.ts`) reads a `LICENSE_TIER` from a `redirect` to `/app` or `/app/license`, with or without a locale prefix. The summary shows the tier's name with its `TIER_PRICES` amount, which of the `PURCHASE_STEP` steps the visitor is on (the account, then the payment on Polar), and the full refund within `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS` days of a purchase that has not been used, linked to the refund policy. The sign-up page offers no regional pricing choice, so the summary shows the list price, and checkout applies the regional discount unless the visitor declined it. `SignUpPending` draws `SignUpOrderSummaryPending` for the same redirects, and any other `redirect` keeps the description.

### After payment

After the payment, the buyer is on `/app?checkoutId=<id>&tier=<tier>`: `CheckoutButton` navigates there when Polar's checkout over the page reports the payment, and Polar's checkout page returns there when the overlay could not open. Either way, that is usually before the `order.paid` webhook has granted the licence. While `checkoutId` is present and the licence is not active, the page hides the tier cards and shows `CheckoutActivation`, which refetches `currentLicenseQuery` every <RepositoryFact name="activationPollSeconds" unit="second" /> (`ACTIVATION_POLL_MS`):

- once the licence is active, it thanks the buyer, celebrates with `triggerConfetti` (`src/lib/confetti.ts`, which draws nothing for a visitor who prefers reduced motion) and shows the [first steps](#first-steps), and the licence appears below;
- after <RepositoryFact name="activationTimeoutSeconds" unit="second" /> (`ACTIVATION_TIMEOUT_MS`) without a licence, it stops polling and says that Polar has not confirmed the payment yet, with the support email, the checkout ID as a reference and a **Check again** button.

A `checkoutId` that is not a UUID is ignored.

`tier` names the tier the checkout bought, in the success URL and in the overlay's navigation alike. An upgrade's licence is already active on the lower tier, so `CheckoutActivation` keeps polling until the licence has that tier (`hasReachedTier` in `license.upgrades.ts`) and only then thanks the buyer, naming the licence's new tier, never the one they had; until then the page shows no first steps and no `UpgradeOffer`. A return without `tier` counts an active licence as the purchase.

Polar's checkout page adds `customer_session_token` (`POLAR_CUSTOMER_SESSION_TOKEN_PARAM`), a token that opens the buyer's Polar customer portal, to the success URL. `/app` and `/app/license` keep only the search params they declare (`onlyDeclaredSearch` in `src/lib/search-params.ts`), so the server answers that address with a redirect to the same address without the token, before the sign-in guard or the page sees it. The token never stays in the address bar or reaches a sign-in `redirect`, a verification email or a request the page makes, and the site never reads it.

### First steps

`GetStarted` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/get-started.tsx`) shows an active licence's steps to a first project under the activation panel and on the overview: the purchase, already done, the licence key and the commands <RepositoryFact name="cliSetupCommands" /> from `CLI_SETUP_LINES` (`src/data/cli.ts`), each with a copy button, and a progress bar. The key's step is done once the key is copied there or activated, the activation's once the licence has `activated_at` and the project's once it has `delivered_at` ([Records and refunds](#records-and-refunds)), and the project's step says that generating a project is first use under the Refund Policy. Until the address is confirmed, the key's step says where the confirmation link went instead of showing the key, and `licenseKeySetupQuery` (`get-current-license-key.ts`) refetches the key of a confirmed address every <RepositoryFact name="licenseKeyPollSeconds" unit="second" /> while it is being issued. `licenseSetupQuery` (`get-current-license.ts`) refetches the licence every <RepositoryFact name="licenseKeyPollSeconds" unit="second" /> while the key is being issued and every <RepositoryFact name="licenseSetupPollSeconds" unit="second" /> while a step is left, and stops once every step is done.

## Webhooks and licences

`src/data/license.polar-events.ts` adds the licence area to the Polar events (see [Payments](/docs/features/payments#webhooks)) with the events listed in `LICENSE_WEBHOOK_EVENTS` (`license.constants.ts`), whose order events come from `POLAR_WEBHOOK_EVENT`, and its `handle` passes each event to the handler of the same name in `licenseWebhookHandlers`. `licenseWebhookHandlers` satisfies `Record<keyof typeof LICENSE_WEBHOOK_EVENTS, unknown>`, so a handler without its event, or an event without its handler, does not compile. `licenseWebhookHandlers` (`src/modules/license/license.webhooks.ts`) match the user through the Polar customer `external_id`. An event carries the customer as Polar recorded it when it created the event, and a redelivery sends that same copy, so when its `external_id` names no account, `onOrderPaid` and `onBenefitGrantCreated` read the order again with `polar.orders.get` and match its current customer. An event for a customer with no account here is logged when it needed one and acknowledged, so Polar does not retry it:

- `onOrderPaid` → `grantLicense`: upserts the user's single licence with the tier of the purchased product and the buyer's consent to immediate delivery (see [Records and refunds](#records-and-refunds)); a newer purchase replaces an older one. An order with `upgrade_from_order` in its metadata goes to `upgradeLicense` instead and is granted only when the licence it upgrades is no longer active ([Upgrades](#upgrades)). Then `sendPurchaseConfirmationEmail` confirms the purchase (see [Purchase confirmation](#purchase-confirmation)); when it fails, the handler fails, so Polar delivers the event again. An order for a product that is not a licence is only logged, and a licence order whose customer matches no account is logged and reported (see [Orders that match no account](#orders-that-match-no-account)).
- `onBenefitGrantCreated` → `attachLicenseKey`: stores the Polar licence key on the licence, or, for an upgrade order whose licence kept its key, disables the order's own key in Polar (`POLAR_LICENSE_KEY_STATUS.DISABLED`). While the paid order is not recorded yet it fails, so Polar retries it; a key for a refunded order or for a product that is not a licence is acknowledged.
- `onOrderRefunded` → `revokeLicense` with the status `refunded`, only once the order is fully refunded; a partial refund keeps the licence. For an upgrade order, `revertLicenseUpgrade` first puts back the earlier tier and activation limit.
- `onBenefitGrantRevoked` → `revokeLicense` with the status `revoked`.

`revokeLicense` sets the status of the licence bought with that order and records the order in `revoked_license_order`, so a late webhook cannot grant it again. It never replaces `refunded`: Polar also revokes the benefit of a refunded order, and the two events can arrive in either order. The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api) answers `licenseRefunded` or `licenseRevoked` from this status.

## Records and refunds

The `license` row keeps four records of the current purchase, each empty until it happens:

| Column                                 | Records                                                                                                                                    | Written by                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `withdrawal_consent_at`                | When the buyer consented at checkout to immediate delivery and acknowledged losing the right of withdrawal: the time the order was created | `grantLicense`, from `order.paid`                                                                                |
| `activated_at`                         | The first activation of the key on any machine                                                                                             | The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api#records-of-use)                                             |
| `delivered_at`                         | The first time the CLI downloaded templates with the key                                                                                   | The [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api#records-of-use)                                             |
| `lesson_opened_at`, `opened_lesson_id` | The first time the buyer opened a paid lesson that the licence unlocks, and that lesson's ID                                               | `getLesson` and `getLessonPlayback`, through the licence's [course access](/docs/features/courses#access-levels) |

The buyer gives the consent by ticking the required checkbox on Polar's checkout ([Consent to immediate supply](#consent-to-immediate-supply)), and Polar stores it on the order as `custom_field_data.<slug>`, where the slug is <RepositoryFact name="licenseWithdrawalConsentField" /> (`LICENSE_CHECKOUT_FIELD` in `license.constants.ts`). `WITHDRAWAL_CONSENT_FIELD` in `src/lib/polar.ts` holds the field's name, type and English label, and `polar:verify` fails while a product does not require it (see [Polar setup](/docs/getting-started/polar-setup#consent-checkbox)). `onOrderPaid` validates the field with Zod and records the consent only when it is `true`. A new order for the same account, such as a purchase after a refund, replaces the consent with its own and empties `activated_at`, `delivered_at`, `lesson_opened_at` and `opened_lesson_id`, so the records always describe the current purchase. Licences bought before the records existed have none from that time.

`refundEligibility` (`src/modules/license/license.refunds.ts`) is a pure function of the purchase time, the consent, the first download, the first paid lesson and the current time. The period is <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> from the purchase, `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS` in `src/data/refunds.ts`, the one place that number is declared. It returns one kind of `REFUND_ELIGIBILITY`:

| Kind                  | When                                                                                                       | Carries         |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| `refundable`          | Within the period, the buyer consented, the CLI has not downloaded templates and no paid lesson was opened | `until`         |
| `statutoryWithdrawal` | Within the period, no consent was recorded, so the statutory withdrawal applies, used or not               | `until`         |
| `used`                | Within the period, the buyer consented and the CLI has downloaded templates or a paid lesson was opened    | `usedAt`, `via` |
| `expired`             | The period has ended                                                                                       | `endedAt`       |

A `used` purchase carries the earlier of the two first uses as `usedAt`, and which one it was as `via`: `FIRST_USE.TEMPLATES` or `FIRST_USE.LESSON` (`license.constants.ts`). An admin sees the records and the result in words on the user's profile; see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#profile).

## Purchase confirmation

After `grantLicense`, `onOrderPaid` sends the buyer the confirmation that the consent needs on a durable medium. `sendPurchaseConfirmationEmail` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/send-purchase-confirmation-email.ts`) renders `PurchaseConfirmationEmail` (`src/presentation/emails/purchase-confirmation-email.tsx`, namespace `emails.purchase-confirmation-email`) and sends it to the address of the account that bought:

- **Language**: the order's `metadata.locale`, validated with `localeField`; an order without a language the site speaks gets English.
- **Order**: the invoice number, or the Polar order ID before the order has one; the order date; the tier, named as the pricing cards name it in that language; and `total_amount` in the order's currency, converted from minor units with `fromMinorUnits`.
- **Right of withdrawal**: when `custom_field_data` holds the consent, the email repeats that the buyer asked for immediate supply and acknowledged losing the right of withdrawal, so it does not apply to this order. Without it, the email says that the buyer can withdraw within <RepositoryFact name="refundWithdrawalDays" unit="day" /> of the order date.
- **Refund**: the deadline for a full refund before first use, `refundDeadline` (`license.refunds.ts`), the purchase time plus `REFUND_TERMS.WITHDRAWAL_DAYS`, the same date `refundEligibility` uses. A full refund needs, as the Refund Policy says, that no project has been generated with the CLI and no paid lesson opened.
- **Links**: the licence page, the [CLI guide](/docs/cli), the Terms of Service, the Commercial Licence, the Refund Policy and the [withdrawal function](#withdrawal-function), which the lines on the right of withdrawal and the refund link, in the email's language, on the host that received the webhook. Under the button, `EmailFallbackLink` writes out the licence page's address for a reader whose email client hides the button.
- **Get started**: a block under the order with a button to the licence page, where the key is, the commands <RepositoryFact name="cliSetupCommands" /> from `CLI_SETUP_LINES`, a link to the CLI guide and the lines other areas add for the buyer, `PURCHASE_EMAIL_STEPS` (`src/data/purchase-email.ts`), which `src/data/purchase-email.entries.ts` joins. The courses area adds a link to the courses for a buyer who holds course access (`src/data/courses.purchase-email.ts`, namespace `purchase-email.courses`).
- **Brand**: `EmailBrandHeader` puts the site's name above the email, as in the auth emails.

Dates are printed in UTC. The idempotency key is `PURCHASE_CONFIRMATION` (`src/data/license.email-purposes.ts`) with the Polar order ID, so Resend sends one email per order however often Polar delivers `order.paid`, and the email is built from the order alone, so a repeated delivery sends the same request. An order that maps to no account or tier gets no confirmation; a licence order without an account sends the site an alert instead (see [Orders that match no account](#orders-that-match-no-account)). See [Email](/docs/features/email).

## Orders that match no account

`startCheckout` ties every checkout to the signed-in account, so an order matches no account only when it was paid through a checkout that `startCheckout` did not create, such as a checkout link from the Polar dashboard, or when the account was deleted between checkout and payment. The buyer has paid and has no licence, so the site reports the order and lists it until it has one.

### Alert

`onOrderPaid` logs such an order and passes it to `sendUnmatchedOrderAlert` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/send-unmatched-order-alert.ts`), which sends `UnmatchedOrderAlertEmail` (`src/presentation/emails/unmatched-order-alert-email.tsx`, namespace `emails.unmatched-order-alert-email`) from `NOTIFICATIONS_EMAIL` to `CONTACT_EMAIL`, in `I18N.DEFAULT_LOCALE`. It names:

- the Polar order ID and the order date, in UTC;
- the tier and `total_amount` in the order's currency;
- the customer's email and name as the event carries them, or that they were not given;
- a link to the order in the Polar dashboard, which `polarOrderUrl` (`src/modules/license/license.utils.ts`) builds from the host in `POLAR_DASHBOARD_HOSTS` for `POLAR_SERVER`, `POLAR_ORGANIZATION_SLUG` (both in `src/data/polar.ts`) and the order ID;
- the steps that [recover the order](#recovering-an-order).

The idempotency key is `UNMATCHED_ORDER_ALERT` (`src/data/license.email-purposes.ts`) with the Polar order ID. Resend keeps a key for 24 hours, so repeated deliveries of the order within that time send one alert, and a redelivery after it alerts again while the order still matches no account. When the alert cannot be sent, the handler fails, so Polar delivers the event again.

### Admin list

The licence area adds `UnlicensedOrders` (`src/presentation/components/custom/admin/license/unlicensed-orders.tsx`) to the panels of the [admin dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard) in `src/data/license.dashboard.ts`, and the dashboard's loader prefetches its query. `unlicensedOrdersQuery` (`src/modules/license/use-cases/get-unlicensed-orders.ts`) checks `order: ["read"]` and lists, newest first, the [copies of Polar's orders](/docs/features/payments#orders-and-subscriptions-in-d1) that:

- have the status `paid`;
- have a `product_id` in `POLAR_PRODUCT_IDS`;
- are the `polar_order_id` of no licence;
- are not in `revoked_license_order`.

Each row (`unlicensed-order-columns.tsx`) shows the order ID, which opens the order in Polar in a new tab, the date, the Polar customer ID and the amount, converted from minor units. Without such an order the panel says that every paid licence order has its licence. The list also holds an order whose `order.paid` delivery has not succeeded yet, and the order of an account that was deleted, whose licence was deleted with it.

### Recovering an order

1. Find the buyer's account in the admin's user management, or create it with **Add user** and the email from the alert (see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin)). The account's ID is the last part of its profile's address, `/admin/users/<ID>`.
2. Open the order in Polar from the alert or the admin list, then its customer, choose **Edit Customer** in the customer's menu, set **External ID** to the account's ID and choose **Save Customer**. With the API, send `external_id` in `PATCH /v1/customers/{id}` with a token that has `customers:write`. Polar keeps an external ID once it is set and requires it to be unique within the organisation, so this works only for a customer without one whose account has no other Polar customer; otherwise refund the order in Polar and ask the buyer to buy again while signed in.
3. In Polar's webhook settings, open the endpoint's deliveries, find the order's `order.paid` event and choose **Redeliver**, then do the same for its `benefit_grant.created` event. `onOrderPaid` grants the licence and sends the purchase confirmation to the account's address, and `onBenefitGrantCreated` stores the licence key; a `benefit_grant.created` that arrives first fails until the licence exists, and Polar retries it. The order then leaves the admin list.

Polar redelivers an event only while it keeps the event's payload, which Polar's server archives after 90 days.

## Withdrawal function

Article 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), inserted by Directive (EU) 2023/2673 and applied from 19 June 2026, requires a trader who concludes contracts on a website to offer a withdrawal function there: a control labelled "withdraw from contract here", prominently displayed and available throughout the withdrawal period, that opens an online withdrawal statement in which the consumer gives or confirms their name, the contract and the electronic address for the confirmation, and submits it with a control labelled only "confirm withdrawal". The trader then sends an acknowledgement of receipt on a durable medium, with the statement's content and the date and time of its submission, without undue delay. A statement submitted before the period ends exercises the right in time.

### Where it is

- `/withdrawal` (`ROUTES.WITHDRAWAL`, `_site._marketing.withdrawal.tsx`, namespace `pages.withdrawal`) is a public, prerendered page that works signed out. The footer of every page links it as **Withdraw from contract here** (`LICENSE_NAVIGATION` in `src/data/license.navigation.ts`), and so do the Refund Policy, the Terms of Service and the purchase confirmation.
- `WithdrawalCard` (`src/presentation/components/custom/app/withdrawal-card.tsx`) sits on `/app/license` below an active licence. It names the tier, the order number and the purchase date, says whether the right of withdrawal or the full refund before first use still applies, or when and how the purchase was first used, from `refundEligibility` at the server's time, and opens the statement in a dialog with **Withdraw from contract here**, with the account's name, email address and order number filled in. After a statement it says when the statement was received.

Both use `WithdrawalForm` (`src/presentation/components/custom/withdrawal-form.tsx`): the statement sentence, the name, the email address for the acknowledgement and the order number, validated with `withdrawalZodSchemas.submitWithdrawal` and the messages in `withdrawal.validations`, and the submit button **Confirm withdrawal**. The form never refuses a statement because the right no longer applies; that is decided afterwards. Once the statement is in, the form gives way to the receipt: the statement, the time it was received and whether the acknowledgement was emailed.

### Submission

`submitWithdrawal` (`src/modules/withdrawal/use-cases/submit-withdrawal.ts`) runs `withRateLimit("submit-withdrawal", RATE_LIMITS.SENSITIVE)`, <RepositoryFact name="sensitiveRateLimit" /> statements per client a minute, and reads the session when there is one. `matchContract` (`src/modules/withdrawal/withdrawal.contracts.ts`) finds the active licence whose current order has the number given, as Polar's order ID or invoice number in any letter case; the statement belongs to that licence when the signed-in account owns it or the email address given is the account's. `recordWithdrawal` (`record-withdrawal.ts`, server-only) then:

1. stores the statement in the `withdrawal` table, with the refund terms of the matched licence at that moment, or `unmatched`;
2. sends `WithdrawalAcknowledgementEmail` (namespace `emails.withdrawal-acknowledgement-email`) to the address given, in the language of the page, with the statement, the name, the order number, the address and the time of submission, at most <RepositoryFact name="recipientEmailsPerHour" /> per address per hour, and records when Resend accepted it in `acknowledged_at`;
3. sends `WithdrawalNotificationEmail` (namespace `emails.withdrawal-notification-email`) from `NOTIFICATIONS_EMAIL` to `CONTACT_EMAIL` in `I18N.DEFAULT_LOCALE`: the statement, how the buyer was identified, the licence, the refund terms, whether the acknowledgement went out, the date a refund is due by, <RepositoryFact name="refundReimbursementDays" unit="day" /> after receipt (`REFUND_TERMS.REIMBURSEMENT_DAYS`), and links to the account in the admin and the order in Polar.

An email that cannot be sent is logged and leaves the statement in place: the receipt then says that the acknowledgement is still to come, and the notification says it was not sent. The idempotency keys are `WITHDRAWAL_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT` and `WITHDRAWAL_NOTIFICATION` (`src/data/license.email-purposes.ts`) with the withdrawal's ID.

Nothing is refunded or revoked automatically: a refund stays the owner's decision. To accept a withdrawal, refund the order in Polar; `order.refunded` then marks the licence refunded and revokes its key, as [Webhooks and licences](#webhooks-and-licences) describes, and reply to the buyer.

### The withdrawal table

| Column                                    | Holds                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`                                      | A UUID v7                                                                                                                                                      |
| `name`, `email`, `reference`              | The name, the email address for the acknowledgement and the order number as the buyer gave them                                                                |
| `locale`                                  | The language of the page, which the acknowledgement uses                                                                                                       |
| `channel`                                 | `account` when the buyer was signed in, `public` otherwise (`WITHDRAWAL_CHANNEL`)                                                                              |
| `user_id`, `license_id`, `polar_order_id` | The matched account, licence and Polar order, and a signed-in buyer's account without a match; `ON DELETE SET NULL` keeps the statement when either is deleted |
| `eligibility`                             | The refund terms when the statement arrived: a kind of `REFUND_ELIGIBILITY`, or `unmatched` (`WITHDRAWAL_ELIGIBILITY`)                                         |
| `acknowledged_at`                         | When Resend accepted the acknowledgement; empty while it is owed                                                                                               |
| `created_at`                              | When the statement was received, the time the acknowledgement states                                                                                           |

The admin's user profile lists an account's statements, newest first; see [Users (admin)](/docs/features/users-admin#profile).

## Customer screens

`/app/license` shows `LicenseCard`. For an active licence, it shows the tier, the key and the commands in `CLI_KEY_LINES`, which activate the key, generate a project, show and free a machine, each with a copy button; while the key is being issued, a line that `licenseKeySetupQuery` replaces with the key once it arrives; a link to the Polar customer portal at `/api/auth/customer/portal`; an **Upgrade to …** link for each higher tier ([Upgrades](#upgrades)); and the support email for a move to a lower tier. The key and the portal wait for a confirmed address, see [Email verification](/docs/features/email-verification#what-waits-for-the-confirmed-address). For a refunded or revoked licence, it shows the status, a link to the offer below it and one to support, without the key, the commands or the machines. Below an active licence with a key comes `LicenseActivations`, which lists key activations from Polar and removes one with `deactivateLicense`; it answers `NOT_FOUND` when Polar no longer holds that activation. The CLI adds and removes activations through the [CLI licence API](/docs/features/cli-licence-api). Polar enforces the activation limit of each tier's licence-key benefit, which must equal `LICENSE_ACTIVATION_LIMITS`: <ActivationLimit tier="core" /> for Core, <ActivationLimit tier="complete" /> for Complete and <ActivationLimit tier="agency" /> for Agency. See [Customer app](/docs/features/customer-app).
