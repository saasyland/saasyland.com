- One header on every public page. The landing page, the blog, the courses, the docs, the legal pages, `/premium` and the newsletter pages sit in one pathless layout, `_site` (`src/routes/_site.tsx`), which renders `Navigation` and `BackToTop` once, so no page or child layout renders a header of its own: `ContentNavigation` is gone, and the docs show the site header above their sidebar instead of only Fumadocs' bar. The header lists **Pricing**, **Courses**, **Blog**, **Docs** and **Sign in**, then **Start building**, in that order on every page and in the mobile menu, from the header entries of `src/data/<area>.navigation.ts` (`HEADER_NAVIGATION`, formerly `NAVIGATION_PAGES`): the core adds **Pricing**, which links to the pricing section through the entry's new `hash`, and the auth area adds **Sign in**. The landing page's section anchors left the header, and the current section is marked on every page, **Docs** included. Inside `_site`, the `_marketing` layout (`_site._marketing.tsx`, formerly `_landing.tsx`) gives every page but the docs the page frame, the footer and the dark theme, and loads the newsletter form's messages for the course pages too, which missed them. The header opens with a **Skip to content** link, its height is the `--spacing-header` theme token, and `html` keeps anchors and focused elements clear of it with `scroll-padding-top`. The section ids, `MARKETING_SECTION_IDS`, moved from `src/data/marketing.ts` to `src/data/navigation.constants.ts`, next to the other navigation constants. See [Landing page](/docs/features/landing-page#navigation-and-back-to-top).
