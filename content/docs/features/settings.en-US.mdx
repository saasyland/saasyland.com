`src/routes/admin.settings.tsx` renders two tabs from widgets in `src/presentation/components/custom/admin/settings`. The loader preloads `getActiveSessionsQuery`. The customer area `/app` has no settings screen.

## Security (live)

- `SettingsPasswordForm` asks for the current, new and repeated password and calls `changePassword` (`src/modules/account/use-cases/change-password.ts`, signed in and rate limited). Success refreshes the session list.
- `SettingsTwoFactor` enables or disables TOTP ([Two-factor authentication](/docs/features/two-factor)).
- `SettingsSessions` lists and revokes sessions ([Sessions](/docs/features/sessions)).

## General (demo)

- `ProfileCard`: workspace name, support email and URL, prefilled from `APP_NAME` and constants in the file.
- `PreferencesCard`: language, time zone and an announcements switch.
- `DangerZoneCard`: a delete button.

These cards render default values only; nothing is saved. `updateUser` and `changeEmail` in `src/modules/account/use-cases` are the server functions to connect when you build a profile form.
