- Search and social previews. Every build writes `sitemap.xml` from the prerendered pages themselves: each page that allows indexing, in every locale, with its `hreflang` alternates and `x-default`; `robots.txt` names it. Every page carries `og:image` with its size and alt text, `og:site_name` and a `summary_large_image` Twitter card, and `og:title` is now the page title without the site name. Each prerendered page has its own card, rendered at build time with Takumi from the site's fonts and light-theme tokens: docs pages and blog posts show their section and title, other pages their title, and pages that are not prerendered share the site card. `/premium`, a placeholder, asks search engines not to index it, and `verify-deployment.ts` also requests the sitemap and the site card. The Worker is unchanged. See [Search and social previews](/docs/features/seo).
