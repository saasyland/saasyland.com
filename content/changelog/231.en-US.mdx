- Changed: the CLI configurator on the landing page lets you pick only what the CLI's templates generate. Every option and extra they do not generate yet keeps its place, disabled, with an hourglass that the key beside the package manager tabs explains, and screen readers hear the same reason. `CLI_TEMPLATE_SUPPORT` in `src/data/cli.ts` decides, so an option is enabled by listing it there once the templates generate it. The configurator opens on the answers `init --yes` resolves from that list, and no answer it can reach puts a flag in the command that the templates do not generate. See [CLI configurator](/docs/features/landing-page#cli-configurator).
- Fixed: the configurator no longer presents Polar billing as generated. `CLI_TEMPLATE_SUPPORT` lists `--no-billing` alone, as the CLI's own template support does, so the opening command answers `--no-billing`.
