- Changed: `no-magic-numbers` allows `1` as well as `0`, so `.limit(1)`, `index + 1` and `toHaveBeenCalledTimes(1)` are written as they read. The constants that only named a `0` or a `1`, such as `SINGLE_CALL` and `SINGLE_ROW_LIMIT`, are gone. A `0` or `1` with a meaning of its own keeps its name, as `FAILURE_EXIT_CODE` and `INCLUSIVE_END` do, and `-1` is still named. See [Code style](/docs/conventions/code-style).
