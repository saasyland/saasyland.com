- Courses and licences no longer import each other. Course access is a list of the courses area's own: an area that sells it declares in `src/data/<area>.course-access.ts` who holds access, what includes it and where to get it, and the licence area grants it to an active Complete or Agency licence (`LICENSE_COURSE_ACCESS`), so `lesson.access.ts` names no licence tier and the locked lessons take their links from the grant. The licence offer ends with the sections that other areas add in `src/data/<area>.license-offer.ts`: the courses area adds the locked courses with their skeleton and catalogue query, so `/app` and `/app/license` no longer read the catalogue, and the panel's text moved to `components.custom.course-unlocks`. The live site checks after a deploy are declared per area in `src/data/<area>.live.ts`, and the public queries that `auth.session.test.ts` runs per area in `<area>.public-queries.ts`. The courses area's `MEDIA` bucket is the last block of each environment in `wrangler.jsonc`, with every value unchanged; `verify-bindings.ts`, `seed-test.ts` and the test runtime's in-memory buckets read the bindings from `wrangler.jsonc` instead of naming them, and `wrangler.test.ts` checks that every environment binds what `development` binds. Who can watch which lesson is unchanged: `license.course-access.test.ts` pins the decision for a guest, an account, staff and every licence tier and status, recorded before the change, and the licence and course pages look the same. The unused `common.cancel` message is gone. A copy of the project without the courses area type-checks, builds and keeps 100% coverage. The course copy that named Complete takes the names from the grant (`offer` and `holders`), so it reads the same on the website and names whatever another area sells.
